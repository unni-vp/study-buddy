# People of the UK: population and urban change

## Explain uneven development

- **Location, industry and infrastructure:** influence investment and jobs. Government policy can alter opportunities.

- Compare income, health, education, employment and access. One average can hide inequality.

## Read population change

- **Natural change:** births minus deaths. **Net migration:** arrivals minus departures.

- **Pyramid:** age and sex structure. **DTM:** birth and death rates, not a fixed national timetable.

## Ageing and migration

- **Ageing:** lower birth rates and longer lives increase demand for pensions and care, but older people also contribute.

- Migration can bring skills and culture. Services and housing must keep pace with local growth.

## Salford Quays: change

- **1982 dock closure → regeneration → Lowry 2000 and MediaCity 2011.**

- Public infrastructure and anchor firms attract activity. Higher values do not guarantee benefits for every resident.

## Leeds: character and challenges

- **2021 population:** about 812,000. Universities and migration shape work, culture and housing demand.

- Housing, transport and waste need joined-up responses. Carnival is a named cultural example.

## Evaluate sustainable responses

- **Connecting Leeds:** alternatives to car journeys. **PIPES:** useful heat from waste recovery.

- Assess who benefits, affordability and access. More investment can coexist with inequality.
