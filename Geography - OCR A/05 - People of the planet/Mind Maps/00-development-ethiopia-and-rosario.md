# Development: Ethiopia and Rosario

## Measure more than wealth

- **Development:** economic, social and environmental improvement. **GNI:** income average.

- **HDI:** health, education and income. **Internet use:** digital access. Each hides some inequalities.

## Explain Ethiopia’s connections

- **Landlocked location:** trade depends on transport and neighbouring countries. Drought affects farming and export income.

- Investment can create jobs and infrastructure. Conflict, unequal access and dependence limit gains.

## Technology and Rostow

- **Phones and payments:** need affordable access, power, skills and markets.

- **Rostow:** five proposed stages. Ethiopia has mixed features; sectors and regions do not follow one timetable.

## Aid: the goat-sharing project

- **Kanbi:** seven goats to initial households; milk, sales and passing offspring spread benefits.

- Water, pasture and animal health matter. Local aid complements wider development and emergency support.

## Rosario: character and needs

- **Paraná port:** links an agricultural region to world trade. Migration shapes communities and urban culture.

- Housing, transport and waste pressures vary between neighbourhoods.

## Two sustainable approaches

- **Hábitat:** infrastructure, housing, tenure and social support. **Gardens:** food, skills, income and green space.

- Evaluate who benefits, secure land and long-term funding. Neither strategy solves every citywide problem.
