# River landscapes and the Wye

## Separate the processes

- **Weathering:** breakdown in place. **Mass movement:** gravity moves material downslope.

- **Erosion, transport and deposition:** removal, movement and laying down.

## Upper-course landforms

- **Vertical erosion:** deepens the channel. Weathering and mass movement help form V-shaped valleys.

- **Waterfall:** softer rock is undercut, the overhang collapses and retreat leaves a gorge.

## Bends and floodplains

- **Meander:** outer-bank erosion and inner-bank deposition. Cut-through plus sealed entrances makes an oxbow.

- **Floods:** alluvium builds floodplains. Coarser deposits beside the channel build levees.

## The Wye: place matters

- **Plynlimon → Hereford → Chepstow:** a 251 km river from Welsh uplands to the Severn Estuary.

- **Symonds Yat:** the limestone gorge interrupts a simple widening-valley model.

## People shape the valley

- **Farming and settlements:** alter soils, vegetation and runoff. Floodplains are useful but exposed.

- **Livox Quarry:** rock extraction alters relief. Restoration can create habitats.

## Judge the management

- **Natural flood management:** trees, ponds and barriers slow and store runoff.

- Combine catchment measures with warnings and targeted protection. Exceptional floods can still exceed capacity.
