# Unseen poetry overview

## Understand the whole poem

- Speaker: voice in the poem, not automatically the poet. Situation: what happens and where.
- Overview: what is described and what deeper idea emerges?
- Read: sentences across line endings. Check title, turns and ending.

## Language and imagery

- Words: precise connotations. Metaphor/simile: qualities transferred by comparison.
- Personification: attitude towards a non-human subject. Sensory detail: imagined experience.
- Sound/repetition: explain this effect, not an automatic meaning.

## Structure and form

- Structure: order, stanza shifts, contrasts and ending. Form: overall kind or shape.
- Enjambment: continuing sentence. Caesura: internal pause. End-stopping: closure or pause at a line end.
- Turn: show what changes. Free verse still has deliberate organisation.

## Build a developed reading

- Point: answer the task. Evidence: short and precise. Method: particular word, image or arrangement.
- Meaning: explain the idea and its effect. Development: connect another moment.
- Avoid: technique lists, unsupported guesses and invented biography.

## Compare both poems

- Shared idea: compare attitudes, images and development, not only matching subjects.
- Both poems: evidence and analysis within linked paragraphs.
- Difference: similar objects can express opposing values. Check each ending.

## Manage the tasks

- Single poem: 15 marks, about 20 minutes. Comparison: 25 marks, about 40 minutes.
- Include reading and planning. Give the comparison its larger share.
- Check: task focus, accurate evidence, methods and the overall meaning.
