# Analysis and comparison

## Literal and deeper meaning

- Literal: what is directly described. Deeper: what the details imply about experience or values.
- Waiting: cup and extra place reveal unresolved attachment.
- Departure: cup and key begin a chosen movement towards independence.

## Tone and viewpoint

- Tone: attitude of the voice. Mood: atmosphere created. They may contrast.
- Pronouns, questions and changes of certainty help establish viewpoint.
- Ambiguity: support plausible readings. Do not turn guesses into facts.

## Analyse a specific choice

- Image: name the relevant qualities and connect them to the idea.
- Example: bus windows carrying other lives place companionship beyond the waiting speaker.
- Effect: public movement contrasts with private stillness.

## Trace structure accurately

- Opening: establishes expectation. Turn: changes time, place or thought. Ending: resolves or complicates.
- Waiting: room, street, return indoors. Departure: house, journey, increasing distance.
- Meaning: similar movement can reinforce attachment or freedom.

## Different responses to absence

- Waiting and Departure are the original practice poems in the notes and Q&A.
- Comparison: the same general subject does not mean the same attitude.

## An effective paragraph

- Claim: a meaningful similarity or difference. Evidence: details from both poems.
- Analysis: explain each method and meaning. Link: why the difference matters.
- Response: informed interpretation with evidence, not only personal liking.
