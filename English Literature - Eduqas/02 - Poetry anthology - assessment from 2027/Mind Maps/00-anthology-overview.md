# Poetry anthology

## Nature and childhood

- **The Schoolboy:** fear restricts natural growth. **Wordsworth:** remembered nature restores joy. **Blackberry Picking:** desired pleasure gives way to decay.
- Compare who or what causes change: authority, recollection or time.

## Home and the past

- **I Shall Return:** homeland remembered; return hoped for. **Drummer Hodge:** burial abroad makes separation permanent.
- Place can preserve identity or expose distance from it. Compare the endings, not just landscape words.

## Love and distance

- **Sonnet 29:** thoughts must give way to the real beloved. **Dusting the Phone:** imagined contact becomes anxious dependence.
- **Cousin Kate:** rejection and public judgement meet maternal pride. Compare power and the speaker’s final position.

## Family and selfhood

- **Catrin:** connection survives conflict over independence. **Origin Story:** imperfect parental love can leave a valuable legacy.
- Both look back, but from different generations. A shared theme does not mean the same attitude.

## War beyond the battlefield

- **Disabled:** injury and neglected homecoming. **Kamikaze:** survival followed by exclusion. **Remains:** a violent event persists in memory.
- Distinguish physical loss, social punishment and psychological harm.

## Looking and responsibility

- **War Photographer:** witness, editor and audience respond differently. **Decomposition:** an observer rethinks making art from hardship.
- For every poem: voice → changing ideas → language/form/structure → relevant context. Compare effects with evidence.
