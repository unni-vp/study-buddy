## Coverage and decisions

The existing notes, original practice examples and independently curated decks were reviewed. New explanations are integrated under their relevant existing headings. Original teaching blocks remain in order. Every current anthology poem retains relevant poet context, form/structure, content/deeper meaning, 5–6 key quotations, language/imagery and a worked analysis. Each poem has recall and interpretation cards. Existing diagrams, mindmaps and combined Q&A documents were preserved.

- **Literature:** PMT's matching Eduqas Macbeth, An Inspector Calls and A Christmas Carol notes were used for coverage checks. Overlapping AQA poem notes and the general poetry-form guide were checked against the official 2027 anthology. StudyRocket's AQA Literature materials were used where the texts overlap; its reviewed index has no matching Macbeth or Christmas Carol unit and no complete new Eduqas anthology course. No missing exact-board resource was invented. PMT's advanced metre taxonomy was not turned into a compulsory GCSE checklist.
- **English Language:** PMT's reviewed GCSE Language index lists AQA and Edexcel rather than Eduqas. Relevant AQA guides and StudyRocket skills pages informed evidence selection, viewpoint, narrative decisions and argument development. Their question numbers, task choices, marks and timings were not imported. The local Eduqas specification controls narrative/recount, two compulsory transactional tasks and the separate spoken-language endorsement. New cards are self-contained; no card relies on an omitted unseen passage. No exact matching PMT/StudyRocket spoken-language unit was located.
- **French:** No matching PMT GCSE French summary series was located in the reviewed subject indexes. StudyRocket's AQA French pages follow the older specification: their vocabulary/grammar explanations provide coverage prompts, while the current AQA 8652 Higher specification, prescribed vocabulary, phonics appendix, sound bank and sample materials control requirements. Older writing mark allocations, four-bullet 90-word tasks, single-photo speaking material and Section B listening comprehension instructions were rejected. Pluperfect and subjunctive material were not added as compulsory grammar. Selected examples practise skills; the decks do not replace the full official vocabulary list or real listening practice.

## Specific corrections and source cautions

- The existing French *chanson* flashcard wrongly said s is followed by a consonant. It follows the consonant n; the corrected explanation distinguishes it from s between vowels in *maison*.
- The existing *heureux* card now gives feminine singular, feminine plural and masculine plural explicitly, removing ambiguity.
- StudyRocket's *Remains* page names Ted Hughes. The notes and cards correctly retain Simon Armitage, as in the official anthology. No unsupported compassionate-leave detail or extra *Kamikaze* stanza was imported.
- PMT's Macbeth overview overstates possession and the final coronation. The notes distinguish the doctor's observations from proof of possession and Malcolm's promised restoration from a reign the audience actually sees.
- Overconfident source rules about third-person omniscience, tense effects, poetic patterns and a universal response to techniques were not adopted. Interpretations remain tied to the particular writing.
- StudyRocket's Inspector historical page incorrectly expands the 1912 miners' legislation into a minimum wage for all workers. This claim was not imported.
