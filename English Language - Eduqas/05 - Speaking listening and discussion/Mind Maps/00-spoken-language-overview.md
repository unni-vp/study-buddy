# Speaking, listening and discussion

## Focus the purpose

- **Topic and audience:** agree a specific subject with your teacher. Explain why it matters to these listeners.

- A question such as Should school offer a quiet space? gives the talk a clear direction.

## Develop the thinking

- **Claim + reason + example + significance:** explain each point, rather than listing opinions.

- Research claims carefully. Identify an illustration as an example, not a statistic about everyone.

## Organise the journey

- **Opening:** establish the question. **Development:** connect the reasons and a genuine objection.

- **Conclusion:** return to the purpose and a clear next step. Signpost changes naturally.

## Make it speakable

- **Language:** precise, appropriate and understandable on one listening.

- **Delivery:** audible voice, controlled pace, useful pauses and emphasis. Rehearse aloud.

## Listen and develop

- **Response:** answer the actual question, give a reason and add an example or qualification.

- Acknowledge missing evidence. Explain what would need checking instead of inventing a fact.

## Prepare the whole task

- **Rehearse:** check the route, length and clarity. Leave room for questions within the assessment.

- **Endorsement:** Pass, Merit or Distinction is separate from the written GCSE grade.
