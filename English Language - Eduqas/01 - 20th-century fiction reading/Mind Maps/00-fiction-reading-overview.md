# Fiction reading

## Locate the task

- **Focus:** identify the character, feeling, event or judgement being asked about.

- **Range:** choose evidence from the specified lines. Match the command, not a memorised formula.

## Retrieve and infer

- **Fact:** something directly stated. **Inference:** something supported by a detail.

- Separate distinct facts. Qualify an inference when the evidence permits more than one reading.

## Choose evidence

- **Short quotation:** select words you can explain. A precise reference to an action also supports a point.

- Explain the link between evidence and your interpretation. A quotation is not a finished explanation.

## Explain the craft

- **Language:** consider the particular verb, image, sound or dialogue.

- **Structure:** track focus, pace, revelation and change. Explain why the order matters here.

## Make a judgement

- **Evaluation:** answer the statement with a supported view. Use qualifications when the passage warrants them.

- Consider relevant evidence across the range. Do not invent a counterargument simply to sound balanced.

## Check the response

- **Specific effect:** explain what the reader understands, expects or questions at this point.

- Avoid retelling, unsupported certainty, empty device lists and evidence outside the range.
