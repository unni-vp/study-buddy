# Creative prose writing

## Choose the direction

- **Narrative or recount:** make events and a change clear. Fulfil the exact title, opening or situation.

- A small time span and a few characters can help control, but are choices rather than rules.

## Build a connected plot

- **Want → obstacle → choice → consequence.** The character’s response creates the development.

- One well-developed episode can be stronger than several rushed twists.

## Character through action

- **Contradiction:** a tired worker can be impatient yet choose to help.

- Show decisions, responses and telling speech. Do not announce every emotion or explain a moral.

## Setting with a purpose

- **Select details:** glasses bend a counter’s edge; height marks preserve a childhood.

- Description should support viewpoint, mood or action. Avoid a list of all five senses.

## Shape the reading

- **Viewpoint and time:** keep them clear. Summarise uneventful time and slow down an important moment.

- Change paragraphs when speaker, focus, time, place or action changes.

## Finish and refine

- **Ending:** show the consequence or changed understanding; an echoed detail can gain new meaning.

- Edit relevance, coherence, sentence boundaries, tense, punctuation and spelling.
