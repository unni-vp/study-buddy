# Transactional writing

## A job for the reader

- **Purpose:** inform, advise, argue, persuade, discuss or review. Decide what the reader should understand or do.
- **Audience + form + tone** shape your choices. An advice article needs help, not a sales pitch.

## Protect both tasks

- Two compulsory tasks: **20 marks each**. Communication/organisation 12; language accuracy 8.
- Recent papers suggest 300–400 words and 30 minutes each. Plan, write and leave checking time.

## Ideas need substance

- Choose distinct points. Ask **why, how, what example, what consequence?**
- A few developed ideas are stronger than repeating a general claim. Use credible details, not invented research.

## Give paragraphs jobs

- Opening → clear direction. Middle → develop ideas in a useful order. Ending → judgement, recommendation or next step.
- **Coherence:** whole piece makes sense. **Cohesion:** its parts connect.

## Voice & form

- Article: headline and connected prose. Letter: greeting, purpose and sign-off. Speech: words listeners can follow.
- Review: detail + judgement + reasons. Advice: action + how + why. Adapt register to the reader.

## Control, then edit

- Use precise vocabulary and varied sentences for meaning. Devices must serve the idea.
- Check relevance, development, paragraph links, tone, sentence boundaries, agreement, tense and spelling.
