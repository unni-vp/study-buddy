# Forms & audiences

## Article or guide

- Article: headline, engaging opening, developed paragraphs and an ending with a purpose.
- Guide: organise around the reader’s needs. **Action + how + why** turns a tip into useful advice.

## Letter

- State why you are writing, develop the case, finish with an appropriate request or conclusion.
- Named recipient: Yours sincerely. Unknown: Yours faithfully. Be firm and courteous; challenge ideas, not people.

## Speech or talk

- Address listeners and use words that sound natural aloud. Signpost changes so listeners can follow.
- Short emphasis can follow a longer explanation. A talk is written speech, not a list of stage directions.

## Review

- **Detail + judgement + reason.** Choose criteria such as accessibility, atmosphere, quality or value.
- Give strengths and limitations; recommend for a named type of reader. Avoid retelling the entire plot.

## Report or leaflet

- Report: purpose → grouped findings → implications → recommendation if requested.
- Leaflet: clear sections with useful developed information. Headings help navigation; decoration does not replace content.

## Adapt the same idea

- To a friend: warm and direct. To a headteacher: respectful, clear and specific.
- Keep the main purpose in view. Match details to what the reader can know, value and realistically do.
