# Non-fiction reading

## Understand the task

- **Identity and purpose:** use the introduction, date, audience and form. Do not invent a biography.

- **Command and range:** identify the focus and whether you need one text or both.

## Retrieve and infer

- **Retrieve:** select different relevant details. **Infer:** explain a meaning supported by those details.

- Use surrounding sentences for unfamiliar words. Attribute the writer’s claims accurately.

## Analyse the argument

- **Idea + evidence + choice + effect:** explain the precise words, tone or development.

- A command, anecdote or contrast matters because of what it does in this argument.

## Evaluate with support

- **Judgement:** weigh a view or its presentation using the relevant text.

- Qualify where appropriate. Separate a convincing example from proof that a claim applies to everyone.

## Synthesise information

- **Both sources:** bring together relevant ideas under the question’s shared focus.

- For why, give reasons. For how, give actions. Keep source identities clear.

## Compare meaningfully

- **Ideas and methods:** connect attitudes and explain how they are conveyed in each text.

- Develop similarities and differences with evidence from both. Avoid two disconnected summaries.
