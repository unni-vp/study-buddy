## What was reviewed

The revision notes were compared with PMT reference notes and relevant Study Rocket pages. The official specification for the project's board, qualification and tier controls inclusion. Revisions use original, complete explanations and preserve existing case-study evidence, practical methods, useful diagrams, tables and worked examples. New explanations are integrated into the relevant existing sections. Curated flashcards are retained in full, with reviewed additions and explicit corrections recorded separately.

Each topic contains `source-review.json`. This records the original reference URLs, local PDF paths and SHA-256 hashes, Study Rocket pages, sections refined, decisions about source errors or scope, flashcard counts and browser checks. `revision-flashcards.json` preserves the original curated deck and its additions when cards are regenerated. Source bibliographies remain in the documents and HTML but are hidden in normal reading and print.

The Q&A audit checked question/answer counts, mark-group structure and command-word indicators. Existing science banks contain groups from 1 to 6 marks; Geography uses 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 marks. This structural audit is not a claim that every possible past-paper question is represented. Existing Q&A content was preserved apart from explicit factual corrections recorded in topic metadata. Existing mindmaps were preserved.

## Source choices and limitations

- PMT offers AQA Biology Detailed Notes in the reviewed index, rather than a separate Summary Notes series. These original PDFs and the individual practical notes are retained.
- Chemistry uses PMT's individual subsection summaries and its general practical-skills notes. Study Rocket's AQA Chemistry index is labelled Combined Science but also includes some separate-science pages. The full 8462 specification determines required content.
- Physics uses all eight PMT topic summaries and all ten individual required-practical PDFs. Incorrect source instructions and equations were not imported into the student notes.
- PMT and Study Rocket do not provide an exact matching OCR A Geography GCSE course in the reviewed indexes. Overlapping materials from other boards supply coverage prompts; the local OCR J383 specification and confirmed school case studies control the notes. There is no separate PMT summary PDF for every skills or fieldwork topic.
- The unmodified PMT Ecosystems Detailed Notes PDF is approximately 138.7 MB, exceeding GitHub's single-file limit. It remains locally in Geography topic 04's Reference Materials folder. The exact file is ignored in Git; its public URL, hash and review decisions are committed. The hidden bibliography uses the public URL for the published site. Other retained reference PDFs are committed.

## Validation

Each topic was built and checked before its own commit and push. Checks covered all direct note groups at desktop and narrow-phone widths, loaded diagrams, local links, hidden source sections, complete print notes, preserved card counts, initial card focus and keyboard reveal/navigation. Final checks also exercise Q&A expand/collapse controls and the longest card in every deck at laptop and phone sizes. Mobile comparison tables retain readable text and use local scrolling when necessary.
