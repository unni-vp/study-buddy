# Wave and infrared practicals

## Ripple tank

Lamp and dipper reveal waves in shallow water.

Measure multiple wavelengths and calibrated frequency.

Six crests contain five wavelengths.

Keep depth fixed.

Repeat and use v = fλ.

## String waves

Generator drives a stretched string at known f.

Stationary loops span λ/2.

Find λ then v = fλ.

Keep string type and tension fixed.

Electronic timing is suitable for tiny sound delays.

## Reflection

Trace mirror. Mark narrow incident/reflected rays at two points.

Join points and draw normal.

**i = r** from the normal.

Compare smooth and rough surfaces.

Use fine marks and consistent eye position.

## Refraction

Trace block and incident/emerging rays.

Join entry and exit points inside block.

Draw normal and measure angles. Vary incidence and substance.

Slower at oblique entry: bends towards normal.

## IR emission

Leslie cube faces share temperature.

Same detector distance and angle. Repeat readings.

Matt black generally emits more

shiny metal less.

Do not confuse temperature with surface-finish effects.

## IR absorption

Matched samples, same mass and starting temperature.

Same lamp, distance, orientation and exposure time.

Compare rises and repeat. Control other energy transfers.

Avoid burns and keep electrical equipment dry.
