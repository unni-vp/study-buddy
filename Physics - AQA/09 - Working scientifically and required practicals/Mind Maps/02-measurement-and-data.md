# Measurement and data

## Accuracy and precision

**Accuracy:** close to true value.

**Precision:** values cluster closely.

A precise set can still have a systematic offset.

Resolution is the smallest distinguishable change.

## Repeat and reproduce

Same investigator and conditions: **repeatability**.

Different investigators or equipment: **reproducibility**.

Repeated readings reduce random variation in a mean.

They do not remove a fixed zero error.

## Types of error

Random: unpredictable variation. Repeat and average.

Systematic: consistent shift. Calibrate or correct cause.

Parallax: read at eye level.

Anomalies: investigate, repeat and justify exclusion.

## Tables and uncertainty

Headings give quantity and unit. Keep sensible precision.

Mean = sum/count. Range = maximum − minimum.

Half-range can estimate repeat uncertainty.

Percentage uncertainty = uncertainty/value ×100.

## Choose a graph

Bar chart: categories. Scatter graph: paired quantities.

Histogram: grouped numerical intervals.

Label axes and units. Choose regular, useful scales.

Independent variable usually goes on x.

## Interpret the graph

Use a best-fit line or curve, not automatic dot-to-dot.

Gradient = Δy/Δx. Use a large triangle.

Direct proportion needs a straight line through origin.

Use specific data and evaluate uncertainty.
