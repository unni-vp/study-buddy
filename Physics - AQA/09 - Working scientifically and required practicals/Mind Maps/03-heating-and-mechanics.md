# Heating and mechanical practicals

## Specific heat capacity

Measure mass, energy input and temperature rise.

Insulate and improve heater/probe contact.

**c = E/(mΔθ)**. Room losses tend to overestimate c.

Avoid hot heater and block.

## Insulation

Change material at equal thickness, or thickness of one material.

Same water mass, start temperature, container, lid and room.

Repeat temperature readings at equal times.

Smaller drop means less cooling under matched conditions.

## Density

**ρ = m/V.** Balance gives mass.

Regular solid: dimensions give volume. Irregular solid: displacement.

Liquid: subtract empty-container mass.

Match units, remove bubbles, read meniscus at eye level.

## Force and extension

Measure unloaded and loaded lengths. **e = loaded − unloaded**.

Known loads give F = mg, including hanger.

Plot F against e. Gradient in proportional region = k.

## Vary force

Trolley pulled by hanging mass; measure a electronically.

Transfer masses trolley to hanger to keep total moving mass fixed.

Plot a against driving force.

Minimise friction and secure the pulley.

## Vary mass

Keep hanging mass fixed and add trolley mass.

Total moving mass changes, driving weight stays fixed.

Plot a against 1/total moving mass. Repeat.

Measure before hanger hits floor. Catch trolley safely.
