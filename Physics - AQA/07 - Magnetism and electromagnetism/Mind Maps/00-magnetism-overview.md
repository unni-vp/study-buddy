# Magnetism overview

## Magnets and fields

**Like poles:** repel. **Unlike:** attract.

Permanent magnets retain magnetism. Induced magnets respond to an external field.

Outside a magnet, field runs N to S. A compass gives direction.

## Electromagnets

Current makes a magnetic field.

**Solenoid:** strong, uniform field inside.

Larger current, more turns per length and iron core strengthen it.

Reverse current to reverse poles.

## The motor effect

Current in a field gives force perpendicular to both.

**F = BIl** for a perpendicular wire. Units: N, T, A, m.

Left hand: field, current, force.

Motor and speaker use this effect.

## The generator effect

A changing linked field induces potential difference.

Current requires a complete circuit.

Faster change, stronger field and more turns increase induced pd.

Induced current opposes the original change.

## Generating devices

**Alternator:** AC, slip rings.

**Dynamo:** pulsing DC, split ring.

**Microphone:** sound moves a coil, inducing signal.

Generator input supplies the electrical energy.

## Transformers and Grid

AC in primary creates changing field in iron core, inducing secondary pd.

**Vₚ/Vₛ = nₚ/nₛ.**

Ideal: **VₚIₚ = VₛIₛ**.

Higher grid voltage lowers current for same power, reducing I²R loss.
