# Induction and generators

## Induced potential

Relative motion or changing field linkage induces pd.

**Current:** needs a complete circuit.

Stationary magnet and coil give no sustained induced pd.

Changing field can act on a stationary coil.

## Strength and direction

Move faster, use stronger magnet, or more coil turns for greater pd.

Reverse motion or poles: reverse induced polarity.

Induced current’s field **opposes the original change**.

Mechanical work supplies the electrical output.

## AC and DC output

Alternator: complete slip rings maintain each end’s connection.

Dynamo: split ring swaps external connections each half-turn.

## Reading generator graphs

**AC:** changes sign, above and below zero.

**DC:** stays one direction but can pulse.

Faster rotation raises frequency and usually peak pd.

Greater field or turns can raise amplitude without changing rotation frequency.

## Microphone

Sound vibrates diaphragm and coil in a magnetic field.

Changing linked field induces a changing electrical signal.

Uses the **generator effect**.

Frequency follows the sound vibrations.

## Compare with a speaker

**Speaker:** electrical variations cause force, motion and sound.

**Microphone:** sound-driven motion causes electrical variations.

Motor effect and generator effect are different mechanisms.

Neither creates extra energy.
