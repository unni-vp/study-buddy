# Transformers and the Grid

## Changing field link

Primary AC creates a changing field in an easily magnetised iron core.

This induces AC pd in the separate secondary coil.

## Turn ratio

**Vₚ/Vₛ = nₚ/nₛ.** Keep matching subscripts together.

More secondary turns: step-up. Fewer: step-down.

240 V, 300 primary turns, 30 secondary: **24 V**.

Steady DC does not sustain transformer action.

## Power and current

Ideal: **VₚIₚ = VₛIₛ**.

12 V at 4 A gives 48 W. At 240 V input, Iₚ = **0.20 A**.

Higher output voltage gives lower output current at equal power.

No extra power is created.

## Real transformer

**Efficiency = output power / input power.**

48 W output at 80% efficiency needs 60 W input.

240 V supply then draws **0.25 A**.

Do not assume ideal power balance if losses are given.

## Transmission reasoning

Step-up raises voltage. For the same **P = VI**, current decreases.

**Cable heating = I²R.** Lower current gives lower heating loss.

Half the current gives one quarter of loss for fixed R.

High voltage needs insulation and safe separation.

## From station to consumer

Power station, step-up, transmission cables, step-down, consumers.

Secondary and primary are linked magnetically, not by charges crossing the core.

Explain why voltage is raised and then reduced.

Use same transmitted power when comparing losses.
