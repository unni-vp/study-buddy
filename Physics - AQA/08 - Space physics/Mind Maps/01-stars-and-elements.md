# Stars and elements

## Starting stages

**Nebula:** gas and dust, mainly hydrogen.

Gravity contracts cloud, increasing temperature and pressure.

**Protostar:** heats until fusion starts.

## Two mass-dependent routes

Both routes share the main-sequence stage.

Initial mass determines the later route.

## Main-sequence stability

Hydrogen nuclei fuse into helium, releasing energy.

Inward gravity balances outward pressure effects maintained by fusion.

Stable means forces balance, not that fusion or gravity stop.

## Sun-like route

**Red giant:** outer layers expand as core fuel changes.

Outer material lost, leaving hot, dense **white dwarf**.

Cooling leads towards expected **black dwarf** over extremely long time.

No supernova in this route.

## Massive route

**Red supergiant** develops before collapse and **supernova**.

Remaining core becomes neutron star or black hole.

Remnant mass affects outcome.

Supernova ejects material into space.

## Creating elements

Fusion joins nuclei, making heavier elements up to iron in massive stars.

Supernova conditions can produce elements heavier than iron.

Explosion spreads elements for later stars and planets.

Nuclear fusion changes nuclei, unlike chemical reactions.
