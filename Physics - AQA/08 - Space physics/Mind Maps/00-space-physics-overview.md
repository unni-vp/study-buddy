# Space physics overview

## Nested systems

Sun, eight planets, dwarf planets and natural satellites form our solar system.

Solar system is part of the **Milky Way galaxy**.

Galaxy is one of many in the universe.

## Birth and balance

Gravity contracts gas and dust in a **nebula**.

Protostar heats until hydrogen fusion starts.

**Main sequence:** inward gravity balances outward effects maintained by fusion.

## Different star endings

**Sun-like:** red giant, white dwarf, eventual black dwarf.

**Much more massive:** red supergiant, supernova, neutron star or black hole.

Fusion builds elements. Supernovae produce heavy elements and spread material.

## Orbital motion

Gravity supplies inward force. Velocity is tangent.

Circular speed can be constant while direction and velocity change.

For same central body: smaller stable circular radius, faster orbit.

Natural and artificial satellites both orbit under gravity.

## Red-shift evidence

Matching spectral lines shift to **longer wavelengths**.

More distant galaxies generally recede faster and show greater shift.

Evidence supports an expanding universe and a hot, dense early state.

## Developing models

Supernova observations indicate **accelerating expansion**.

Dark matter has inferred gravitational effects.

Dark energy is associated with the unknown cause of acceleration.

New evidence can require models to change.
