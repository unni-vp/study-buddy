# Red-shift and expansion

## Observe a shift

Compare corresponding spectral lines with a laboratory pattern.

**Red-shift:** longer observed wavelengths.

## Distance and recession

Most distant galaxies recede.

Further galaxies generally have greater red-shift and recession speed.

Red-shift is not simply red colour or greater brightness.

Use the comparison or data given.

## Expanding space

Distance–speed pattern supports expansion of space itself.

Balloon dots help picture separations increasing.

The analogy does not put Earth at a special centre.

Tracing expansion backwards gives a more compact past.

## Big Bang model

Earlier universe: **extremely hot and dense**.

Model describes expansion from that state.

It is not a stellar explosion in already empty space.

Observations support and test the explanation.

## Accelerating expansion

Distant supernova observations indicate expansion speeding up over time.

This differs from the trend of recession speed with distance.

**Dark energy:** associated with unknown cause of acceleration.

Its name is not a complete explanation.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Dark matter is inferred from gravitational effects without expected visible radiation.

Some important causes remain unknown.

Models predict observations. Reliable mismatches can require revision.

Separate observation, interpretation and uncertainty.
