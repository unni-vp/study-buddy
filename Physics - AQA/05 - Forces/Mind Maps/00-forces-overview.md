# Forces overview

## Vectors & resultants

- Vectors have magnitude + direction; scalars have magnitude only. Force in N; weight **W = mg**.
- Resultant combines forces. Zero resultant → constant velocity, possibly zero. Resolve perpendicular components with scale drawings.

## Work & springs

- **W = Fs; F = ke; Eₑ = ½ke².** Use extension in m. Spring equations within proportional limit.
- Elastic returns to original shape; inelastic leaves deformation. Measure force/extension with ruler and known loads.

## Moments & pressure

- **M = Fd**, perpendicular d. Balance clockwise/anticlockwise moments. Levers/gears transmit turning.
- **p = F/A; p = hρg**. Greater depth → greater p; pressure difference gives upthrust.

## Motion & laws

- **v = s/t; a = Δv/t; v²−u² = 2as** for constant acceleration.
- Distance–time slope = speed; velocity–time slope = a, area = displacement. **F = ma**; third-law forces act on different objects.

## Stopping & falling

- Stopping = thinking + braking. Thinking = vt; braking transfers kinetic energy to thermal stores.
- Weight > drag → accelerates. Drag grows with speed until forces balance at terminal velocity.

## Momentum

- **p = mv; F = Δp/Δt.** Closed system: total momentum conserved. Use signed velocities.
- Airbags/mats increase stopping time, reducing average force for the same momentum change.
