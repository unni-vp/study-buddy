# Motion & graphs
## Distance vs displacement
- Distance: total path, scalar.
- Displacement: straight start-to-finish change with direction, vector.
- Round trip → zero displacement, nonzero distance.
- Average speed = total distance / total time.
## Useful estimates
- Walking ≈ 1.5 m/s
- running 3
- cycling 6
- sound in air 330. Conditions change values.
- **s = vt** at constant speed.
- Convert minutes to seconds and km to m before calculating.
## Velocity & acceleration
- Velocity = speed with direction.
- Circle: constant speed can still mean changing velocity.
- **a = (v−u)/t**, m/s².
- 5 → 17 m/s in 4 s gives 3 m/s².
## Uniform acceleration
- **v²−u² = 2as** only for constant a.
- From rest
- a=2 over 25 m gives v=10 m/s.
- Free fall near Earth ≈ 9.8 m/s² when air resistance is negligible.
## Distance–time
- Gradient = speed.
- Flat = rest
- straight slope = constant speed.
- Curved graph: draw tangent at required time
- gradient estimates instantaneous speed. Use a large gradient triangle.
## Velocity–time
- Gradient = acceleration.
- Flat = constant velocity.
- Signed area = displacement
- sum absolute areas for distance.
- Triangle + rectangle areas give movement. Count squares for a curved section if needed.
