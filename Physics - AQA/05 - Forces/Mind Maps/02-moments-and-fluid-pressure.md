# Moments & fluid pressure

## Moment

- **M = Fd**, in Nm
- d is perpendicular pivot-to-force-line distance.
- 20 N × 0.30 m = 6 Nm.
- Longer lever → less force for the same turning effect.

## Balance, levers & gears

- Clockwise moments = anticlockwise moments.
- 30 N at 0.20 m balances 15 N at 0.40 m.
- Adjacent gears rotate oppositely. Larger driven gear turns more slowly with greater turning effect in an ideal system.

## Pressure on an area

- Fluid = liquid or gas.
- Forces are normal to surfaces.
- **p = F/A**
- Pa = N/m².
- Smaller area → greater p for same F.
- **1 cm² = 10⁻⁴ m²**.

## Pressure with depth

- **p = hρg** for a liquid column
- increases with depth and density.
- 2 m water at g = 9.8 → 19,600 Pa above surface pressure.
- Δp = Δhρg
- add atmospheric p if total requested.

## Upthrust

- Greater pressure on bottom than top gives upward resultant.
- Floating at rest: **upthrust = weight**.
- Compare average density including air spaces
- hollow steel ships can float.

## Atmosphere

- Air molecules collide with surfaces. Higher altitude has less air above, lower density and lower pressure.
- For submerged objects, compare weight/upthrust and include other forces if present
- sinking does not mean zero upthrust.
