# Atoms & nuclear equations
## Count particles
- Proton +1, neutron 0, electron −1.
- Neutral: protons = electrons.
- Carbon-14: 6p, 8n, 6e. Isotopes share proton number
- ions differ in electron count.
## Size & levels
- Atom radius ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ m
- nucleus less than 1/10,000 of that, holding almost all mass.
- Absorb radiation → higher electron level. Emit → lower. Removing an electron ionises the atom.
## Alpha scattering
- Most pass straight → mostly empty space. Some deflect → positive repulsion.
- Very few return → mass and charge concentrated in tiny nucleus
- plum pudding fails.
## Models develop
- Indivisible sphere → electron discovery → plum pudding → nuclear atom.
- Bohr adds specific electron levels
- later proton identification
- Chadwick gives neutron evidence.
## Alpha & beta-minus
- Alpha is ⁴₂He: daughter **A−4, Z−2**.
- Beta-minus: neutron → proton + emitted electron
- daughter **A unchanged, Z+1**. Electron is from nucleus, not shell.
## Balance both numbers
- ²³⁸₉₂U → ²³⁴₉₀Th + ⁴₂He.
- ¹⁴₆C → ¹⁴₇N + ⁰₋₁e. Totals of A and Z match on both sides.
- Gamma loses energy with no change in A or Z.
