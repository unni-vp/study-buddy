# Atomic structure overview
## Particles & isotopes
- Tiny positive nucleus: protons + neutrons
- negative electrons in energy levels.
- **Z = protons**
- **A = protons + neutrons.**
- Isotopes differ in neutrons.
- Losing electrons forms positive ions.
## Evidence changes models
- Electron → plum pudding. Alpha scattering → tiny dense nucleus, mostly empty space.
- Bohr: specific levels
- Chadwick: neutrons. Absorb radiation → electron rises
- emit → falls.
## Decay & equations
- Random nuclear decay.
- Activity in **Bq = decays/s**
- detector count rate may be lower.
- Alpha: A−4, Z−2. Beta-minus: A unchanged, Z+1.
- Gamma: A and Z unchanged.
## Half-life
- Time for activity/undecayed nuclei to halve. After n half-lives, **(½)ⁿ remains**.
- Subtract background first. After 3 half-lives, 1/8 remains and 7/8 has decayed.
## Hazards & uses
- Contamination = radioactive material present
- irradiation = exposure.
- Alpha especially harmful inside.
- Gamma tracers
- targeted treatment. Match half-life and penetration to use
- balance benefit and dose.
## Fission & fusion
- Fission: large nucleus absorbs neutron → splits, releasing energy, neutrons and gamma.
- Neutrons can cause a chain reaction.
- Fusion: light nuclei join
- some mass becomes radiation energy.
