# Waves overview

## Wave measurements

**Transverse:** perpendicular vibrations.

**Longitudinal:** parallel vibrations, compressions and rarefactions.

Waves transfer energy, not matter overall.

**v = fλ. T = 1/f.** Use m, s and Hz.

## Boundaries

**Reflection:** i = r, measured from normal.

**Refraction:** speed changes. Oblique rays change direction.

Frequency stays constant. Slower speed means shorter wavelength.

Absorption transfers energy to a material.

## Sound and Earth

**Hearing:** about 20 Hz–20 kHz. Ultrasound is above 20 kHz.

**Echo distance:** vt/2. Divide for the return journey.

**P-waves:** solids and liquids. **S-waves:** solids, not liquids.

## Electromagnetic spectrum

Radio, microwave, IR, visible, UV, X-ray, gamma: increasing frequency.

Vacuum speed: **3.0 × 10⁸ m/s**. All are transverse.

Match uses to absorption, transmission and risk.

## Lenses and colour

**Convex:** converges rays. Can make real or virtual images.

**Concave:** virtual, upright, smaller for a real object.

**Magnification:** image height / object height.

Objects reflect colour. Filters transmit it.

## Infrared and practicals

All objects emit and absorb IR. Hotter objects emit more.

**Balance:** equal absorbed and emitted power means constant temperature.

Compare surface finish at equal temperature and detector distance.
