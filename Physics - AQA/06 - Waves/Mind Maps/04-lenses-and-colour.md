# Lenses and colour

## Lens basics

**Convex:** converging. Parallel rays meet at far focus.

**Concave:** diverging, as if from near focus.

**Focal length:** lens to principal focus.

Real rays meet. Virtual rays only appear to meet.

## Convex image

Ray 1: parallel then through far F.

Ray 2: through centre, straight.

At 2F, image is real, inverted and same size.

## Object position

**Beyond 2F:** smaller real image.

**Between F and 2F:** larger real image.

**At F:** parallel outgoing rays, no finite screen image.

**Inside F:** upright, enlarged virtual image on object side.

## Concave and magnification

Parallel ray diverges as if from near F. Centre ray stays straight.

Extend rays backwards with dashed lines.

**Concave image:** virtual, upright, smaller.

**Magnification:** image height/object height. No unit. Match height units.

## Objects and filters

**Opaque colour:** reflected wavelengths reach eye. Others are absorbed.

White reflects visible colours. Black absorbs most.

**Filter colour:** transmitted wavelengths. Filters do not create light.

Red object under only blue light looks dark.

## Light and surfaces

**Transparent:** transmits with little scattering.

**Translucent:** transmits and scatters, blurring a view.

**Smooth:** specular reflection. **Rough:** diffuse.

Ideal red then blue filters transmit very little white light.
