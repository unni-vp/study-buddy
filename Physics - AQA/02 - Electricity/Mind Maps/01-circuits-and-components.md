# Circuits & components

## Charge carries energy

- **Q = It**
- **E = QV.**
- C = A × s
- V = J/C.
- Conventional current: + to − outside supply.
- Metal electrons move oppositely. A cell provides pd
- it does not create charge.

## Resistance

- **R = V/I** in Ω.
- At fixed V, greater R gives smaller I.
- Ammeter in series, voltmeter across component. Use standard symbols
- a dot marks a junction.

## One loop: series

- Same current throughout.
- Supply pd = sum of component pds.
- 4 Ω + 8 Ω across 12 V → 12 Ω total, 1 A
- voltage drops 4 V and 8 V.

## Branches: parallel

- Same pd across supply-connected branches
- total I = sum of branch currents.
- Extra paths reduce total R. One broken branch need not stop others. Appliances can switch independently.

## Resistor & lamp

- I vertical, V horizontal: ohmic line through origin at constant temperature
- slope = 1/R.
- Lamp heats as current rises → ions vibrate more → R increases
- curve flattens. Use V/I at a point.

## Diode & sensors

- Diode: high reverse R
- conducts forward with sufficient pd. LED emits light.
- Thermistor R decreases with temperature
- thermostat. LDR R decreases with light
- dark-triggered lamp controls.
