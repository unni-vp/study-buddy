# Electricity overview

## Current, pd & resistance

- **Q = It**
- **V = IR.**
- Current = charge per second.
- Pd = energy per coulomb.
- A complete circuit needs a source of pd. Charge is not used up.
- Ammeter in series
- voltmeter in parallel.

## Series vs parallel

- Series: same I, shared V
- **Rtotal = R₁ + R₂**.
- Parallel: same V, branch currents add. More paths reduce total resistance below the smallest branch R.

## Components

- Ohmic: constant-temperature straight I–V line. Lamp heats → R rises. Diode conducts one way.
- Thermistor R falls when hotter. LDR R falls when brighter
- both can be sensors.

## Circuit practicals

- Wire: vary length, measure V/I
- fix material, thickness and temperature. Switch off between readings.
- I–V: vary supply, record paired values, reverse polarity
- use protective resistor with diode.

## Power, mains & Grid

- **P = VI**
- **P = I²R**
- **E = Pt**
- **E = QV.**
- UK mains: 230 V, 50 Hz ac.
- Live brown
- neutral blue
- earth green/yellow. Grid: higher V → lower I at same power → less I²R heating.

## Static & fields

- Rubbing transfers electrons: gain → negative
- loss → positive. Like repel
- unlike attract.
- Field acts on charges without contact. Strong fields can ionise air → spark.
