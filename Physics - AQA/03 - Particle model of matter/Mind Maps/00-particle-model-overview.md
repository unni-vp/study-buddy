# Particle model overview

## States & density

- Solid: vibrate in fixed positions. Liquid: close, move past. Gas: far apart, random movement.
- **ρ = m/V.**
- Gas has low density from large spacing.
- Ice is less dense than liquid water.

## Density practical

- Balance → mass. Regular solid: dimensions → volume. Irregular solid: displaced water → volume.
- Liquid: subtract empty-container mass. Read meniscus at eye level
- avoid trapped bubbles.

## Temperature changes

- Internal energy = total particle kinetic + potential energy.
- Temperature links to average kinetic energy.
- **ΔE = mcΔθ.**
- c: energy to warm 1 kg by 1°C, J/kg°C.

## State changes

- Physical and reversible
- mass conserved in a closed sample.
- **E = mL.**
- During melting/boiling, potential energy rises, temperature stays constant.
- Fusion: solid/liquid
- vaporisation: liquid/gas.

## Pressure from collisions

- Gas particles hit walls → force per area. Force is perpendicular to wall.
- At fixed volume and mass, warming → faster particles, more frequent/harder collisions → higher pressure.

## Compress a gas

- Fixed mass, constant temperature: **p₁V₁ = p₂V₂**.
- Smaller volume → more wall collisions → greater p.
- Rapid compression does work on gas → internal energy and temperature can increase.
