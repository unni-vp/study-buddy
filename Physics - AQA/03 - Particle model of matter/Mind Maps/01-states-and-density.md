# States & density

## Three states

- Solid: close, fixed arrangement
- vibration. Liquid: close, irregular, able to flow.
- Gas: widely spaced, random motion
- easily compressed. Individual particles do not expand when heated.

## Physical changes

- Melting/freezing
- boiling or evaporation/condensation
- sublimation solid → gas.
- No new substance
- reversing restores properties. Open-container mass loss may be escaping vapour.

## Regular & irregular solids

- Weigh solid.
- Cuboid volume = lwh
- cylinder = πr²h.
- Use ruler, callipers or micrometer.
- Irregular: fully submerge
- final water volume − initial = solid volume.
- Displacement can is an alternative.

## Liquids & good readings

- Weigh empty dry cylinder, then cylinder + liquid. Subtract to find liquid mass.
- Read meniscus at eye level. Avoid bubbles/splashes
- unsuitable for solids that dissolve or absorb water.

## Density equation

- **ρ = m/V**
- **m = ρV**
- **V = m/ρ.**
- 240 g / 80 cm³ = 3.0 g/cm³ = **3,000 kg/m³**.
- 1 cm³ = 10⁻⁶ m³.
- Keep units consistent.

## Explain changes

- Fixed mass spreads over larger volume → lower density. Gas spacing explains low density.
- Do not claim every solid is denser than its liquid: ice is an exception. Repeats assess random variation
- check zero errors.
