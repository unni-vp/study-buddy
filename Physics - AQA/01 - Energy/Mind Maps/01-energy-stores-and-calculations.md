# Stores & calculations
## Describe a change
- Thrown ball: kinetic → gravitational
- air resistance also warms surroundings.
- Brakes: kinetic → thermal.
- Kettle: electrical transfer → water’s thermal store.
- In collisions, kinetic energy may decrease while total energy is conserved.
## Conservation
- Closed system: no net change in total energy. Include surroundings receiving transfers.
- Dissipation spreads energy into less useful stores. “Wasted” does not mean destroyed.
## Motion & height
- **Eₖ = ½mv²**
- **Eₚ = mgΔh.**
- Use given g.
- A 2 kg object at 3 m/s has 9 J.
- Rearrange: **v = √(2Eₖ/m)**.
- Double speed → four times kinetic energy.
## Springs
- **Eₑ = ½ke²** within proportional limit.
- e = stretched length − original length, in metres.
- k = 200 N/m
- e = 0.050 m → Eₑ = 0.25 J.
- Square the converted extension.
## Power is a rate
- **P = E/t**
- **P = W/t**
- **W: work done (J)**
- **E = Pt.**
- 1,800 J in 30 s → 60 W.
- 1 kW = 1,000 W
- 1 kJ = 1,000 J.
- Powerful does not automatically mean efficient.
## Efficiency
- Useful output / total input, for energy or power. No unit
- at most 1 or 100%.
- 350 J useful from 500 J → 70%
- 150 J dissipated. Lubrication reduces friction and unwanted heating.
