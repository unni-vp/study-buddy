# Energy overview

## Stores → transfers

- **Energy is conserved.** Stores include kinetic, gravitational, elastic, thermal, chemical and nuclear.
- Transfers: mechanically, electrically, by heating or radiation. Light and sound are not stores.

## Calculate store changes

- **Eₖ = ½mv²; Eₚ = mgΔh; Eₑ = ½ke².** Use kg, m, m/s and N/m.
- Spring: extension, not total length; stay within proportional limit. Doubling v or e quadruples energy.

## Power & efficiency

- **P = E/t = W/t.** Watts = joules per second. Same work in less time → greater power.
- Efficiency = useful output / total input; ×100 for %. Dissipated energy is less useful, not destroyed.

## Heating & insulation

- **ΔE = mcΔθ.** Temperature change, not final temperature. Measure mass, energy and temperature rise.
- Insulation: low conductivity and greater thickness reduce transfer. Compare cooling with controls.

## Resources & reliability

- Non-renewable: coal, oil, gas, nuclear. Renewable: biofuel, wind, Sun, tides, waves, hydro, geothermal.
- Uses: heating, transport, electricity. Wind/Sun vary; tides are predictable but not continuous.

## Evaluate a choice

- Compare emissions, waste, habitat effects, cost, reliability and suitable sites.
- A resource mix helps match demand. Science informs decisions; economic and social choices matter.
