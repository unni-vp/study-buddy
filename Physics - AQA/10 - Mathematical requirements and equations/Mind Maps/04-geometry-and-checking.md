# Geometry and physical checks

## Angles

Normal is 90° to a surface.

Reflection: i = r from normal.

Perpendicular moment distance is pivot to force’s line of action.

Draw vectors to scale, preserving directions.

Do not add angled force magnitudes as if along one line.

## Area and volume

Rectangle: lw. Triangle: ½bh.

Cube: volume s³, total surface area 6s².

Cuboid volume: lwh.

A 2 cm cube has 8 cm³ volume and 24 cm² surface area.

## SI dimensions

2 cm = **0.020 m**.

Cube volume = **8 × 10⁻⁶ m³**.

Surface area = **2.4 × 10⁻³ m²**.

Tiny dimension errors can matter for small objects.

## Condition checks

v² − u² = 2as needs constant acceleration.

Gas pV requires fixed mass and temperature.

F = BIl needs perpendicular field/current.

Transformer equal-power relation assumes ideal efficiency.

## Interpret the answer

Correct unit, sign and sensible precision.

Mass is kg

weight is N. Work is J

moment is Nm.

Temperature rise and extension are changes.

Echo distance is half total travelled distance.

## Sanity checks

Efficiency at most 100%. Input exceeds useful output with losses.

At fixed force, larger mass gives smaller acceleration.

Check squares, roots, time conversions and powers of ten.

Plausibility supports a check

it does not replace working.
