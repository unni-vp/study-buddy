# Equation toolkit

## Stores and thermal energy

**Eₖ = ½mv².**

**Eₚ = mgh.**

**Eₑ = ½ke².**

**ΔE = mcΔθ:** temperature rise.

**E = mL:** state change.

Add separate stages if both happen.

## Power and electricity

**P = E/t**

**P = W/t**

**W: work done (J).**

**E = Pt.**

Efficiency: useful output/total input, energy or power.

**Q = It.**

**V = IR.**

**P = VI**

**P = I²R.**

**E = QV.**

Use seconds, A, V, Ω, W and J.

## Matter and pressure

**ρ = m/V.**

**p = F/A.**

**p = hρg.**

Convert area and volume carefully.

**p₁V₁ = p₂V₂:** fixed gas mass and temperature.

Check whether liquid-column or total pressure is requested.

## Forces and motion

**W = mg.**

**Work = Fs.**

**F = ke.**

**M = Fd.**

**s = vt.**

**a = (v − u)/t.**

**v² − u² = 2as.**

**F = ma.**

Use resultant force, extension and perpendicular distance.

**p = mv.**

**F = Δp/Δt.**

Include direction.

## Waves and images

**T = 1/f.**

**v = fλ.**

Echo distance = vt/2, because time covers both journeys.

Magnification = image height/object height.

No unit.

Frequency stays constant across a medium boundary.

## Magnetism and links

**F = BIl** for a perpendicular conductor.

**Vₚ/Vₛ = nₚ/nₛ.**

Ideal: VₚIₚ = VₛIₛ.

Energy to useful power to input power uses linked equations.

Real losses need efficiency, not ideal balance.
