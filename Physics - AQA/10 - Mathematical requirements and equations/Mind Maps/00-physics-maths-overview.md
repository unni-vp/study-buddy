# Physics maths overview

## Plan the calculation

Identify unknown and known quantities with units.

Choose relationship and check conditions.

Convert, rearrange, substitute, calculate and check.

An equation sheet does not choose the method for you.

## Numbers and units

Use standard form with 1 ≤ coefficient < 10.

kilo 10³, milli 10⁻³, micro 10⁻⁶, nano 10⁻⁹.

Area conversion squares the scale factor. Volume cubes it.

Round final answer, not every intermediate step.

## Relationships

Direct proportion: straight line through origin.

Inverse: doubling one halves the other.

Squared: doubling variable multiplies result by four.

Conditions include constant temperature or acceleration and ideal transformers.

## Graph tools

Gradient = Δy/Δx, with y-unit/x-unit.

Tangent gives rate at one point on a curve.

Velocity–time signed area gives displacement.

Force–extension area gives work transferred.

## Data summaries

Mean, median, mode and range answer different questions.

Percentage change uses original denominator.

Frequency-table mean is weighted.

Correlation alone does not prove cause.

## Geometry and checking

Use perpendicular moment distance and angles from normal.

Areas and volumes need matching units.

Check physical meaning, direction and plausible size.

Efficiency cannot exceed 100%.
