# Ratio proportion and rates of change: questions and answers

## 1 mark

**1.** Simplify the ratio 0.75:1.25.

**Answer:** 75:125 = 3:5.

**2.** Write the multipliers for an increase of 18% and a decrease of 18%.

**Answer:** Increase: 1.18. Decrease: 0.82.

**3.** A = 12 and B = 8. Express A as a fraction of B.

**Answer:** A/B = 12/8 = 3/2. A fraction can be greater than 1.

## 2 marks

**4.** Divide £420 in the ratio 2:5.

**Answer:**

- There are 7 parts, so each part is £60.
- The shares are £120 and £300.

**5.** Convert 2.4 m² to cm², and 0.003 m³ to litres.

**Answer:**

- 1 m² = 10000 cm², so 2.4 m² = 24000 cm².
- 1 m³ = 1000 litres, so 0.003 m³ = 3 litres.

**6.** A map has scale 1:25000. A path is 7.2 cm long on the map. Find its real length in kilometres.

**Answer:**

- Real length = 7.2 × 25000 = 180000 cm.
- 100000 cm = 1 km, so the path is 1.8 km long.

**7.** A price rises from £80 to £92. Find the percentage increase.

**Answer:**

- The increase is £12. Divide by the original price: 12/80.
- (12/80) × 100 = 15%.

**8.** After a 20% discount, a coat costs £68. Find its original price.

**Answer:**

- The sale price is 80% of the original.
- Original price = £68 ÷ 0.8 = £85.

**9.** £800 is invested at 3% simple interest per year for 5 years. Find the final amount.

**Answer:**

- Annual interest = 0.03 × £800 = £24.
- Five years add £120, giving £920. Simple interest is calculated from the original capital each year.

**10.** A recipe for 6 people uses 450 g of flour. How much flour is needed for 10 people at the same rate?

**Answer:**

- The scale factor is 10/6.
- 450 × 10/6 = 750 g.

**11.** A straight-line graph passes through the origin and (4,10). Write y in terms of x, and explain why it represents direct proportion.

**Answer:**

- Gradient = 10/4 = 2.5, so y = 2.5x.
- The ratio y/x is constant for x ≠ 0, and the graph passes through the origin.

## 3 marks

**12.** y is inversely proportional to x². When x = 3, y = 8. Find y when x = 6.

**Answer:**

- Write y = k/x².
- k = 8 × 3² = 72.
- At x = 6, y = 72/36 = 2.

**13.** y is directly proportional to x². When x = 4, y = 48. Find the positive x when y = 75.

**Answer:**

- y = kx² and k = 48/16 = 3.
- 75 = 3x², so x² = 25.
- The requested positive value is x = 5.

**14.** A cyclist travels 36 km in 1 hour 12 minutes. Find the average speed in km/h and m/s.

**Answer:**

- 1 hour 12 minutes = 1.2 hours.
- Speed = 36/1.2 = 30 km/h.
- Convert using 30 × 1000/3600 = 8.333… m/s, or 8.33 m/s to 3 significant figures.

**15.** A metal block has mass 540 g and volume 200 cm³. Find its density in g/cm³ and kg/m³.

**Answer:**

- Density = mass/volume = 540/200 = 2.7 g/cm³.
- 1 g = 0.001 kg and 1 cm³ = 0.000001 m³.
- Therefore 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³, giving 2700 kg/m³.

**16.** Shop A sells 750 g of cereal for £2.70. Shop B sells 1.2 kg for £4.08. Which is better value by mass? Show working.

**Answer:**

- Shop A costs £2.70/0.75 = £3.60 per kg.
- Shop B costs £4.08/1.2 = £3.40 per kg.
- Shop B is better value, assuming the cereal is comparable and the larger pack can be used.

**17.** Two similar solids have corresponding lengths in the ratio 2:3. The smaller has surface area 40 cm² and volume 80 cm³. Find the larger surface area and volume.

**Answer:**

- The length scale factor is 3/2.
- Area scale factor = (3/2)² = 9/4, so area = 90 cm².
- Volume scale factor = (3/2)³ = 27/8, so volume = 270 cm³.

**18.** £1500 earns 4% compound interest each year. Find its value after 3 years, to the nearest penny.

**Answer:**

- Use the annual multiplier 1.04.
- Value = £1500 × 1.04³ = £1687.296.
- The final amount is £1687.30. Keep unrounded values until the final money rounding.

**19.** A machine loses 12% of its value each year. Its initial value is £20000. Find its value after 2 years.

**Answer:**

- The yearly multiplier is 1 − 0.12 = 0.88.
- Value = £20000 × 0.88².
- The result is £15488.

**20.** A tangent to a volume-time curve passes through (2,15) and (8,39). Time is in minutes and volume is in litres. Estimate the instantaneous rate at the tangent point. Explain the units.

**Answer:**

- Tangent gradient = (39 − 15)/(8 − 2).
- This is 24/6 = 4 litres per minute.
- It estimates the rate at that moment, rather than necessarily the average rate over a longer interval.

## 4 marks

**21.** A:B = 2:3 and B:C = 4:5. Their total is 350. Find A, B and C.

**Answer:**

- Make the B parts match: A:B = 8:12 and B:C = 12:15.
- A:B:C = 8:12:15, containing 35 parts.
- One part is 350/35 = 10.
- A = 80, B = 120 and C = 150.

**22.** A shop increases a price by 25%, then reduces the new price by 20%. A student says the final price is 5% higher than the original. Is the student correct?

**Answer:**

- The multipliers are 1.25 and 0.80.
- Their product is 1.25 × 0.80 = 1.
- The final price equals the original price, so the student is incorrect.
- The percentages act on different base amounts; adding 25 and −20 does not give the combined change.

**23.** Six identical pumps empty a tank in 15 hours. How long would ten pumps take? State two assumptions of the model.

**Answer:**

- For identical pumps working at a constant rate, pump number × time is constant.
- 6 × 15 = 90 pump-hours.
- Ten pumps take 90/10 = 9 hours.
- Assume each pump has the same constant output and their outputs add without interference. The tank contains the same amount of water.

**24.** A force of 600 N acts uniformly on a rectangular face 5 cm by 4 cm. Find the pressure in N/m².

**Answer:**

- Area = 20 cm².
- Since 1 cm² = 0.0001 m², the area is 0.002 m².
- Pressure = force/area = 600/0.002.
- The pressure is 300000 N/m².

## 5 marks

**25.** A drink contains concentrate and water in the ratio 1:4. There are 2 litres of drink. How much concentrate must be added to make concentrate 30% of the new mixture?

**Answer:**

- Initially there are 0.4 litres of concentrate and 1.6 litres of water.
- Let x litres of pure concentrate be added. The new total is 2 + x litres.
- Form (0.4 + x)/(2 + x) = 0.30.
- 0.4 + x = 0.6 + 0.3x, so 0.7x = 0.2.
- x = 2/7 litre, approximately 0.286 litre. The total volume changes when concentrate is added.

**26.** A savings account starts at £1000. At the end of each year it earns 5% interest, then £200 is withdrawn. Find the balances after 1 and 2 years. Write a recurrence for balance Bₙ and find the starting balance that would remain unchanged.

**Answer:**

- The operations occur in order: multiply by 1.05, then subtract £200.
- B₁ = 1.05 × 1000 − 200 = £850.
- B₂ = 1.05 × 850 − 200 = £692.50.
- The recurrence is Bₙ₊₁ = 1.05Bₙ − 200.
- For an unchanged balance B, B = 1.05B − 200. Hence 0.05B = 200 and B = £4000.

## 6 marks

**27.** A population is 2645 after two years of growth at the same annual percentage rate. It started at 2000. Find the annual growth rate. A second model predicts adding 300 people each year. Compare the two models after 3 years from the same starting value.

**Answer:**

- Let the annual multiplier be m. Then 2000m² = 2645.
- m² = 1.3225, so m = 1.15, taking the positive growth multiplier.
- The annual percentage growth rate is 15%.
- After 3 years the percentage model predicts 2000 × 1.15³ = 3041.75.
- The additive model predicts 2000 + 3 × 300 = 2900.
- The percentage model predicts 141.75 more before rounding. As whole-person estimates these are about 3042 and 2900. Neither model accounts for changing real-world conditions.
