# Speaking and reading aloud questions & answers

## 1 mark

**1.** Translate: où ?

**Answer:** Where?

**2.** Translate: quand ?

**Answer:** When?

**3.** Translate: pourquoi ?

**Answer:** Why?

**4.** Translate: combien ?

**Answer:** How much / how many?

**5.** Translate: au premier plan.

**Answer:** In the foreground.

**6.** Translate: à l'arrière-plan.

**Answer:** In the background.

**7.** How many photos are on the photo card?

**Answer:** Two.

**8.** Is reading the text aloud allowed in supervised preparation?

**Answer:** No.

**9.** Translate: ils ont l'air contents.

**Answer:** They look happy.

**10.** Translate: je voudrais.

**Answer:** I would like.

## 2 marks

**11.** Ask in French where the station is.

**Answer:** Où est la gare ?

**12.** Ask in French how much a ticket costs.

**Answer:** Combien coûte le billet ?

**13.** Ask in French when the train leaves.

**Answer:** À quelle heure part le train ?

**14.** Request a room for two people in French.

**Answer:** Je voudrais une chambre pour deux personnes.

**15.** Say in French that you prefer the train because it is comfortable.

**Answer:** Je préfère le train parce que c'est confortable.

**16.** Say in French that yesterday you visited a museum.

**Answer:** Hier, j'ai visité un musée.

**17.** Say in French that tomorrow you are going to play tennis.

**Answer:** Demain, je vais jouer au tennis.

**18.** Ask politely in French for repetition.

**Answer:** Vous pouvez répéter, s'il vous plaît ?

**19.** For a written scene prompt showing people playing in a park, give one accurate photo-description sentence.

**Answer:**

Sur la photo, les personnes jouent dans un parc.

Describe the supplied action and setting without inventing identities.

**20.** Explain why ils ont l'air contents is safer than stating the people's exact feelings as facts.

**Answer:** It means they **look** happy, so it presents an inference from their appearance rather than claiming certain knowledge of their feelings.

**21.** Why does a reply to pourquoi need more than j'aime le sport?

**Answer:** Pourquoi asks **why**. Add a reason, such as parce que c'est amusant.

**22.** Give two sound checks before reading aloud.

**Answer:** Check u/ou and nasal vowels; check silent endings and possible liaison.

## 3 marks

**23.** Answer in French: Qu'est-ce que tu as fait le week-end dernier ? Include an activity and a reaction with a reason.

**Answer:**

Le week-end dernier, j'ai joué au tennis avec mon ami. C'était amusant parce que nous avons gagné notre match.

---

The completed activity answers the past question; the reaction is developed with a reason.

**24.** Answer in French: Qu'est-ce que tu vas faire pendant les vacances ? Include a plan and a reason.

**Answer:**

Je vais visiter ma grand-mère pendant les vacances parce que j'aime passer du temps avec elle. Nous allons préparer un repas ensemble.

---

Vais visiter fits the future; the reason and shared activity develop the answer.

**25.** Explain three useful actions in the 15-minute preparation.

**Answer:**

- Read each role-play instruction and prepare the exact information or question.
- Read the aloud text silently and identify sound groups/phrasing.
- Prepare relevant visible details for both photos.

**26.** For two written scene prompts—people playing in a park; people eating inside—give one statement about each and a supported contrast.

**Answer:**

Sur la première photo, les personnes jouent dans un parc. Sur la deuxième photo, les personnes mangent à l'intérieur. La première scène est dehors, tandis que la deuxième est dedans.

---

Both scenes are addressed; the contrast follows the supplied locations.

## 4 marks

**27.** Answer in French: Que penses-tu de ton quartier ? Give a view, a reason, a local detail and a drawback.

**Answer:**

J'aime mon quartier parce qu'il est calme. Il y a un parc où je retrouve mes amis. Cependant, il n'y a pas assez de bus.

---

The opinion answers directly; a reason, an example and a contrast add relevant detail.

**28.** Correct four problems in this speaking plan: describe one photo only; read the text aloud in preparation; answer an 'ask a price' prompt with a price; memorise only one theme.

**Answer:**

- Describe **both** photos.
- Read silently in supervised preparation.
- Ask **Combien coûte... ?** when instructed to ask a price.
- Prepare to communicate across all three themes.

## 10 marks

**29.** Complete a practice station role-play in French: request a ticket to Paris; say you want to travel tomorrow; say you prefer the train because it is comfortable; say you travelled by car last week; ask the departure time.

**Answer:**

1. Je voudrais un billet pour Paris.
2. Je voudrais voyager demain.
3. Je préfère le train parce que c'est confortable.
4. La semaine dernière, j'ai voyagé en voiture.
5. À quelle heure part le train ?

---

Each reply fulfils one instruction. The past event uses the perfect; the final task is a question. Actual role-play marking considers the communication of each task.
