# Plant health

## Plant infections

- **TMV:** virus; mosaic leaf discolouration → less photosynthesis/growth. Limit transfer from infected plants/tools.
- **Rose black spot:** fungus; purple/black spots, yellow leaves, early leaf fall → less photosynthesis. Wind/water spread spores; remove affected leaves or use fungicides.

## Pests & symptoms

- **Aphids** feed on sugar-rich sap, reducing growth; may transmit viruses.
- Look for stunted growth, spots, decay, growths, malformed stems/leaves, discolouration and pests.
- A symptom alone may have several possible causes.

## Mineral deficiencies

- **Nitrate:** needed for amino acids/proteins. Shortage → stunted growth.
- **Magnesium:** needed for chlorophyll. Shortage → **chlorosis** and reduced photosynthesis.
- Deficiencies are not communicable; mineral supply will not cure a virus.

## Identify the problem

- Compare symptoms with reliable gardening references.
- Laboratories identify pathogens; **monoclonal-antibody kits** test for specific targets.
- Combine evidence; a negative test for one pathogen does not rule out other causes.

## Physical & chemical

- **Cellulose cell walls**, tough waxy cuticle and dead bark resist entry. Shedding bark can remove pathogens.
- Antibacterial chemicals act against bacteria. **Poisons deter herbivores**.

## Mechanical adaptations

- **Thorns and hairs** deter feeding.
- Leaves may **droop or curl** when touched.
- **Mimicry** tricks animals into avoiding plants. Link each feature to how it protects.
