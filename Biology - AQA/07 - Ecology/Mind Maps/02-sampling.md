# Sampling populations and distribution

## Random quadrats

Known quadrat and habitat areas.

## Consistent observations

Identify species accurately.

Use same quadrat size and boundary rule.

Many well-spread samples reduce bias.

Suitable for plants and slow-moving organisms.

## Population estimate

Mean = total count/number of quadrats.

Density = mean/quadrat area.

Estimate = density × habitat area.

Keep area units consistent.

## Transects

Lay line across environmental gradient.

Quadrats at regular intervals: systematic.

Record abundance and factor at each position.

Repeat along comparable parallel transects.

## Data and reliability

Mean, median, mode and range.

Plot abundance against distance/factor.

Consider patchiness and other influences.

Estimate is not an exact census.

## Worked scaling

Mean 5 in 0.25 m².

Density 20 plants m⁻².

100 m² habitat: 2000 plants estimated.

Respect habitat, safe access and hand hygiene.
