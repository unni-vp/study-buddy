# Biodiversity and human impacts

## Why diversity matters

Variety of species in a habitat or Earth.

Alternative food/shelter links support stability.

Population growth increases resource use and waste.

Protecting diversity supports human wellbeing.

## Pollution

Water: sewage, fertiliser, toxic chemicals.

Air: smoke and acidic gases.

Land: landfill and toxic chemicals.

Damage or deaths reduce biodiversity.

## Land and peat

Building, quarrying, farming remove habitat.

Peat extraction destroys specialist habitats.

Decay/burning releases stored carbon dioxide.

Peat-free alternatives reduce extraction pressures.

## Forests and climate

Clearing for cattle, rice and biofuel crops.

Habitat loss and carbon dioxide release.

Fewer trees remove carbon dioxide.

Carbon dioxide/methane warming shifts species ranges.

## Protection measures

Endangered breeding and rare-habitat restoration.

Hedgerows/field margins support more species.

Reduce deforestation and emissions.

Recycle rather than increase extraction/landfill.

## Evaluate competing needs

Compare biodiversity, food, jobs and cost.

Use before/after data and suitable comparisons.

Local uncertainties differ from overall evidence.

Give a supported judgement for the stated setting.
