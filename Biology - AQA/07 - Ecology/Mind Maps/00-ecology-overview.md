# Ecology

## Community relationships

Population: one species. Community: many.

Ecosystem includes abiotic surroundings.

Competition for limited resources.

Interdependence links species.

## Factors and adaptations

Abiotic: light, temperature, water, soil, gases.

Biotic: food, predators, pathogens, competitors.

Structural, behavioural, functional adaptations.

Environmental changes alter distribution.

## Sampling

Random quadrats estimate abundance.

Mean × habitat area/quadrat area.

Transects investigate gradients systematically.

Repeat and assess representativeness.

## Cycling material

Photosynthesis takes in carbon dioxide.

Feeding, decay and respiration move carbon.

Water evaporates, condenses and precipitates.

Materials cycle. Energy transfers to surroundings.

## Biodiversity

Pollution and habitat loss reduce variety.

Peat stores carbon and supports species.

Deforestation and warming affect habitats.

Protection, margins and recycling can help.

## Biomass and food

Biomass decreases along trophic levels.

Transfer % = next/previous biomass ×100.

Food security needs sustainable supply.

Farming, fishing and biotechnology need evaluation.
