# Bioenergetics overview

## Photosynthesis

- **Carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen.** Light transfers energy to chloroplasts; chlorophyll absorbs it.
- **Endothermic:** energy is taken in. Plants respire all the time; photosynthesis needs light.

## Use the glucose

- Respiration; starch and fat/oil storage; cellulose cell walls.
- Glucose plus nitrate-derived nitrogen → amino acids → proteins.

## Limiting factors

- Light, CO₂, temperature and chlorophyll can limit rate. A plateau means extra light alone will not help.
- **Intensity ∝ 1/distance²**; doubling distance quarters intensity.
- Greenhouses: balance extra crop income against heat/light/CO₂ costs.

## Pondweed practical

- Change lamp distance; measure oxygen volume/time. Control temperature, CO₂ supply, plant and background light.
- Acclimatise; repeat; calculate mean. Bubbles vary in size, so volume is usually better.
- Rate = volume/time; plot against light intensity or 1/distance².

## Respiration pathways

- **Aerobic:** glucose + O₂ → CO₂ + water; more energy transferred.
- **Anaerobic:** muscles → lactic acid; yeast/plants → ethanol + CO₂. Less energy per glucose.
- Respiration is exothermic and continuous; fermentation makes bread rise and produces alcohol.

## Exercise & metabolism

- Exercise raises heart rate, breathing rate/volume. Oxygen shortage → more anaerobic respiration and fatigue.
- Oxygen debt: extra oxygen needed afterwards. Blood takes lactic acid to liver → glucose.
- Metabolism = all cell/body reactions; builds carbohydrates, lipids and proteins; excess nitrogen → urea.
