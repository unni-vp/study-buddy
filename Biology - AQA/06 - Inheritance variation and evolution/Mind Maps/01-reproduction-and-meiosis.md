# Reproduction and meiosis

## Sexual reproduction

Gametes fuse, mixing genetic information.

Variation helps survival under change.

Mate finding, time and energy are costs.

## Asexual reproduction

One parent, no gamete fusion.

Mitosis makes genetically identical offspring.

Rapid reproduction in favourable conditions.

Little variation can increase shared vulnerability.

## Chromosome sequence

Copy once, then divide twice.

One chromosome pair shown below.

## Fertilisation and growth

Human gametes usually have 23 chromosomes.

Fusion restores 46 in fertilised egg.

Mitosis grows embryo. Cells differentiate.

Joined copies count as one replicated chromosome.

## Organism examples

Malaria: asexual in humans, sexual in mosquito.

Fungi: asexual spores and sexual reproduction.

Flowering plants: sexual seeds.

Strawberry runners, daffodil bulbs: asexual.

## Compare precisely

Sexual offspring differ from parents.

Clones can still experience different environments.

Variation affects population survival, not a guarantee.

Asexual reproduction does not require meiosis.
