# Inheritance, variation and evolution

## Reproduction

Sexual: gamete fusion creates variation.

Asexual: one parent, no fusion, clones.

Meiosis halves chromosome number.

Fertilisation restores it.

## DNA and proteins

Genome: entire genetic material.

Genes code for amino-acid sequences.

Base order affects protein shape/function.

Most mutations have little or no effect.

## Inheritance

Alleles: versions of a gene.

Genotype: allele combination.

Phenotype: expressed characteristic.

Punnett crosses predict probabilities, not certainties.

## Selection and new species

Inherited variation and selection pressures.

Survivors reproduce and pass on alleles.

Populations evolve over generations.

Isolation can prevent fertile interbreeding.

## Changing genes and copying

Selective breeding chooses parents/offspring.

Genetic engineering transfers a gene.

Cloning copies a source genome.

Evaluate benefits, diversity, costs and risks.

## Evidence and relationships

Fossils and DNA support shared ancestry.

Resistance evolves through selection.

Genus + species: binomial name.

Three domains: Archaea, Bacteria, Eukaryota.
