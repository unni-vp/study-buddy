# Inheritance and probability

## Essential terms

Allele: version of a gene.

Genotype: allele combination.

Phenotype: expressed characteristic.

Homozygous: same alleles. Heterozygous: different.

## Dominant and recessive

Dominant expressed with one copy.

Recessive expressed with two copies in simple model.

Dominant does not mean common or stronger.

Many characteristics involve several genes.

## Carrier cross

Aa × Aa: both parents produce A or a.

## Inherited conditions

CF: recessive. Two carriers can have affected child.

Polydactyly: dominant allele.

Family trees require linked parent/child reasoning.

Skipped generations alone is weak evidence.

## Sex inheritance

Usual model: XX female, XY male.

Eggs X. Sperm X or Y.

XX/XY each probability 1/2.

Chance does not guarantee equal family counts.

## Interpret and evaluate

Expected number: probability × sample size.

1/4 affected means affected:unaffected 1:3.

Embryo screening can inform choices.

Evaluate severity, uncertainty, cost and ethics.
