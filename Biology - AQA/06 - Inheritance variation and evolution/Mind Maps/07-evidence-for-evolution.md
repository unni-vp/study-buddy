# Evidence, inheritance discoveries and extinction

## Darwin and Wallace

Both proposed natural selection.

Darwin published Origin of Species in 1859.

Wallace’s work prompted joint publication.

Later evidence helped acceptance.

## Earlier explanations

Lamarck: acquired changes passed to offspring.

This is generally unsupported genetically.

Darwin challenged fixed-creation beliefs.

Early evidence and inheritance knowledge were limited.

## Mendel to DNA

Pea crosses suggested inherited units.

Later chromosome studies supported gene theory.

DNA structure and function explained inheritance.

New evidence develops scientific explanations.

## Fossil formation

Hard parts mineralised.

Decay prevented: amber, ice, suitable conditions.

Traces: footprints, burrows.

Soft bodies and geological change leave gaps.

## Evidence and extinction

Fossils show life, change and extinction.

DNA similarities support shared ancestors.

Change, disease, predators or competition can cause extinction.

No survivors remain in an extinct species.

## Resistance and responses

Selection increases resistant bacterial populations.

Resistant strains can spread between people.

Restrict unnecessary antibiotic use.

New drug development is slower than bacterial reproduction.
