# DNA, genes and proteins

## Information packaging

DNA forms a double helix.

Chromosomes contain many genes.

Gene: codes for an amino-acid sequence.

Genome includes coding and non-coding DNA.

## DNA building blocks

Unwound segment: paired bases and backbone.

## From code to chain

Three bases code for one amino acid.

Template reaches ribosome.

Carrier molecules bring amino acids.

Amino acids join in specified order.

## Shape and function

Chain folds into a specific shape.

Enzyme: active site fits substrate.

Hormone: binds suitable receptors.

Structural proteins provide support.

## Variants and expression

Coding variants can change amino-acid sequence.

Shape and activity may change.

Non-coding variants can affect expression.

Most mutations have no or small effects.

## Genome research

Identify genes linked to disorders.

Understand inherited disease and treatments.

Trace historical human migration.

DNA is the chemical, not another organelle.
