# Mathematical requirements questions & answers

## 1 mark

**1.** Convert 2 mm to micrometres.

**Answer:** 2000 µm.

**2.** Write 0.000004 m in standard form.

**Answer:** 4 ×10⁻⁶ m.

**3.** How many significant figures are in 0.00620?

**Answer:** Three.

**4.** What is the range of 5, 6, 8 and 12?

**Answer:** 7.

**5.** What is the probability of an impossible event?

**Answer:** Zero.

**6.** What does ∝ mean?

**Answer:** Proportional to.

**7.** Which graph shows paired numerical measurements to identify a correlation?

**Answer:** Scatter diagram.

**8.** Does magnification have a unit?

**Answer:** No.

## 2 marks

**9.** Convert 0.035 mm to µm and then nm.

**Answer:**

- 0.035 ×1000 = **35 µm**.
- 35 ×1000 = **35 000 nm**.

**10.** Convert 3.2 cm² to mm², explaining the factor.

**Answer:**

- Each linear dimension is multiplied by 10, so area is multiplied by **100**.
- 3.2 ×100 = **320 mm²**.

**11.** Calculate (6 ×10⁵)/(2 ×10²).

**Answer:**

- Divide coefficients: 6/2 = 3. Subtract powers: 5−2 = 3.
- Answer = **3 ×10³**.

**12.** Round 0.03476 to two significant figures and explain where counting starts.

**Answer:**

- Count from the first non-zero digit, 3.
- Rounded result = **0.035**.

**13.** Simplify 15:25 and express the first group as a fraction of the total.

**Answer:**

- Ratio = **3:5**.
- Fraction = 3/(3+5) = **3/8**.

**14.** Calculate the percentage represented by 27 successful outcomes out of 90.

**Answer:**

- Percentage = 27/90 ×100.
- Result = **30%**.

**15.** A microscope has ×10 eyepiece and ×40 objective. Determine its total power.

**Answer:**

- Multiply eyepiece by objective: 10 ×40.
- Total = **×400**.

**16.** An image is 30 mm long at ×500 magnification. Calculate actual size in µm.

**Answer:**

- Actual = 30/500 = **0.060 mm**.
- Convert: 0.060 ×1000 = **60 µm**.

**17.** A pondweed produces 18 cm³ oxygen in 6 minutes. Calculate rate per minute.

**Answer:**

- Rate = volume/time = 18/6.
- Rate = **3 cm³ min⁻¹**.

**18.** A reaction reaches a fixed endpoint in 40 s. Calculate reciprocal-time rate.

**Answer:**

- Rate proxy = 1/40.
- **0.025 s⁻¹**, for a comparable endpoint.

**19.** A distance doubles in an inverse-square light model. What happens to intensity?

**Answer:**

- Intensity ∝ 1/d².
- New intensity = **1/4** of the original.

**20.** Explain why a positive correlation does not prove causation.

**Answer:**

- Values tend to rise together in the data.
- Another variable may affect both, so a controlled comparison is needed to isolate a cause.

**21.** Distinguish interpolation from extrapolation.

**Answer:**

- Interpolation estimates **within** the measured range.
- Extrapolation extends **outside** it, where the relationship may change.

**22.** A bacterium is 2 µm long and a cell is 20 µm long. Compare their linear scale.

**Answer:**

- 20/2 = **10 times**.
- The cell is about **one order of magnitude** larger in length.

## 3 marks

**23.** Calculate percentage mass change from 2.50 g to 2.10 g.

**Answer:**

- Change = 2.10−2.50 = **−0.40 g**.
- Percentage = −0.40/2.50 ×100.
- **−16%**, a 16% decrease.

**24.** A success rate rises from 20% to 30%. Calculate percentage-point change and relative percentage increase.

**Answer:**

- Difference = **10 percentage points**.
- Relative increase = (30−20)/20 ×100.
- Relative increase = **50%**.

**25.** A 4 µm scale bar measures 8 mm on an image. A cell measures 30 mm. Calculate cell size.

**Answer:**

- Cell/bar image-length ratio = 30/8 = 3.75.
- Actual cell size = 3.75 ×4.
- **15 µm**.

**26.** Calculate biomass-transfer efficiency if 45 kg is gained from 600 kg available.

**Answer:**

- Efficiency = next/previous ×100.
- 45/600 ×100.
- **7.5%**, using matching mass units/basis.

**27.** For counts 1, 2, 2, 7, calculate mean, median and mode.

**Answer:**

- Mean = 12/4 = **3**.
- Median = (2+2)/2 = **2**.
- Mode = **2**.

**28.** Counts 0, 1 and 2 occur with frequencies 3, 4 and 3. Calculate the mean.

**Answer:**

- Total count = 0×3 +1×4 +2×3 = **10**.
- Total frequency = 3+4+3 = **10**.
- Mean = 10/10 = **1**.

**29.** A carrier cross predicts a 1/4 affected probability. Calculate expected affected count in 120 births and explain its limitation.

**Answer:**

- Expected count = 120 ×1/4.
- **30** affected births expected.
- Random variation means an actual sample need not have exactly 30.

**30.** A line passes through (2, 6) and (8, 18). Find gradient and intercept.

**Answer:**

- Gradient = (18−6)/(8−2) = **2**.
- Use y = 2x+c: 6 = 4+c, so **c = 2**.
- Equation: **y = 2x+2**.

**31.** A tangent to a pH-time curve passes through (1 day, pH 7) and (5 days, pH 5). Calculate slope and describe the change.

**Answer:**

- Slope = (5−7)/(5−1).
- **−0.5 per day**.
- At the tangent point, pH decreases at magnitude **0.5 per day**.

**32.** A 12 mm circular inhibition zone includes its disc. Calculate area to three significant figures.

**Answer:**

- Radius = **6 mm**.
- Area = π ×6² = 113.097…
- **113 mm²**.

## 4 marks

**33.** A 2 cm cube exchanges substances through its surface. Calculate surface area, volume and SA:V ratio.

**Answer:**

- Surface area = 6 ×2² = **24 cm²**.
- Volume = 2³ = **8 cm³**.
- SA:V = 24:8 = **3:1**.
- It has less surface per unit volume than a 1 cm cube, making diffusion less effective for meeting internal demand.

**34.** Ten 0.25 m² quadrats contain a total of 30 plants. Estimate population in a 150 m² habitat and state a limitation.

**Answer:**

- Mean = 30/10 = **3 per quadrat**.
- Density = 3/0.25 = **12 plants m⁻²**.
- Estimated population = 12 ×150 = **1800**.
- Patchy distribution or biased/limited sampling may make the estimate unrepresentative.

**35.** Explain how to calculate the rate at one instant on a curved biological graph.

**Answer:**

- Draw a **tangent at the specified point**.
- Use two well-separated points on that tangent to form a large triangle.
- Calculate change in y divided by change in x using actual axis scales.
- State units/sign meaning; a chord across the curve gives an average rather than instantaneous rate.

**36.** A 10 mm inhibition zone contains a 4 mm disc. Calculate clear-agar area excluding the disc.

**Answer:**

- Zone radius = 5 mm; total area = **25π mm²**.
- Disc radius = 2 mm; disc area = **4π mm²**.
- Clear area = (25−4)π = **21π mm²**.
- ≈ **66.0 mm²**, to three significant figures.

**37.** Two independent births each have affected probability 1/4. Find probabilities of both affected and exactly one affected.

**Answer:**

- Both affected = 1/4 ×1/4 = **1/16**.
- First affected, second unaffected = 1/4 ×3/4 = **3/16**.
- First unaffected, second affected also **3/16**.
- Exactly one = 3/16+3/16 = **6/16 = 3/8**.

**38.** A rate rises linearly from 0 to 6 cm³ min⁻¹ over 4 minutes. Calculate total gas produced from the rate-time graph.

**Answer:**

- Total change is the **area under the rate–time graph**.
- Region is a triangle: ½ ×base ×height.
- ½ ×4 ×6 = **12**.
- Units min ×cm³ min⁻¹ give **12 cm³**.
