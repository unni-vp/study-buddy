# Magnification, rates and inheritance

## Image sizes

M = image/actual. Actual = image/M.

Use same unit for both sizes.

36 mm at ×600: 60 µm actual.

Magnification has no unit.

## Scale bars

Cell/bar image ratio ×bar actual length.

36 mm cell, 10 mm bar labelled 5 µm.

Actual cell = 18 µm.

Resizing preserves ratios when bar resizes too.

## Rate calculations

Change/time; state time basis.

12 cm³ in 4 min: 3 cm³ min⁻¹.

Same-endpoint rate proxy: 1/time.

25 s gives 0.040 s⁻¹.

## Transfer and light

Efficiency = next/previous biomass ×100.

40/500 ×100 = 8%.

Intensity ∝ 1/d². Triple distance: 1/9 intensity.

Photosynthesis can have other limiting factors.

## Inheritance probabilities

Aa ×Aa: AA, Aa, Aa, aa.

aa: 1/4 affected. Aa: 1/2 carrier.

Expected number = probability ×trials.

Actual counts vary randomly.

## Independent events

Multiply independent probabilities.

Both affected: 1/4 ×1/4 = 1/16.

Exactly one: add both possible orders.

Each pregnancy is a new event.
