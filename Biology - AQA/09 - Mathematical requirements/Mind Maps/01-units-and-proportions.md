# Units, standard form and proportions

## Unit ladder

1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm.

1 mm = 1000 µm. 1 µm = 1000 nm.

Smaller unit: larger numerical value.

kg, g, mg follow ×1000 steps.

## Area and volume

1 cm² = 100 mm².

1 cm³ = 1000 mm³.

1000 cm³ = 1 dm³.

Convert powers, not just one length.

## Standard form

a ×10ⁿ with 1 ≤a <10.

0.0000062 = 6.2 ×10⁻⁶.

Multiply: add powers. Divide: subtract powers.

Negative exponent is a fraction, not negative length.

## Rounding and estimates

0.00450 has three significant figures.

Round final result, not intermediate steps.

Estimate expected scale to catch errors.

Tenfold difference: one order of magnitude.

## Fractions and ratios

Part/whole ×100 = percentage.

1:3 gives first fraction 1/4.

Match units before simplifying ratios.

Probability lies between 0 and 1.

## Percentage change

(New − original)/original ×100.

Keep negative sign for a decrease.

20% to 30%: +10 percentage points.

Relative increase: 50%.
