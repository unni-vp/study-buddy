# Geometry and exchange surfaces

## Area formulas

Rectangle: length ×width.

Triangle: ½base ×perpendicular height.

Areas use squared units.

Use consistent lengths.

## Cube formulas

Surface area = 6a².

Volume = a³.

SA:V = surface/volume.

Double side: fourfold surface, eightfold volume.

## Same-scale comparison

Larger total surface, smaller surface per volume.

## Diffusion consequence

Lower SA:V gives less exchange surface per internal volume.

Longer distances also slow diffusion.

Large organisms need specialised exchange/transport.

Total surface area is not the ratio.

## Circles and zones

Radius = diameter/2.

Area = πr².

12 mm diameter: about 113 mm².

Subtract disc area only if requested.

Use squared length units.
