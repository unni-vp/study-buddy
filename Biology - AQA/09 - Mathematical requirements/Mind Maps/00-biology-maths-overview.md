# Biology maths

## Units and size

Convert before substituting.

mm, µm and nm differ by factors of 1000.

Square/cube conversion factors for area/volume.

Standard form: 1 ≤ coefficient <10.

## Fractions and changes

Part/whole ×100 gives percentage.

(New − original)/original ×100 gives change.

Ratios need both groups in the total.

Check percentage points versus relative change.

## Magnification and rates

Magnification = image/actual size.

Rate = change/time.

Same-endpoint proxy = 1/time.

Efficiency = next/previous biomass ×100.

Light intensity ∝ 1/distance² in suitable model.

## Probability and data

Expected count = probability × trials.

Mean, median, mode and range describe data.

Random representative samples reduce bias.

Correlation alone does not prove causation.

## Graphs and algebra

y = mx+c: gradient and intercept.

Large triangle on best-fit line/tangent.

Rearrange with matching operations.

Use units and sensible significant figures.

## Geometry

Rectangle: length ×width. Triangle: ½bh.

Cube: surface 6a², volume a³.

SA:V falls as size rises.

Circle area πr², using half-diameter.
