# Data, graphs and algebra

## Averages and spread

Mean: total/count. Median: ordered middle.

Mode: most frequent. Range: max−min.

Frequency mean: Σ(value ×frequency)/Σfrequency.

Investigate anomalies before exclusion.

## Sampling estimate

Mean per quadrat ×habitat/quadrat area.

Mean 6 in 0.5 m², habitat 200 m²: 2400.

Representative random samples for overall abundance.

Transects for distribution gradients.

## Choose a graph

Bars: categories. Histogram: numerical intervals.

Scatter: paired numerical variables.

Line/curve: suitable ordered relationship.

Label quantity, unit and sensible scale.

## Interpret relationships

Positive, negative or no correlation.

Use data values and consider other influences.

Interpolate within measured range.

Extrapolation beyond range is less secure.

## Gradient and intercept

y = mx+c.

m = Δy/Δx

c at x=0.

Large triangle on best-fit line.

Tangent at specified point gives instantaneous rate.

A chord gives an interval average.

## Algebra and units

Rearrange using same operation on both sides.

M = I/A gives A = I/M.

Rate–time area gives total change where appropriate.

Check units, sign and final precision.
