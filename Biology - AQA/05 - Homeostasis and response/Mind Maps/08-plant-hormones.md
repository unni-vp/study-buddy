# Plant hormones and responses

## Growth responses

**Phototropism:** growth response to light. Shoots positive.

**Gravitropism:** response to gravity. Roots positive, shoots negative.

Auxin distribution changes growth rates.

Plants bend through unequal growth, not locomotion.

## Shoot towards light

More auxin on shaded side promotes greater cell elongation.

Unequal elongation curves the shoot towards light.

## Gravity and auxin

Horizontal shoot: more lower-side auxin stimulates elongation, bending upwards.

Root: more lower-side auxin inhibits elongation.

Upper root side grows more, bending down.

Do not apply the shoot response unchanged to roots.

## Seedling practical

Same species, age, starting size, water, temperature and medium.

Light: one-sided versus appropriate all-round control. Gravity: horizontal seedlings in darkness and suitable control.

Measure lengths and make labelled drawings of growth direction.

Use several seedlings and repeat.

## Auxin applications

**Rooting powders:** help cuttings establish roots.

**Selective weedkillers:** control unwanted plants, with biodiversity effects.

**Tissue culture:** promotes growth.

Link hormone effect to the practical benefit.

## Gibberellins and ethene

**Gibberellins:** initiate germination, end dormancy, promote flowering and larger fruit.

**Ethene:** regulates cell division and fruit ripening.

Control ripening during storage and transport.

Do not confuse a hormone’s role with every plant response.
