# Homeostasis overview

## Nervous control

**Homeostasis:** regulate internal conditions for optimum function.

Receptors detect change. Centres coordinate. Effectors respond.

Reflexes are rapid and automatic. CNS is brain and spinal cord.

## Eyes and temperature

Near: ciliary muscles contract, ligaments loosen, lens thickens.

Iris changes pupil size for light intensity.

Hot: vasodilation and sweating. Cold: vasoconstriction and shivering.

## Hormones and glucose

Hormones travel in blood to target cells.

**Insulin:** lowers glucose through uptake and glycogen storage.

**Glucagon:** raises glucose through liver glycogen breakdown.

Feedback opposes the original change.

## Kidneys and water

Liver makes urea. Kidneys filter and selectively reabsorb.

**More ADH:** more water reabsorbed, smaller concentrated urine volume.

Dialysis removes waste

transplant has benefits and rejection risks.

## Reproduction

FSH matures egg. Oestrogen rebuilds lining. LH triggers ovulation.

Progesterone maintains lining and inhibits FSH/LH.

Contraception alters hormones or prevents sperm meeting an egg. IVF fertilises eggs outside body.

## Plant responses

Unequal auxin changes elongation and bends roots/shoots.

Shoots grow towards light and away from gravity. Roots towards gravity.

Auxin, gibberellin and ethene have agricultural uses.
