# Eyes and vision

## Light path and signals

Cornea refracts; lens adjusts focus.

Retina detects intensity and colour. Optic nerve carries impulses.

Sclera protects. Iris changes pupil size.

Ciliary muscles control tension in suspensory ligaments.

## Near focus

**Ciliary muscles:** contract.

**Ligaments:** loosen.

**Lens:** thicker, more curved.

Light refracted more strongly, focusing on retina.

## Distant focus

**Ciliary muscles:** relax.

**Ligaments:** pulled tight.

**Lens:** thinner, less curved.

Light refracted less strongly.

## Pupil response

Bright: circular muscles contract, radial relax, pupil smaller.

Dim: radial contract, circular relax, pupil larger.

Pupil size controls light quantity. Lens shape controls focusing.

Pupil is an opening, not a muscle.

## Focusing defects

**Myopia:** distant focus in front of retina. Concave lens diverges incoming light.

**Hyperopia:** near focus behind retina. Convex lens increases convergence.

Read the focus position, not just a picture’s lens thickness.

## Correction choices

Glasses and hard/soft contacts refract light.

Laser surgery reshapes cornea. Replacement lenses change focusing.

Compare effectiveness, maintenance, cost, reversibility and risks.

No one option suits every defect.
