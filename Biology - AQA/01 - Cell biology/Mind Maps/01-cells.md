# Cells

## Animal cells

- **Eukaryotic:** DNA is enclosed in a nucleus.
- **Nucleus** → genetic material; controls activities through genes.
- **Cytoplasm** → many enzyme-controlled reactions.
- **Membrane** → controls entry and exit.
- **Mitochondria** → aerobic respiration transfers energy.
- **Ribosomes** → protein synthesis.

## Plant cells

- Also have a membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus, mitochondria and ribosomes.
- **Cellulose wall** → strength and support. Algal cells also have cellulose walls.
- **Permanent vacuole** → cell sap; water helps keep the cell firm.
- **Chloroplasts** → photosynthesis; chlorophyll absorbs light.
- Chloroplasts occur in **photosynthetic cells**; root hair cells normally lack them.

## Bacterial cells

- **Prokaryotic** and much smaller than plant or animal cells.
- **No nucleus:** one main DNA loop lies in the cytoplasm.
- Some have **plasmids**: small DNA rings carrying extra genes.
- Have a membrane, cytoplasm, ribosomes and a wall; the wall is **not cellulose**.
- No mitochondria or chloroplasts.

## Sperm cells

- **Tail** → swimming towards the egg.
- Many **mitochondria** → respiration supplies energy for movement.
- **Acrosome enzymes** → help penetrate the egg's outer layers.
- Link each structure to its effect and the cell's job.

## Nerve & muscle cells

- **Nerve cell:** a long fibre carries electrical impulses over long distances.
- **Branched ends** connect with other cells.
- **Muscle cell:** contractile proteins allow shortening.
- Many **mitochondria** provide energy for contraction.

## Root hair cells

- Long extension → **large surface area** for absorption.
- Water enters by **osmosis**.
- Mineral ions enter by **active transport** when their concentration is lower in the soil.
- Mitochondria supply energy from respiration for active transport.
- Underground → normally **no chloroplasts**.

## Xylem & phloem

- **Xylem:** dead, hollow cells join into tubes → carry water and mineral ions.
- **Lignin** strengthens walls → support and resistance to collapse.
- **Phloem:** sieve tubes move dissolved sugars.
- Companion cells supply energy for loading sugars.
- Use **feature → effect → function**, including for unfamiliar cells.

## Differentiation

- A cell develops specialised structures to carry out a particular function.
- Most animal cells differentiate **early in development**.
- Many plant cells can differentiate **throughout life**.
- In mature animals, cell division mainly **repairs and replaces** cells.
- Remember: differentiation is the process; a specialised cell is the result.
