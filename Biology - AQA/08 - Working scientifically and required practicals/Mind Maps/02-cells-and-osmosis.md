# Microscopy, cultures and osmosis practicals

## Microscope technique

Thin tissue, suitable stain, coverslip at angle.

Low power first, coarse then fine focus.

Increase power carefully.

Draw observed structures with labels and scale.

## Magnification

Microscope power = eyepiece × objective.

Image magnification = image/actual size.

Convert both sizes to the same unit.

Resized drawings need their own scale.

## Agar comparison

Sterile agar, tools and harmless school culture.

Same disc size, agent amount and starting bacteria.

Space discs; include solvent control.

Repeat inhibition-zone measurements.

## Agar safety and interpretation

Tape at a few points, not airtight.

Incubate inverted at about 25°C.

Keep closed after growth; approved disposal.

Zone size depends on inhibition and diffusion.

## Potato method

Equal-dimension pieces, initial masses.

Known solution range, same volume/time/temperature.

Blot consistently then measure final masses.

Repeat and calculate percentage changes.

## Osmosis graph

(Final − initial)/initial ×100.

Dilute solution: net water gain.

Concentrated solution: net loss.

Zero-change point: no net movement, not no molecular movement.
