# Biology investigations and practicals

## Fair investigations

Hypothesis: testable prediction and reason.

Change independent variable. Measure dependent.

Keep relevant controls consistent.

Control experiment checks an alternative cause.

## Evidence quality

Accuracy: close to true value. Precision: clustered.

Repeats reduce random scatter.

Calibrate systematic bias.

Evaluate uncertainty and justified anomalies.

## Cells and transport

Microscopy: thin/stained slide, scale, labels.

Agar: aseptic technique and inhibition zones.

Osmosis: percentage tissue-mass change.

Use defined measurements and comparable samples.

## Food and reactions

Food reagents identify biological molecules.

Amylase: pH, iodine every 30 s.

Pondweed: light and oxygen rate.

Control temperature and compare common endpoints.

## Responses and habitats

Ruler catch distance converted to time.

Seedlings: lengths and growth drawings.

Random quadrats/transects measure populations.

Milk lipase: temperature and falling pH.

## Interpret and communicate

Label graphs and show units.

Correlation is not proof of causation.

Peer review and replication strengthen evidence.

Link specific improvements to actual limitations.
