# Milk decay and practical evaluation

## Milk mechanism

Lipase produces fatty acids from fat.

Falling pH gives a timed endpoint.

Milk/indicator/carbonate give consistent start.

Models an enzyme component of decay.

## Temperature method

Equilibrate enzyme and mixture in water bath.

Add equal enzyme and start timing.

Compare same endpoint at several temperatures.

Repeat and use a no-enzyme control.

## Controls

Same milk, volumes/concentrations.

Same starting pH, indicator and mixing.

Same endpoint judgement.

A probe needs calibration and common pH interval.

## Rates and results

Relative rate = 1/time, units s⁻¹.

Half the endpoint time: double rate.

Warmer increases activity up to optimum.

Very high temperature can denature enzyme.

## Specific improvements

Shorter sampling reduces endpoint uncertainty.

Gas volume avoids variable bubble sizes.

Calibration corrects a systematic offset.

More representative samples reduce sampling bias.

## Safe, evidence-led decisions

Eye protection and safe hot-water handling.

Never taste experimental mixtures.

Use data, spread and limits in conclusions.

Avoid vague “better equipment” or unsupported causation.
