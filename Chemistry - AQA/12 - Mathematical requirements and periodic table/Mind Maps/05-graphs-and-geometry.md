# Data, algebra, graphs and geometry

## Means and frequency

Mean=total/count.

Frequency mean=Σ(value×frequency)/Σfrequency.

Grouped midpoints give estimated mean.

Average suitable concordant titres, not rough overshoots.

## Present data

Bars for categories, histogram for numerical intervals.

Scatter/line/curve for appropriate paired data.

Axes: quantity, unit, suitable scale.

Best fit, not forced point-to-point joining.

## Gradient and rate

y=mx+c. m=Δy/Δx. c at x=0.

Large triangle on best fit.

Tangent at specified curve point: instantaneous rate.

Chord: interval mean, not one instant.

## Algebra and graph meaning

Same operation both sides when rearranging.

c=n/V gives V=n/c.

Correlation alone not proof of causation.

Rate-time area gives total change where appropriate.

## Area and volume

Rectangle lw. Triangle: ½bh.

Cube surface: 6a². Volume: a³.

2 cm cube: 24 cm² and8 cm³.

Eight 1 cm cubes: 48 cm², same 8 cm³.

## Represent and check

Real molecules are 3D; drawings/model keys simplify.

Keep atoms, valency and connectivity.

Projected crossing lines need not be bonds.

Convert, calculate, round, attach unit and check scale.
