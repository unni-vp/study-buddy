# Chemistry maths and periodic-table skills

## Numbers and units

Convert before substituting.

1000 cm³ =1 dm³

1000 mg =1 g.

Standard form and order-of-magnitude checks.

Round final result to suitable significant figures.

## Periodic-table information

Atomic number counts protons.

Ar is a weighted isotope mean.

Mr sums all atoms, including brackets.

Ion charge balances compound formulas.

## Moles and equations

n =m/Mr.

Particle count =n×Avogadro constant.

Balanced coefficients give mole ratios.

Compare moles/coefficient for limiting reactant.

Empirical formula from simplest mole ratio.

## Solutions and gases

c=n/V with volume in dm³.

Mass concentration =c×Mr.

Room conditions:24 dm³ gas per mole.

Titration uses equation ratio, not always 1:1.

## Percentages and energy

Yield =actual/theoretical ×100.

Atom economy includes balanced mass contributions.

Bond energy =broken−formed.

Rate =change/time.

Rf =spot/front distance.

## Graphs and geometry

Best-fit slope/tangent gives rate.

y=mx+c, gradient and intercept.

Cube surface 6a², volume a³.

Preserve molecular connectivity in representations.
