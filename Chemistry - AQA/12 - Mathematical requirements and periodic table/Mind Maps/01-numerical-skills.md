# Units, proportions and precision

## Unit conversions

kg/g/mg: factors of 1000.

dm³/cm³: factors of 1000.

kJ/J: factors of 1000.

Minutes to seconds: ×60.

## Small scales

mm/µm/nm: factors of 1000.

Prefixes kilo, milli, micro, nano and larger powers.

Area: square factor. Volume: cube factor.

1 cm² =100 mm²

1 cm³ =1000 mm³.

## Standard form

a×10ⁿ, 1≤a<10.

Multiply coefficients and add powers.

Divide coefficients and subtract powers.

Negative exponent is small fraction, not negative amount.

## Ratios and percentages

Part/whole ×100.

2:3 gives first fraction 2/5.

(New−original)/original ×100.

Percentage-point difference is not relative change.

## Precision

0.00340 has three significant figures.

Round only at the end.

Use requested precision and actual supplied values.

Decimal places differ from significant figures.

## Estimate and check

0.048/24 is about 0.002.

Tenfold scale change: one order of magnitude.

Check units and plausible magnitude.

Small percentages may act on very different totals.
