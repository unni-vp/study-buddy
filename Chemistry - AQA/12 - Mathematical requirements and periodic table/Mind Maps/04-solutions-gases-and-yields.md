# Solutions, gases, yield and energy

## Concentrations

Mass/volume: g dm⁻³.

c=n/V

n=cV.

Convert cm³ first.

Mass concentration=c×Mr.

Titration: known moles, equation ratio, unknown volume.

## Gas quantities

24 dm³ =24 000 cm³ per mole at room conditions.

n=V/24 or V/24 000 with matching units.

Volume ratios equal mole ratios at same T/P.

Not valid unchanged for every condition.

## Yield versus atom economy

Yield:actual/theoretical ×100.

Atom economy:desired/total balanced product mass ×100.

CaCO₃ →CaO+CO₂:56% for CaO.

Recovery losses differ from reaction by-product formation.

## Bond energy

Breaking absorbs, forming releases.

Change=broken−formed.

Count all bonds and coefficients.

Negative exothermic, positive endothermic.

## Rates and other ratios

Mean rate=quantity/time.

Fixed endpoint proxy=1/time.

Rf=baseline-to-spot/front distance, no unit.

Gold %=carats/24 ×100.

## Temperature and interpretation

ΔT=final−initial.

Use supplied Q=mcΔT where applicable.

Matching mass/heat-capacity units.

Check direction of energy transfer and conditions.
