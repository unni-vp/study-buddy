# Chromatography, ion tests and water

## Chromatography sequence

Pencil baseline, small dry spots.

Solvent below starting samples.

Remove then promptly mark front.

Same solvent/paper for references.

## Rf and limits

Spot-centre/front distance from baseline.

Same units, no unit on Rf.

One spot may hide overlapping compounds.

Different solvent can resolve overlap.

## Cations

Clean loop: Li crimson, Na yellow, K lilac.

Ca orange-red, Cu green flame.

NaOH: Al/Ca/Mg white

Al dissolves in excess.

Cu blue, Fe²⁺ green, Fe³⁺ brown precipitates.

## Anions

Carbonate +acid: CO₂, limewater milky.

Nitric acid/AgNO₃: Cl white, Br cream, I yellow.

HCl/BaCl₂: sulfate white.

Fresh portions prevent cross-contamination.

## Water measurements

pH indicator or calibrated probe.

Known volume, weighed basin, evaporate/cool.

Residue mass =final basin−empty mass.

Distil water and compare samples.

## Safety and conclusions

Open receiver

never distil dry.

Cooling jacket water separate from vapour.

No tasting

normal pH alone not proof of potable water.

Small masses/contamination limit accuracy.
