# Chemistry investigations and practicals

## Fair tests and evidence

Change one variable, measure a named quantity.

Control other influential factors and repeat.

Use data, uncertainty and justified conclusions.

Peer review and replication strengthen evidence.

## Salt preparation

Warm acid, add excess insoluble solid.

Filter, concentrate filtrate, cool.

Separate, wash and dry crystals.

Use safe heating and chemical handling.

## Titration and electrolysis

Pipette known volume, burette measured titre.

Concordant titres and mole-ratio calculation.

Aqueous electrolysis: inert electrodes, DC.

Identify cathode/anode products using valid tests.

## Energy and rates

Insulated cup, initial and final temperatures.

Gas-volume method: measured quantity/time.

Colour/turbidity method: common endpoint, 1/time.

Both concentration-rate approaches required.

## Separation and tests

Chromatography: pencil line, solvent below spots.

Rf uses baseline distances to centre/front.

Ion tests: fresh samples and correct acid/reagent.

Record observation before identification.

## Water and evaluation

pH, residue mass and distillation.

Open receiver, separate cooling-water jacket.

Match improvement to the actual error.

No taste tests or drinkability inference from clarity.
