# Electrolysis and temperature investigations

## Aqueous cell

Separate inert electrodes in solution.

Low-voltage DC supply.

Cathode negative, anode positive.

Ions in liquid

electrons in wires.

## Predict products

Less-reactive-than-H metal deposited at cathode

otherwise H₂.

Halide may form halogen at anode

otherwise O₂.

Use supplied concentration/context.

CuSO₄/inert: cathode Cu, anode O₂.

## Identify and control

H₂ burning splint pop

O₂ glowing splint relights.

Cl₂ damp litmus bleaches white.

Compare voltage, concentration, time and immersion.

Small quantities, ventilation, no inhalation/short circuit.

## Temperature setup

Insulated supported cup with suitable lid.

Measured reagents at controlled starting temperature.

Mix and record regular temperatures.

Change one chosen variable, repeat.

## Interpret changes

ΔT =final−initial.

Rise usually exothermic, fall endothermic.

Equal ΔT is not equal energy for different masses.

Use supplied Q=mcΔT where appropriate.

## Improve specifically

Insulation reduces heat exchange.

Consistent stirring and response timing.

Consider limiting reagent and incomplete reaction.

Eye protection and safe gas-producing setup.
