# Concentration-rate investigations

## Gas-volume method

Known concentrations, fixed total volume.

Same solid mass/surface area and temperature.

Seal promptly, measure gas at fixed times.

Repeat and compare mean curves/rates.

## Gas interpretation

Quantity/time gives mean rate.

Tangent gives instantaneous rate.

Leaks/start-up loss underestimate volume.

Final amount depends on limiting reagent.

## Turbidity method

Thiosulfate +acid forms cloudy sulfur.

Time a fixed cross disappearing.

Dilute thiosulfate but keep total volume/acid fixed.

Rate proxy 1/time for same endpoint.

## Endpoint validity

Same flask/depth/cross/lighting/observer.

Same temperature and mixing.

Fixed light sensor threshold improves judgement.

Do not treat proxy as molar rate without amount data.

## Collision explanation

Higher concentration: more particles per unit volume.

More frequent successful collisions.

Consumed reactants reduce frequency over time.

Temperature changes energy too; keep it controlled.

## Safe and repeatable

Eye protection and dilute/small amounts.

Ventilate sulfur-dioxide-producing reaction.

Gas syringe must move freely.

Use specific checks, not vague “be careful”.
