# Gas tests and metal-ion identification

## Gas splints

Hydrogen: burning splint at tube mouth, pop.

Oxygen: glowing splint inserted, relights.

Use the specified splint condition.

Do not deliberately smell gases.

## Other gases

Carbon dioxide +limewater: milky.

Limewater is calcium hydroxide solution.

Chlorine: damp litmus bleached white.

Extinguished flame alone is not a specific CO₂ test.

## Flame colours

Lithium crimson. Sodium yellow.

Potassium lilac. Calcium orange-red.

Copper green.

Clean loop in blue non-luminous flame.

## White hydroxides

Aluminium, calcium and magnesium: white.

Only aluminium dissolves in excess NaOH here.

Calcium flame can support differentiation.

Colourless liquid is not a white solid.

## Coloured hydroxides

Cu²⁺ blue, Fe²⁺ green, Fe³⁺ brown.

Observe initial result.

Cu²⁺+2OH⁻ →Cu(OH)₂(s).

Balance charge and atoms in equations.

## Reliable testing

Fresh portions, clean equipment and references.

Mixture flame colours may be masked.

Wear eye protection and avoid hot-wire burns.

Match observations to the exact reagent.
