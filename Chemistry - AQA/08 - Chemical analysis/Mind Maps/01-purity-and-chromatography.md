# Purity, formulations and chromatography

## Substances and mixtures

One element/compound is chemically pure.

Impure solids often melt over a lower range.

Compare with known reference conditions.

Formulations have purposeful ingredient proportions.

## Separation mechanism

Stationary phase stays. Solvent moves.

More attraction to paper: shorter travel.

More time in mobile phase: further travel.

Solvent/paper changes can alter separation.

## Practical sequence

Pencil baseline, small dried spots.

Solvent below baseline, suitable covered vessel.

Remove before front reaches paper top.

Mark front immediately.

## Spot comparison

All distances measured from baseline.

## Rf calculation

Spot-centre distance/front distance.

3.6/6.0 = 0.60, no unit.

Usually 0 to 1. Same conditions for comparison.

Match reference patterns, not colour alone.

## Limits and safety

One spot may hide overlapping compounds.

Try a different solvent if necessary.

Small spots improve centre measurement.

Keep flammable solvents away from flames.
