# Corrosion, protection and alloys

## Rust conditions

Iron needs oxygen and water.

Compare wet air, dry air, boiled water under oil.

Same clean nails, time and temperature.

Excluded factor must remain excluded.

## Barrier protection

Paint, grease and electroplating block access.

Scratches can expose underlying metal.

Aluminium oxide forms protective layer.

Less-reactive coatings do not sacrifice themselves.

## Sacrificial metal

More-reactive metal oxidises preferentially.

Zinc galvanises iron.

Barrier plus sacrificial action protects some scratches.

Enough connected zinc must remain.

## Alloy layers

Different-sized atoms disrupt sliding.

## Named compositions

Bronze: copper/tin. Brass: copper/zinc.

Gold jewellery often alloyed for hardness.

18 carat =18/24 =75% gold.

Steels: iron with carbon/other elements.

## Uses and properties

High-carbon steel: hard/strong, brittle tools.

Low-carbon: easier shaping, car panels.

Stainless: corrosion-resistant cutlery.

Aluminium alloy: low-density aircraft structures.
