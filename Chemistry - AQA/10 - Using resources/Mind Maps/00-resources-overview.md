# Using resources

## Sustainable use

Meet present needs without limiting future needs.

Finite ores/fuels versus managed renewable resources.

Agricultural/synthetic products supplement natural ones.

Read data scale, resource and energy impacts.

## Water

Potable safe, not chemically pure.

Freshwater: filter and sterilise.

Saltwater: distillation/reverse osmosis.

Wastewater: screening, settling, biological treatment.

## Recovery and LCA

Plants/bacteria recover metal compounds.

Copper recovered by displacement/electrolysis.

LCA includes all stages and transport.

Reduce, reuse, recycle with realistic comparisons.

## Materials and protection

Iron rust needs water and oxygen.

Barriers and more-reactive sacrificial metal.

Alloy composition changes properties.

Ceramics/polymers/composites selected by use.

## Haber conditions

N₂+3H₂ ⇌2NH₃, exothermic forward.

Air/natural gas supply nitrogen/hydrogen.

Iron, about 450°C and 200 atm.

Cool/remove ammonia, recycle unused gases.

## Fertiliser supply

NPK supplies compounds of N, P and K.

Ammonia makes salts/nitric acid.

Mined potassium salts and treated phosphate rock.

Industrial integrated manufacture differs from lab scale.
