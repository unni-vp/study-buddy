# Haber process and industrial compromises

## Feedstock

Nitrogen from air. Hydrogen commonly natural gas.

Purify and mix in 1:3 molar ratio.

N₂+3H₂ ⇌2NH₃.

Exothermic forward, reversible.

## Commercial conditions

Iron catalyst.

About 450°C and 200 atmospheres.

Partial conversion per pass.

Cool ammonia to liquid and recycle unused gases.

## Temperature compromise

Lower: higher equilibrium yield but slow rate.

Higher: faster rate but lower ammonia proportion.

Choose useful output per time.

Yield percentage alone does not set best conditions.

## Pressure compromise

Four reactant gas moles become two.

High pressure favours ammonia and increases collision rate.

Compression/strong equipment cost money and energy.

Very high pressure brings safety demands.

## Catalyst role

Different pathway, lower activation energy.

Both directions faster.

Same equilibrium position and yield.

Useful production without extra yield shift.

## Practical operation

Remove condensed product.

Recycle feed gases to improve overall use.

Compare costs, energy, availability and safety.

Interpret supplied graphs and operating constraints.
