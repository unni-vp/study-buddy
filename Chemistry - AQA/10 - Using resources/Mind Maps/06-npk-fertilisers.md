# NPK fertilisers and manufacture

## Nutrient formulation

Compounds supplying nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium.

Appropriate percentages for crop needs.

Fertilisers are mixtures/formulations of salts.

Not nitrogen gas or potassium metal added directly.

## Nitrogen supply

Ammonia used for ammonium salts and nitric acid.

Ammonia +nitric acid gives ammonium nitrate.

Integrated processes link production stages.

Nitrate/ammonium compounds supply usable nitrogen.

## Potassium and phosphate

Potassium chloride/sulfate mined.

Phosphate rock poorly soluble.

Treat rock to make available nutrients.

Untreated rock is not an effective direct fertiliser.

## Acid-treatment products

Nitric: calcium nitrate and phosphoric acid.

Sulfuric: single superphosphate, including calcium sulfate.

Phosphoric: triple superphosphate.

Soluble phosphate differs from starting rock.

## Laboratory preparation

Neutralise suitable acid/ammonia solution.

Titration finds proportions; fresh mix without indicator.

Concentrate, crystallise, separate and dry.

Control purity and safe handling.

## Industrial comparison

Large scale, often continuous and automated.

Feedstock/acid manufacture integrated.

Heat control, recycling and quality checks.

Compare supplied yield, energy, safety and costs.
