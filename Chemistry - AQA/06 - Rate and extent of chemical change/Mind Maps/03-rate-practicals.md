# Concentration and reaction-rate practicals

## Gas method

Fixed chip mass/surface area and acid volume.

Change concentration, collect gas by syringe.

Seal promptly and record fixed-time volumes.

Hypothesis links concentration to collision frequency.

## Control and repeat

Same temperature, apparatus and intervals.

Repeat each condition and compare means.

Leaks/start delay underestimate collected gas.

Check syringe movement and use eye protection.

## Rate versus amount

Initial slope compares speed.

Same limiting carbonate/excess acid: same final gas.

Less acid can change limiting reactant/yield.

Explain which comparison is controlled.

## Turbidity method

Thiosulfate + acid produces cloudy sulfur.

Time until cross hidden.

Dilute thiosulfate, same total volume and acid.

Rate proxy = 1/time for common endpoint.

## Endpoint consistency

Same flask, depth, cross, lighting and observer.

Same temperature and mixing.

Repeat and check unusual readings.

Fixed light-sensor threshold reduces judgement variation.

## Safe interpretation

Small quantities and suitable ventilation.

Avoid inhaling sulfur dioxide.

Half the time: double reciprocal-time rate.

s⁻¹ proxy is not automatically mol s⁻¹.
