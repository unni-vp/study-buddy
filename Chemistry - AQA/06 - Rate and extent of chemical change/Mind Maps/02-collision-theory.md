# Collision theory and catalysts

## Activation energy

Minimum energy for successful reaction.

Particles must collide and reach threshold.

Not every collision reacts.

More successes each second: higher rate.

## Concentration and pressure

More solution particles per unit volume.

Higher gas pressure packs particles closer.

Collision frequency increases.

Same temperature does not increase individual speed.

## Surface area

Smaller solid pieces at same mass.

Greater exposed area.

More particles accessible for collisions.

Not more total substance.

## Temperature

Particles gain kinetic energy and move faster.

Collisions more frequent and energetic.

More exceed activation energy.

Explain both effects.

## Catalytic pathway

Different pathway, lower activation energy.

Catalyst regenerated, not used up overall.

Suitable catalyst needed for reaction.

Enzymes act as biological catalysts.

## Profiles and yield

Lower peak but same reactant/product levels.

Overall energy change unchanged.

Both reversible directions faster.

Equilibrium sooner, same position/yield.
