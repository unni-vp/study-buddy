# Rates and equilibrium

## Measure rate

Reactant used or product formed/time.

g s⁻¹, cm³ s⁻¹ or mol s⁻¹.

Gradient shows rate. Final height shows amount.

Tangent gives rate at one instant.

## Successful collisions

Particles collide with sufficient energy.

Concentration/pressure: more frequent collisions.

Surface area: more exposed sites.

Temperature: frequency and energy increase.

## Catalysts

Different pathway, lower activation energy.

Not used up overall.

Overall energy transfer unchanged.

No change to equilibrium yield.

## Two practical methods

Gas volume over measured time.

Colour/turbidity common endpoint: 1/time.

Control other factors, repeat and compare.

Evaluate leaks, timing and subjective endpoints.

## Dynamic equilibrium

Reversible reaction in closed system.

Forward/reverse rates equal and nonzero.

Concentrations constant, not necessarily equal.

Temperature changes favour endo/exothermic direction.

## Condition changes

Adding reactant/removing product favours products.

Higher pressure favours fewer gas moles.

Equal gas totals: no pressure shift.

Balance rate, yield, energy, cost and safety.
