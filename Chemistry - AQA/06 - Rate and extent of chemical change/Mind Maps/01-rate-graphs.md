# Measuring rates and reading curves

## Mean rates

Amount changed/time, with correct unit.

48 cm³/60 s: 0.80 cm³ s⁻¹.

0.015 mol/30 s: 5.0 ×10⁻⁴ mol s⁻¹.

Do not confuse time with rate.

## Two reaction curves

Same starting limiting amount in this model.

## Slopes and plateaus

Steep slope: faster reaction.

Slope decreases as reactants are used.

Horizontal: no further measured change.

Plateau height: final yield.

## Tangent method

Rate at the marked instant.

## Interpret consumed reactant

Remaining-amount curve slopes down.

Consumption rate usually quoted positive.

Use amount used, not total amount remaining.

Rates need a stated time interval or instant.

## Reliable graph reading

Use actual scales and units.

Large triangle on tangent or best-fit line.

Chord: average rate over an interval.

Change rate does not always change final yield.
