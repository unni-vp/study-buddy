# Reversible reactions and dynamic equilibrium

## Reversible and energy

Products reform reactants: ⇌.

Exothermic forward, endothermic reverse.

Equal energy magnitude for matching amounts.

Copper sulfate hydration reverses dehydration.

## Equal ongoing rates

Closed system prevents escape.

## Concentration changes

Add reactant: use some, favour products.

Remove product: replace some, favour products.

Add product/remove reactant: favour reactants.

New equilibrium opposes, not completely cancels change.

## Temperature changes

Heating favours endothermic direction.

Cooling favours exothermic direction.

Cooling exothermic reaction improves yield but slows rate.

Distinguish equilibrium position from speed.

## Gas-pressure changes

Higher pressure: fewer gas moles favoured.

Lower pressure: more gas moles favoured.

Count coefficients of gases, not atoms.

Equal gas totals: no position shift.

## Catalyst and industry

Both directions faster, same equilibrium yield.

N₂+3H₂ ⇌2NH₃: four gas moles become two.

High pressure increases ammonia yield.

Choose practical rate/yield/cost/safety compromise.
