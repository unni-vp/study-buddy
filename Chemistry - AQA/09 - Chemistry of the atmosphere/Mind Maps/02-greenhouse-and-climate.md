# Greenhouse effect and climate evidence

## Essential radiation routes

Some infrared escapes, some is absorbed.

## Greenhouse gases

Water vapour, CO₂ and methane.

Natural effect keeps suitable temperature.

More gases enhance absorption/re-emission.

Not a solid reflecting blanket.

## Human activities

Fossil-fuel burning and deforestation: CO₂.

Cattle and rice production: methane.

Anaerobic landfill decay releases methane.

Link activity to the chemical/biological process.

## Potential effects

Sea-level rise from warming water/land ice.

Rainfall/extreme-weather changes.

Habitat and species-range shifts.

Food production and water availability affected.

## Evaluate evidence

Long records and broad coverage.

Reliable measurements and independent studies.

Peer review checks methods/reasoning.

One local weather event is not a global trend.

## Uncertainty and communication

Models involve interacting complex systems.

Future emissions and local outcomes uncertain.

Use all relevant data, not selected fragments.

Uncertainty does not erase evidence for overall process.
