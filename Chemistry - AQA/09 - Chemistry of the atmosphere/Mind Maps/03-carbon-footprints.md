# Carbon footprints and reducing emissions

## Definition

Total greenhouse-gas emissions.

Full life cycle of product/service/event.

Include raw materials through disposal.

Use supplied CO₂-equivalent basis where relevant.

## Fair comparisons

Same useful service, lifetime and units.

Manufacturing and fuel production matter.

Local exhaust alone is incomplete.

Use quantitative evidence, not “zero impact”.

## Reduce energy emissions

Insulation and efficiency reduce fuel demand.

Lower-carbon electricity and transport.

Consider capital cost and infrastructure.

Energy supply must meet demand reliably.

## Carbon and methane

Protect forests and restore habitats.

Reduce organic waste and capture methane.

Avoid leaks and monitor performance.

Land, food and time needs can limit actions.

## Material choices

Reduce, reuse and recycle.

Less extraction/manufacture can save emissions.

Sorting, transport and processing also use energy.

Compare complete systems.
