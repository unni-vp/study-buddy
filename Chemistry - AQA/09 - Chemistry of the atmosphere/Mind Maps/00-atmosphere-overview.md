# Chemistry of the atmosphere

## Current and early air

Today: mainly nitrogen, then oxygen.

Early model: volcanic carbon dioxide, little oxygen.

Cooling condenses water into oceans.

Nitrogen accumulates over time.

## Changing carbon and oxygen

Photosynthesis removes CO₂ and releases O₂.

Carbonates/limestone store carbon.

Coal from vegetation

oil/gas from marine biomass.

Burning returns stored carbon rapidly.

## Greenhouse mechanism

Short-wave sunlight warms surface.

Surface emits long-wave infrared.

Gases absorb/re-emit some infrared.

Natural effect supports life

increased gases enhance it.

## Activities and climate

Fossil fuels/deforestation: CO₂.

Cattle/rice/landfill: methane.

Sea level, weather, habitats, food/water affected.

Evaluate broad long-term peer-reviewed evidence.

## Full-life-cycle footprint

All greenhouse gases, not just use-stage CO₂.

Materials, manufacture, transport, use, disposal.

Efficiency, low-carbon energy, forests, methane capture.

Compare cost, access and equivalent services.

## Combustion pollutants

CO/soot: incomplete burning.

Sulfur impurities: SO₂. High-temperature air: NOx.

Acid rain/respiratory harm from SO₂ and NOx.

Particulates cause dimming and health problems.
