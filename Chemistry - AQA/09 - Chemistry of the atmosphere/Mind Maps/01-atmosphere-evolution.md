# Atmosphere evolution and carbon stores

## Today’s mixture

About 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen in dry air.

Other gases include argon and CO₂.

Water vapour varies.

Small proportions can still have large effects.

## Early model

Volcanoes release CO₂, water vapour, nitrogen.

Little or no free oxygen.

Possible methane/ammonia.

Comparison with Mars/Venus supports, not proves, model.

## Oceans and sediments

Cooling: water vapour condenses.

CO₂ dissolves in seawater.

Carbonates precipitate into sediments.

Atmospheric CO₂ decreases.

## Photosynthesis

Algae produced oxygen about 2.7 billion years ago.

Later plants increased oxygen further.

6CO₂+6H₂O →C₆H₁₂O₆+6O₂.

Enough oxygen supported animal evolution.

## Long-term stores

Limestone: carbonate sediment and shells.

Coal: buried vegetation.

Oil/gas: buried marine biomass.

Heat/pressure over millions of years

finite fuels.

## Evidence and change

Earth’s early history spans billions of years.

Rocks altered/destroyed, direct measurements absent.

Use multiple surviving observations.

New evidence can revise explanations.
