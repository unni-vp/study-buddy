# Addition and condensation polymers

## Monomers and chains

Monomer: small joining molecule.

Polymer: long repeating chain.

Keep side groups and atom counts.

Functional groups determine joining.

## Addition unit

Ethene double bond opens.

## Other addition units

Poly(propene): –CH₂–CH(CH₃)–.

Single backbone bonds, outward links, brackets, n.

Restore C=C to find monomer.

No small molecule eliminated.

## Condensation

Monomers have two reactive functional groups.

Groups join and eliminate a small molecule.

Usually water by-product.

Some monomer atoms leave the polymer.

## Polyesters

Diol: two –OH groups.

Diacid: two –COOH groups.

Ester links repeatedly connect them.

Two joining sites allow extended chains.

## Avoid drawing errors

Do not keep C=C in addition backbone.

Do not remove methyl or halogen side groups.

Chain bonds pass through both brackets.

One alcohol +one acid forms a small ester.
