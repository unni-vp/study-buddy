# Alkenes and addition reactions

## Series and structure

C=C functional group, unsaturated.

CₙH₂ₙ for acyclic one-double-bond series.

Ethene, propene, butene, pentene.

Every carbon has four bonds total.

## Displayed formulas

Show every atom and bond.

C=C counts as two bonds.

Ethene CH₂=CH₂, propene CH₂=CHCH₃.

Retain the rest of any longer chain.

## Hydrogen addition

Heated nickel catalyst.

Add H to each former double-bond carbon.

C=C changes into C–C.

Ethene becomes ethane.

## Steam addition

Elevated temperature/pressure, acid catalyst.

H and OH add across C=C.

Ethene becomes ethanol.

Products depend on supplied structure/conditions.

## Halogen addition

Cl₂, Br₂ or I₂ adds across double bond.

One halogen on each former C=C carbon.

Ethene +Br₂: CH₂BrCH₂Br.

No missing atoms or unchanged double bond.

## Tests and combustion

Bromine water orange to colourless.

Alkane remains coloured in ordinary test.

Alkenes often burn smokily in air.

Complete combustion still yields CO₂ and water.
