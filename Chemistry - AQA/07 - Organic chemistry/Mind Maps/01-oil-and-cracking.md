# Crude oil, hydrocarbons and cracking

## Alkane family

CₙH₂ₙ₊₂, saturated single C–C bonds.

Methane CH₄, ethane C₂H₆.

Propane C₃H₈, butane C₄H₁₀.

Hydrocarbon contains no oxygen.

## Column process

Heat mixture to vaporise hydrocarbons.

Hot bottom, cooler top.

High-boiling long chains condense lower.

Short chains rise further before condensing.

## Fractions and materials

Fraction: similar boiling-range mixture.

Fuels: petrol, diesel, kerosene, LPG.

Feedstocks: solvents, lubricants, polymers, detergents.

Distillation does not break molecules.

## Size trends

Longer molecules: boiling point increases.

Viscosity increases, flammability decreases.

Stronger intermolecular attractions.

Boiling does not break covalent bonds.

## Combustion

Enough oxygen: carbon dioxide and water.

Balance C, then H, then O.

C₃H₈+5O₂ →3CO₂+4H₂O.

Insufficient oxygen can produce CO and soot.

## Cracking

Vapour over hot catalyst, or high heat with steam.

Large molecules become smaller alkanes/alkenes.

C₁₀H₂₂ →C₈H₁₈+C₂H₄.

Supplies demanded fuels and reactive feedstocks.
