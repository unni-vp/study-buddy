# Alcohols, carboxylic acids and esters

## Alcohol family

–OH functional group.

Methanol, ethanol, propanol, butanol.

Uses: fuels/solvents

ethanol in drinks.

Methanol is toxic, not drinkable.

## Alcohol reactions

Sodium: hydrogen and sodium compound.

Complete combustion: carbon dioxide/water.

Small members dissolve to varying extents.

Primary examples oxidise to carboxylic acids.

## Fermentation

Sugar +yeast, warm and anaerobic.

Ethanol and carbon dioxide form.

Excess heat denatures enzymes/kills yeast.

Dilute product needs separation.

## Compare production

Fermentation: renewable crop input, land/time needs.

Ethene hydration: steam, acid catalyst, high T/P.

Often continuous, finite petrochemical feedstock.

Compare purity, yield, energy, cost and land.

## Carboxylic acids

–COOH functional group.

Methanoic, ethanoic, propanoic, butanoic.

Partial ionisation: weak acid, not necessarily dilute.

Carbonates give salt, water and CO₂.

## Esters

Carboxylic acid +alcohol →ester +water.

Ethanol +ethanoic acid: ethyl ethanoate.

Ester link –C(=O)–O–.

Condensation, often acid-catalysed.
