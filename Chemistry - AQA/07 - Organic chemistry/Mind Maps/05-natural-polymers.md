# Amino acids and natural polymers

## Amino-acid groups

–NH₂ and –COOH in same molecule.

Glycine: H₂NCH₂COOH.

Two different joining groups.

Recognise them in given structures.

## Condensation joining

Amino and carboxyl groups link.

Water eliminated per joining step.

Repeated joining forms polypeptide.

Different amino acids can share a chain.

## Proteins

Monomers: amino acids.

Sequence influences folding and function.

Different sequences yield different proteins.

Not made from nucleotide monomers.

## Starch and cellulose

Both use glucose monomers.

Different arrangements give different structures.

Starch stores energy

cellulose supports plant walls.

Do not infer identical properties from shared monomer.

## DNA

Four types of nucleotide monomer.

Most DNA has two polymer chains.

Chains form double helix.

Encodes genetic instructions.

## Key comparisons

Addition: same atoms, no by-product.

Condensation: joining groups, small by-product.

Polyester has ester links.

Polypeptide joins amino-acid groups.
