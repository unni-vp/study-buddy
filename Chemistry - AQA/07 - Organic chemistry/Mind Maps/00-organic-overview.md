# Organic chemistry

## Oil and hydrocarbon families

Hydrocarbon: carbon and hydrogen only.

Crude oil: finite mixture from ancient biomass.

Alkanes saturated, CₙH₂ₙ₊₂.

First four: methane, ethane, propane, butane.

## Separation and fuels

Fractional distillation: evaporate then condense.

Longer chains: higher boiling point/viscosity, lower flammability.

Complete combustion: carbon dioxide +water.

Cracking makes smaller alkanes and alkenes.

## Alkenes and addition

C=C, unsaturated, CₙH₂ₙ.

Ethene, propene, butene, pentene.

Hydrogen

steam or halogen adds across C=C.

Bromine water: orange to colourless.

## Alcohols and acids

Alcohol –OH

fermentation makes ethanol.

Carboxylic acid –COOH, partial ionisation.

Acid +carbonate: salt, water, carbon dioxide.

Acid +alcohol: ester and water.

## Synthetic polymers

Addition: double bond opens, no by-product.

Repeat unit: brackets, outward bonds and n.

Condensation: two functional groups, small by-product.

Diol +diacid makes polyester.

## Natural polymers

Amino acids condense into polypeptides.

Proteins use amino-acid monomers.

Starch/cellulose use glucose.

DNA: nucleotide chains, double helix.
