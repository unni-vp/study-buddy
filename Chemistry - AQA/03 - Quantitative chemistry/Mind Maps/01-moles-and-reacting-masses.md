# Moles & reacting masses

## Mᵣ & composition

- Ca(OH)₂: 40 + 2 × (16 + 1) = **74**. Brackets multiply every atom inside.
- Oxygen percentage = 32/74 × 100 = **43.2%**. Mᵣ has no unit; molar mass is 74 g/mol.

## Moles & particles

- n = mass / molar mass. 11 g CO₂ / 44 g/mol = **0.25 mol**.
- Particles = n × 6.02 × 10²³. This is 1.505 × 10²³ CO₂ molecules; each has three atoms.
- State whether counting atoms, molecules, ions or electrons.

## Reacting masses

- **2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO.** 6.0 g Mg / 24 = 0.25 mol Mg.
- Mg : MgO = 2 : 2 → 0.25 mol MgO. Mass = 0.25 × 40 = **10 g MgO**, oxygen in excess.
- Coefficients are mole ratios, not mass ratios.

## Find equation coefficients

- Divide each mass by its molar mass; divide all moles by smallest; multiply to whole numbers.
- 2.4 g Mg, 1.6 g O₂, 4.0 g MgO → 0.10 : 0.05 : 0.10 mol → **2 : 1 : 2**.
- Check atoms and total mass on both sides.

## Limiting vs excess

- 0.30 mol Mg + 0.10 mol O₂: oxygen needs only 0.20 mol Mg.
- O₂ is limiting. MgO formed = **0.20 mol**; Mg left = **0.10 mol**.
- Use limiting amount for theoretical yield; extra excess reactant cannot increase it.

## Mass & uncertainty

- An open vessel gains mass if oxygen enters; loses mass if gas escapes. Count all substances to conserve mass.
- Readings 24.2, 24.4, 24.6 → mean 24.4; range 0.4; half-range uncertainty **±0.2**.
- Percentage uncertainty = 0.2/24.4 × 100 = 0.82%.
