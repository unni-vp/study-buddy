How the sample informs this practice

The 2023 paper includes a school-magazine article and a letter giving views; the 2024 paper includes a class talk and a school-magazine advice article. Articles occur in both papers in this two-paper sample, with different purposes. This does not predict a future form or topic. The practice develops transferable writing decisions rather than reproducing those tasks. Review practice broadens coverage of forms named in the specification.

Each full task is assessed through communication and organisation (12 marks) and vocabulary, sentences, spelling and punctuation (8 marks). The commentary explains choices; it is not a point-by-point official mark scheme.
