Maps & GIS

Map types & GIS

Map types

MapBest use
ChoroplethShaded areas show rates or values; use comparable data such as percentages.
IsolineLines join equal values, such as temperature or pressure.
Flow-lineLine width shows the amount moving between places.
Desire-lineStraight lines connect an origin to destinations.
Sphere of influenceShows the area served by a settlement or service.
Route mapShows a journey or pathway.
Sketch mapSimplifies the most relevant features.

GIS

A Geographical Information System stores, layers and analyses location-based data.

  • Layers might include roads, flood risk, population and land use.
  • Overlaying layers helps reveal patterns and relationships.
  • GIS is powerful, but results depend on the accuracy, date and scale of the source data.
