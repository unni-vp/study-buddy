Number: questions and answers

1 mark2 marks3 marks4 marks5 marks6 marks

1 mark

1. Put these numbers in ascending order: −0.6, −2/3, −0.65.

Answer:

−2/3, −0.65, −0.6. Since −2/3 = −0.666…, it is the smallest.

2. Write the reciprocal of −5/8.

Answer:

−8/5. A number multiplied by its reciprocal equals 1.

3. Find the cube root of −125.

Answer:

−5, because (−5)³ = −125.

4. Write 0.000072 in standard form.

Answer:

7.2 × 10⁻⁵.

5. Round 0.004786 to 2 significant figures.

Answer:

0.0048. The first significant digit is 4, and the next retained digit rounds up.

2 marks

6. Work out −3² + 2(5 − 8).

Answer:
  • The power is evaluated first: −3² = −9.
  • 2(5 − 8) = −6, so the answer is −15. Brackets would be needed for (−3)² = 9.

7. Without a calculator, work out 0.84 ÷ 0.035.

Answer:
  • Multiply both numbers by 1000 to obtain 840 ÷ 35.
  • 840 ÷ 35 = 24.

8. Work out 3/4 − 5/6. Give a fraction in its simplest form.

Answer:
  • Use denominator 12: 9/12 − 10/12.
  • The answer is −1/12.

9. Work out 2 1/4 ÷ 3/5.

Answer:
  • 2 1/4 = 9/4. Dividing by 3/5 means multiplying by 5/3.
  • (9/4) × (5/3) = 15/4 = 3 3/4.

10. Express 360 as a product of prime factors.

Answer:
  • 360 = 36 × 10 = (2² × 3²) × (2 × 5).
  • 360 = 2³ × 3² × 5.

11. Find the HCF and LCM of 84 and 126.

Answer:
  • 84 = 2² × 3 × 7 and 126 = 2 × 3² × 7.
  • HCF = 2 × 3 × 7 = 42. LCM = 2² × 3² × 7 = 252.

12. Evaluate 27^(2/3) and 16^(−3/4).

Answer:
  • 27^(2/3) = (∛27)² = 3² = 9.
  • 16^(−3/4) = 1/(⁴√16)³ = 1/2³ = 1/8.

13. Simplify √72 − √8.

Answer:
  • √72 = 6√2 and √8 = 2√2.
  • 6√2 − 2√2 = 4√2.

14. Rationalise 5/√3.

Answer:
  • Multiply numerator and denominator by √3.
  • 5/√3 = 5√3/3.

15. Calculate (6 × 10⁷) ÷ (2.5 × 10³). Give the answer in standard form.

Answer:
  • 6 ÷ 2.5 = 2.4 and 10⁷ ÷ 10³ = 10⁴.
  • The answer is 2.4 × 10⁴.

16. Express 0.375 as a fraction in its simplest form.

Answer:
  • 0.375 = 375/1000.
  • Divide numerator and denominator by 125 to obtain 3/8.

17. Find 135% of £240.

Answer:
  • 135% = 1.35.
  • 1.35 × £240 = £324.

18. Convert 2 hours 18 minutes to hours, and 0.065 kg to grams.

Answer:
  • 18 minutes = 18/60 = 0.3 hours, so the time is 2.3 hours.
  • 0.065 × 1000 = 65 g.

19. Estimate (49.2 × 0.198) ÷ 0.051 by rounding each number to 1 significant figure.

Answer:
  • Use (50 × 0.2) ÷ 0.05.
  • 10 ÷ 0.05 = 200. This is an estimate, not the exact value.

20. A length x is 8.4 cm when rounded to the nearest 0.1 cm. Write its error interval.

Answer:
  • Half of 0.1 cm is 0.05 cm.
  • 8.35 ≤ x < 8.45 cm. The upper endpoint is excluded because it would round to 8.5 cm.

21. A positive number y is truncated to 1 decimal place to give 3.7. Write the error interval.

Answer:
  • Truncation removes the later digits rather than rounding.
  • 3.7 ≤ y < 3.8.

3 marks

22. A code has two different letters chosen from A, B, C, D, followed by a digit from 0 to 9. How many different codes are possible?

Answer:
  • There are 4 choices for the first letter and 3 for the second.
  • There are 10 choices for the digit, including 0.
  • The total is 4 × 3 × 10 = 120 codes.

23. Let x = 0.2777…, where only the 7 repeats. Express x as a fraction.

Answer:
  • 10x = 2.777… and 100x = 27.777….
  • Subtract to remove the recurring tail: 90x = 25.
  • x = 25/90 = 5/18.

24. Simplify 4/(√5 − 1). Give an exact answer.

Answer:
  • Multiply top and bottom by the conjugate √5 + 1.
  • The denominator becomes (√5)² − 1² = 4.
  • The result is 4(√5 + 1)/4 = √5 + 1.

25. Red and blue beads are in the ratio 3:5. There are 64 beads. One quarter of the red beads are removed. What fraction of the remaining beads are red?

Answer:
  • There are 64 × 3/8 = 24 red beads and 40 blue beads.
  • One quarter of 24 is 6, leaving 18 red and 58 beads in total.
  • The required fraction is 18/58 = 9/29.

26. A florist has 96 roses and 144 tulips. She makes the greatest possible number of identical bunches, using every flower. How many bunches are made, and what does each contain?

Answer:
  • The number of bunches must divide both totals. HCF(96,144) = 48.
  • Each bunch contains 96 ÷ 48 = 2 roses.
  • Each also contains 144 ÷ 48 = 3 tulips. There are 48 bunches.

27. Without using a calculator, show that √50 lies between 7 and 8. Then decide whether it is nearer 7 or 8.

Answer:
  • 7² = 49 < 50 < 64 = 8², so 7 < √50 < 8.
  • The midpoint is 7.5, and 7.5² = 56.25.
  • Since 50 < 56.25, √50 < 7.5, so it is nearer 7.

4 marks

28. A club has £250. It buys 18 tickets at £7.65 each. It then buys as many £12.50 equipment packs as possible. Find the number of packs and the money left.

Answer:
  • Tickets cost 18 × £7.65 = £137.70.
  • Money remaining = £250 − £137.70 = £112.30.
  • £112.30 ÷ £12.50 = 8.984, so only 8 complete packs can be bought.
  • The packs cost £100, leaving £12.30.

29. A rectangle has sides √18 cm and (√8 + √2) cm. Find its exact area and perimeter.

Answer:
  • √18 = 3√2 and √8 + √2 = 3√2.
  • Area = (3√2)(3√2) = 18 cm².
  • Perimeter = 2(3√2 + 3√2) = 12√2 cm.
  • The dimensions happen to be equal, so this rectangle is also a square.

30. A car travels 150 km, rounded to the nearest 10 km, in 2.0 hours, rounded to the nearest 0.1 hour. Find upper and lower bounds for its average speed. Give the bounds to 3 decimal places.

Answer:
  • 145 ≤ distance < 155 km; 1.95 ≤ time < 2.05 hours.
  • Lower bound = 145 ÷ 2.05 = 70.731707… km/h, reported as 70.732 km/h.
  • Upper bound = 155 ÷ 1.95 = 79.487179… km/h, reported as 79.487 km/h.
  • Use the unrounded boundary values in the divisions. These are limiting bounds; the excluded endpoints need not be attained.

5 marks

31. A tank has volume 3.2 × 10⁵ cm³, rounded to 2 significant figures. Water enters at 250 cm³/s, rounded to the nearest 10 cm³/s. The tank is initially empty. Find the upper bound for the filling time in minutes, to 3 significant figures.

Answer:
  • The volume lies from 315000 up to, but not including, 325000 cm³.
  • The rate lies from 245 up to, but not including, 255 cm³/s.
  • The greatest limiting time uses the largest volume and smallest rate.
  • Upper bound = 325000 ÷ 245 = 1326.530612… seconds.
  • Divide by 60 to obtain 22.108843… minutes, so the requested bound is 22.1 minutes to 3 significant figures.

6 marks

32. A rectangular sheet is 24 cm by 18 cm, each measurement rounded to the nearest centimetre. Squares of side 3 cm are cut from each corner; the 3 cm lengths are exact. The sides are folded to make an open box. Find the upper bound for its capacity in litres.

Answer:
  • The original length is less than 24.5 cm, and the width is less than 18.5 cm.
  • Cutting two exact 3 cm strips from each dimension gives base bounds of 18.5 cm and 12.5 cm.
  • The box height is exactly 3 cm.
  • The volume increases with each positive base dimension, so its upper bound is 18.5 × 12.5 × 3 = 693.75 cm³.
  • 1000 cm³ = 1 litre.
  • The capacity has upper bound 0.69375 litres; actual capacity is less than this.
