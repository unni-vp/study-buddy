Pollutants

Text version

Fuel pollutants and their effects

Complete versus incomplete

Enough oxygen: CO₂ and water from hydrocarbons.

Insufficient oxygen: CO and soot can form.

Fuel composition determines possible products.

More complete burning does not eliminate CO₂.

Carbon monoxide

Toxic, colourless and odourless.

Strong haemoglobin binding limits oxygen transport.

Cannot rely on smell for detection.

Different gas and effect from carbon dioxide.

Sulfur dioxide

Sulfur impurities react with oxygen.

Causes respiratory problems.

Acidic moisture damages ecosystems/buildings.

Low-sulfur fuel or gas treatment can help.

Nitrogen oxides

Air nitrogen/oxygen react at high temperature.

Fuel need not contain nitrogen.

Respiratory harm and acid rain.

Control combustion/exhaust conditions.

Particulates

Soot and unburned material.

Scatter/reflect light: global dimming.

Inhalation causes health problems.

Filters target particles, not every greenhouse gas.

Evaluate control measures

Match treatment to named pollutant.

Compare effectiveness, costs and upkeep.

Include extra energy and life-cycle impacts.

Warming, acid rain and dimming are different mechanisms.
