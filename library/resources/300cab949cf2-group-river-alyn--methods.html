VelocityTime water over a measured distance using a float, or use a flow meterSpeed = distance ÷ time; exact school method is not confirmed
Bedload sizeMeasure selected stones consistentlyUse a clear sampling rule and calculate an average
Bedload shapeClassify roundness using the same chartRepeat with several stones to reduce the effect of one unusual rock
