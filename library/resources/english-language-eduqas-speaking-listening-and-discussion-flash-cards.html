What should you identify before answering an audience question?

Answer

How can you answer a challenge about cost?

Answer
  • Acknowledge the concern, explain a realistic limit or trial, and show how that addresses the proposal’s cost.
QuestionListening

Why is repeating your conclusion not always a useful response?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • It may ignore the particular question. Listening requires responding to the point that was actually raised.
QuestionRehearsal

What is a useful speaking cue?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • A brief prompt for an idea, example or transition, rather than a paragraph that must be read aloud.
QuestionRehearsal

Why practise recovery as well as fluency?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • If a phrase is forgotten, you need to return to the argument naturally instead of stopping or restarting the whole talk.
QuestionSpeaking

How should an unknown answer be handled?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Acknowledge what you cannot establish and explain what information would help. Do not invent evidence.
QuestionAudience

What can a practice listener tell you about organisation?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Ask them to identify the main claim and the reasons. Their response shows whether the argument can be followed.
QuestionDelivery

What is the purpose of a deliberate pause?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • It gives listeners time to process an important point and gives the speaker time to move to the next idea.
QuestionAssessment

Does the spoken-language endorsement add to the written GCSE grade?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • No. It is reported separately as a spoken-language endorsement.
QuestionDiscussion

How can you disagree constructively?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Refer accurately to the other person’s point, explain your reason and support it with a relevant example.
QuestionSlides

Why can a slide full of prose weaken delivery?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • It encourages reading and divides attention. Short prompts or a useful visual can support rather than duplicate the talk.
QuestionOrganisation

What should an audience understand by the end?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • They should understand the purpose, the developed main ideas and the final conclusion or request.
