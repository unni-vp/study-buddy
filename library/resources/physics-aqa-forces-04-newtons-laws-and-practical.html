Newton’s laws & practical

First law & inertia

  • Zero resultant → rest or constant velocity. Inertia is the tendency to retain that motion.
  • Balanced forces do not mean no forces. A steady car has driving force = resistance.

Second & third laws

  • F = ma, using resultant F. Inertial mass = F/a. More mass → less a for same F.
  • Third law: equal opposite forces of same type on different objects. Weight and normal force on one book are not a pair.

Terminal velocity

  • Initially weight > drag → downward acceleration. Speed rises → drag rises → resultant and a fall.
  • Drag = weight → constant terminal velocity. Opening parachute increases drag, slowing fall to a lower terminal speed.

Measure acceleration

  • Low-friction trolley, string, pulley, hanging mass. Light gates or motion sensor measure velocity changes.
  • Calculate a = Δv/Δt before hanger hits floor. Secure pulley; stop trolley safely; control friction.

Vary force

  • Transfer masses from trolley to hanger: total moving mass constant, driving weight increases.
  • For whole system, driving force ≈ hanger weight if friction negligible. Trolley tension alone is not hanger weight while accelerating.

Vary mass

  • Keep hanging mass fixed; add mass to trolley. Force approximately constant, total mass increases.
  • Repeat; a vs F should be proportional at fixed mass. a vs 1/m should be proportional at fixed force.
