Useful words and phrases

Words as useful phrases

A word is easier to remember when it has a job in a sentence. Learn la gare → à la gare → Je vais à la gare: station, at/to the station, I go to the station.

For nouns, keep article + noun + meaning together. For verbs, learn the infinitive, a useful form and the following preposition: aider quelqu'un, help someone; parler à quelqu'un, speak to someone.

An infinitive stays unchanged after a modal: je peux lire. Learn the spelling and sound together, not just an English translation.

Time, amount and opinion

These small words often decide the meaning of a whole answer.

FrenchMeaning
maintenant / aujourd'huinow / today
hier / demainyesterday / tomorrow
autrefois / à l'avenirformerly / in future
d'habitude / parfoisusually / sometimes
toujours / souvent / rarementalways / often / rarely
déjà / pas encorealready / not yet
plus / moins / assez / tropmore / less / enough / too much
beaucoup de / peu dea lot of / little or few
parce que / doncbecause / therefore
mais / cependant / pourtantbut / however / yet
à mon avis / selon moiin my opinion

Je n'aime plus ce sport means I no longer like this sport. Je n'aime pas ce sport means I do not like it. The tiny word plus changes the message.

Useful paired opinions: amusant / ennuyeux (fun/boring), utile / inutile (useful/useless), facile / difficile (easy/difficult), cher / bon marché (expensive/cheap).

Learn oppositions, not isolated words

ContrastMeaningSentence to test the distinction
toujours / jamaisalways / neverJe ne joue jamais le lundi. — I never play on Mondays.
déjà / pas encorealready / not yetJe n'ai pas encore fini. — I have not finished yet.
assez / tropenough / too muchIl y a trop de bruit. — There is too much noise.
meilleur / mieuxbetter as an adjective / better as an adverbCe film est meilleur. Elle joue mieux. — This film is better. She plays better.
avant / aprèsbefore / afterAprès les cours, je rentre. — After lessons, I go home.

These distinctions change the answer even when the topic noun stays the same. Practise a word inside a sentence, then change one detail and explain how the meaning changes.
