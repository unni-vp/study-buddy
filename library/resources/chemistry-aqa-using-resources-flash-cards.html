QuestionApply and calculate

Explain why thermosetting polymers do not melt like thermosoftening polymers.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Thermosetting chains have covalent cross-links.
  • These prevent chains moving freely on heating.
  • Thermosoftening chains are separate, so overcoming intermolecular attractions allows movement and melting.
QuestionApply and calculate

State the Haber equation and explain the gas ratio.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • N₂+3H₂ ⇌2NH₃.
  • One nitrogen molecule reacts with three hydrogen molecules.
  • Feed gases therefore use a 1:3 nitrogen:hydrogen molar ratio.
QuestionApply and calculate

Explain why high pressure favours ammonia in the Haber process.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • There are four gas moles on the reactant side and two on the product side.
  • High pressure favours fewer gas moles.
  • Equilibrium shifts towards ammonia, increasing its proportion.
