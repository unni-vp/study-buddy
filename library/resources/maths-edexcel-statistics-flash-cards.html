For 4, 7, 7, 10, 12, find the mean and median.

QuestionStratified sample

A school has 600 students, including 150 in Year 11. A proportional stratified sample contains 80 students. How many should be from Year 11?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Year 11 forms 150/600 = 1/4 of the population.
  • Select 80 × 1/4 = 20 Year 11 students randomly within that stratum.
QuestionBias

A student estimates the whole school’s favourite sport by questioning only the football team. Explain the problem and suggest an improvement.

QuestionIqr

A data set has lower quartile 18, median 24 and upper quartile 31. Find its interquartile range and interpret it.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • IQR = 31 − 18 = 13.
  • The middle 50% of the data span 13 units.
QuestionInterpolation

A line of best fit uses data for ages 10 to 16. Explain why predicting a value at age 14 is generally safer than at age 30.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Age 14 is within the observed range, so the estimate is interpolation.
  • Age 30 is extrapolation far beyond the data; the relationship may not continue.
QuestionFrequency density

A histogram class 10 < x ≤ 15 has frequency 18. Another class 15 < x ≤ 25 has frequency 24. Find their frequency densities and explain which bar is taller.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • First class width = 5, so density = 18/5 = 3.6.
  • Second class width = 10, so density = 24/10 = 2.4.
  • The first bar is taller even though it contains fewer values. In a histogram, area represents frequency.
QuestionCombined means

Eight parcels have mean mass 2.5 kg. Three of them have mean mass 1.5 kg. Find the mean mass of the other five parcels.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Mass of all eight = 8 × 2.5 = 20 kg.
  • Mass of the three = 3 × 1.5 = 4.5 kg.
  • The other five total 15.5 kg, so their mean is 15.5/5 = 3.1 kg.
QuestionAppropriate averages

Salaries in a small business are £20000, £22000, £23000, £25000 and £150000. Find the mean and median. Explain which better describes a typical salary here.

Answer

  • The median is £23000.
  • The very high salary pulls up the mean, so the median better describes the typical employee in this set.
QuestionMisleading chart

A bar chart compares values 90 and 100, but its vertical axis starts at 80. Explain why it may exaggerate the difference and how to improve it.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • The visible bar heights are proportional to 10 and 20, making one appear twice the other.
  • The actual values are 90 and 100, so the second is only about 11.1% greater.
  • Use a zero baseline for bars, or display the values and any axis break clearly without implying bar-length proportionality.
