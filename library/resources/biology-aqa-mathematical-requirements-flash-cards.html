QuestionMulti-step application

A 10 mm inhibition zone contains a 4 mm disc. Calculate clear-agar area excluding the disc.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Zone radius = 5 mm; total area = 25π mm².

  • Clear area = (25−4)π = 21π mm².

QuestionMulti-step application

Two independent births each have affected probability 1/4. Find probabilities of both affected and exactly one affected.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Both affected = 1/4 ×1/4 = 1/16.
  • First affected, second unaffected = 1/4 ×3/4 = 3/16.
  • First unaffected, second affected also 3/16.
  • Exactly one = 3/16+3/16 = 6/16 = 3/8.
