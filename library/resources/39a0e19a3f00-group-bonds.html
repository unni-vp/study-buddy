Chemical bonding

Three types of bonding

Chemical bonds hold particles together through electrostatic attraction. The important question is: which charged particles attract each other?

BondWhat happensWhat attracts?
IonicElectrons transfer from metal atoms to non-metal atoms, forming ionsOppositely charged ions
CovalentNon-metal atoms share pairs of electronsPositive nuclei and the shared negative electrons
MetallicOuter electrons become delocalised throughout a metalPositive metal ions and delocalised electrons

The bonds themselves are strong. A low boiling point does not automatically mean weak chemical bonds: it may mean weak forces between separate molecules.
