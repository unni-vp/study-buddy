Photosynthesis & limiting factors

The reaction

  • 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
  • Chlorophyll absorbs light; energy transfers from the environment to chloroplasts. Endothermic.
  • CO₂ enters stomata; roots/xylem supply water. Light is energy, not a material reactant.

Glucose pathways

  • Respiration releases energy; starch and fats/oils store resources. Cellulose strengthens cell walls.
  • Glucose and nitrate ions supply materials for amino acids → proteins.

Four interacting factors

  • More light or CO₂ raises rate only while that factor limits it.
  • Warming towards optimum raises enzyme activity; excess heat can denature enzymes.
  • Less chlorophyll → less light absorption. Magnesium deficiency or disease can reduce chlorophyll.

Interpret the plateau

  • Rising rate with light → light is limiting. Plateau → another factor limits rate.
  • Compare curves at the same other conditions: if added CO₂ raises rate, CO₂ had limited it.
  • A plateau alone does not identify which factor limits rate.

Inverse-square law

  • Intensity ∝ 1/distance². I₂/I₁ = (d₁/d₂)².
  • 20 → 40 cm: intensity becomes ¼. 30 → 15 cm: becomes 4×.
  • Use consistent units. Photosynthesis rate need not change by the same factor.

Greenhouse decisions

  • Adding heat, light or CO₂ costs money. Improve the actual limiting factor.
  • Extra profit = extra crop income − extra cost. £240 − £170 = £70.
  • Highest photosynthesis rate is not always the most profitable setting.
