Anion tests and unknown salts

Carbonates

Add dilute acid.

Evolved gas turns limewater milky.

Carbon dioxide supports carbonate presence.

State gas test, not effervescence alone.

Chloride and bromide

Nitric acid then silver nitrate.

Chloride white, bromide cream.

Use fresh dissolved sample.

Acid removes carbonate interference.

Iodide

Nitric acid then silver nitrate.

Yellow silver iodide precipitate.

Do not acidify halide test with HCl.

Added chloride could cause a false result.

Sulfate

Hydrochloric acid then barium chloride.

White barium sulfate precipitate.

Do not use sulfuric acid: it adds sulfate.

Ba²⁺+SO₄²⁻ →BaSO₄(s).

Unknown compound

Identify cation and anion separately.

Lilac flame +cream halide test supports KBr.

Balance charges in proposed formula.

White alone is insufficient without named reagents.

Validity and safety

Separate portions prevent cross-contamination.

Record observations before identification.

Small quantities, eye protection, approved disposal.

Never taste unknown salts.
