Modal & reflexive verbs

Modals and reflexives

Modals add can, must, want or know how. The next verb is an infinitive: je peux aider.

Je voudrais means I would like; j'ai dû partir means I had to leave.

Reflexives use me, te, se, nous, vous, se before the verb: je me lave; nous nous levons. With a following infinitive: je vais me lever. Some plural uses are reciprocal: nous nous parlons — we talk to each other.

Many reflexive perfect forms use être and subject agreement: elle s'est levée. When the direct object follows, agreement works differently: elle s'est lavé les mains. Do not add -e automatically to every reflexive participle.
