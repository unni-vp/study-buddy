Motor effect and devices

Force direction

A wire carrying current in a magnetic field feels a force.

Left hand: first finger field, second current, thumb force.

All three are mutually perpendicular in the maximum-force case.

Calculate force

F = BIl: N, T, A and m.

Use perpendicular wire length within the field.

0.20 T × 4 A × 0.15 m = 0.12 N.

1 mT = 10⁻³ T.

Change the force

Greater B, I or perpendicular length gives greater force.

Reverse current or field alone to reverse force.

Reverse both: original force direction.

Parallel wire: no motor force in this model.

Coil rotation

Opposite coil sides carry opposite currents, so their forces oppose.

Different positions give a turning effect.

Electrical input supplies motion.

Greater field or current increases the turning effect.

Continuous DC motor

Split ring: reverses coil current every half-turn.

Turning effect continues in the same rotational direction.

Brushes maintain contact.

Do not confuse split ring with alternator slip rings.

Loudspeaker

Changing current makes changing motor force on a coil.

Coil and attached cone vibrate, making air pressure waves.

Frequency follows signal. Larger vibration usually means louder sound.

Headphones use the same principle.
