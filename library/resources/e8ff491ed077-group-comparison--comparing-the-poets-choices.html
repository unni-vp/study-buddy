Comparing poems

Comparing the poets’ choices

Sharing a subject gives you a starting point. A useful comparison explains what each poem suggests about that subject and how its choices produce the difference.

PairShared concernDifference to develop
The Schoolboy and Blackberry PickingChildhood pleasure meets restriction or loss.Blake turns towards an argument about children's development. Heaney revisits disappointment that knowledge does not prevent.
Catrin and Origin StoryFamily helps shape identity.Clarke presents intimacy alongside a child's growing independence. Ewing uses the comic book to find value in the relationship from which the speaker was born.
Sonnet 29 and Dusting the PhoneAn absent loved person occupies the speaker's thoughts.Barrett Browning turns from imaginative substitutes towards real presence. Kay leaves the speaker caught in waiting and uncertainty.
Remains and War PhotographerWar continues to affect a person after the immediate event.Armitage gives the troubled participant a direct voice. Duffy follows a witness whose careful work contrasts with distant readers' brief attention.
Kamikaze and Drummer HodgeWar shapes death, memory and belonging.Garland explores a survivor's rejection by his family. Hardy gives a dead soldier a lasting place in an unfamiliar landscape.
I Shall Return and I Wandered Lonely as a CloudRemembered nature offers emotional connection.McKay's return is a repeated future hope. Wordsworth describes comfort already recovered through memory.

For example, a paragraph on memory after war can first identify how an experience remains present, then compare the voices and endings. Remains moves from a shared military episode into private torment. War Photographer moves from the darkroom towards the limited response of an editor and readers. The first centres on an individual unable to escape; the second also questions how others receive suffering.

These are flexible pairings. Select a poem that answers the question's particular focus, and keep the specified poem involved throughout the comparison. A shared keyword is less valuable than a connection you can explain with evidence from both poems.
