QuestionParallel angles

Two co-interior angles between parallel lines are (3x + 10)° and (2x + 20)°. Find x.

Answer
  • Co-interior angles sum to 180°: 3x + 10 + 2x + 20 = 180.

QuestionCongruence

Two triangles each have sides of lengths 5 cm and 7 cm. Their included angle is 40°. Explain why they are congruent.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • They have two corresponding sides and the included angle equal.
  • This is the SAS congruence criterion. An angle not included between the sides would not establish SAS.
QuestionTranslation

QuestionCentre angle

Points A, B and C lie on a circle with centre O. C lies on the major arc AB. The smaller angle AOB is 126°. Find angle ACB.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • The angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference standing on the same arc.
  • Angle ACB = 126°/2 = 63°.

QuestionTrapezium area

QuestionLocus

A garden plan has points A and B. Describe the region that is within 4 m of A and closer to A than to B. State how the boundaries are constructed.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Draw a circle centred at A with radius representing 4 m. The required points are inside or on it.
  • Construct the perpendicular bisector of AB and choose the side containing A.
  • Take the overlap of these regions. The bisector itself is excluded because points on it are equally close, not closer to A.
QuestionCombined transformations

Reflect a shape in the y-axis, then in the x-axis. Describe the equivalent single transformation and name two properties it preserves.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • The coordinates change (x,y) to (−x,y), then to (−x,−y).
  • This is a rotation of 180° about the origin.
  • It preserves lengths and angles, so the image is congruent to the original.
QuestionSector

A sector has radius 9 cm and angle 80°. Find its exact area and arc length.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Area = (80/360) × π × 9² = 18π cm².
  • Arc length = (80/360) × 2π × 9 = 4π cm.
  • If its perimeter were needed, add both radii to the arc length.
QuestionSine rule

In triangle ABC, A = 35°, B = 70° and side a = 8 cm. Find side b to 3 significant figures.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • a and b are opposite angles A and B respectively.
  • b/sin 70° = 8/sin 35°, so b = 8 sin 70°/sin 35°.
  • b = 13.1 cm to 3 significant figures.
QuestionCosine rule

Two sides of a triangle are 7 cm and 9 cm and their included angle is 60°. Find the third side exactly.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Use the cosine rule with the unknown side opposite the known angle.
  • a² = 7² + 9² − 2(7)(9)cos 60° = 49 + 81 − 63 = 67.
  • a = √67 cm.
QuestionTriangle area

A triangle has sides 10 cm and 8 cm enclosing an angle of 30°. Find its area.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Use area = 1/2 ab sin C.
  • Area = 1/2 × 10 × 8 × sin 30°.
  • The area is 20 cm².

QuestionArea scale

Two similar triangles have areas 36 cm² and 81 cm². A side of the smaller triangle is 8 cm. Find the corresponding larger side.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Area factor = 81/36 = 9/4.
  • Length factor = √(9/4) = 3/2.
  • The larger side is 8 × 3/2 = 12 cm.
