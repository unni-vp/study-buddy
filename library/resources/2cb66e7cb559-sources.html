How the papers inform this practice

The sample is June 2019, 2023 and 2024, Component 1, Macbeth Q21–22. Extract analysis occurs in all three papers. Lady Macbeth is the whole-play focus in two (2019 and 2023); sympathy for a chosen character appears in 2024. These overlapping observations describe this small sample, not a forecast. The remaining essays practise character, theme and dramatic-method skills required by the current specification. The Shakespeare essay has 20 literary marks plus 5 for writing accuracy; the extract has 15. AO3 is not assessed in this Shakespeare section.
