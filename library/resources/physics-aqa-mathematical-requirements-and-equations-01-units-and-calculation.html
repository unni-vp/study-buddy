Units and calculation methods

Convert first

kg, m, s, A and matching SI units are usual starting points.

Minutes ×60, hours ×3,600. km/h ÷3.6.

milli 10⁻³, micro 10⁻⁶, nano 10⁻⁹.

Convert speed or extension before squaring.

Area and volume

1 cm² = 10⁻⁴ m². 1 cm³ = 10⁻⁶ m³.

1 g/cm³ = 1,000 kg/m³.

20 cm² = 0.0020 m².

Each dimension contributes a conversion factor.

Standard form

Coefficient at least 1 and less than 10.

48,000 = 4.8 × 10⁴.

0.00032 = 3.2 × 10⁻⁴.

Use calculator exponent key correctly and check order of magnitude.

Rearrange

Keep both sides balanced. V = IR gives R = V/I.

v = √(2Eₖ/m) follows from Eₖ = ½mv².

Do not forget square root.

Keep symbols distinct: pressure p differs from momentum p.

Proportion

Direct: y = kx. Double x, double y.

Inverse: y = k/x. Double x, halve y.

Squared: y = kx². Double x, quadruple y.

Keep other relevant factors fixed.

Precision and checks

0.00450 has three significant figures.

Keep intermediate digits. Round final answer sensibly.

Use units and plausible values to detect errors.

A car at 10,000 m/s needs a conversion check.
