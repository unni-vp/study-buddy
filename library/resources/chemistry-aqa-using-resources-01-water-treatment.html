Water treatment

Text version

Potable water, wastewater and analysis

Potable versus pure

Safe levels of microbes and dissolved substances.

Potable water usually contains dissolved salts.

Source quality determines treatment.

Clear water or normal pH alone is not proof of safety.

Freshwater

Suitable source, then filter beds.

Filtration removes suspended solids.

Chlorine/ozone/UV kills microorganisms.

Filtering alone does not remove all dissolved salts.

Desalination

Distillation: evaporate then condense water.

Reverse osmosis: pressure drives membrane separation.

Salt retained or concentrated separately.

Compare high energy demand and local resources.

Wastewater

Screening and grit removal.

Sedimentation: sludge and effluent.

Sludge anaerobic digestion; effluent aerobic treatment.

Industrial chemicals may need extra removal.

Water practical

pH indicator or calibrated probe.

Known volume evaporated in weighed basin.

Cool/reweigh: residue mass by subtraction.

Distil another portion and compare samples.

Reliability and safety

Same volumes or compare concentration.

Small masses limited by balance resolution.

Safe heating/eye protection; open receiver.

Do not distil dry or taste samples.
