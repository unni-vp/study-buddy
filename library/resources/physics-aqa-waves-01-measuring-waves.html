Measuring waves

Vibrations

Transverse: perpendicular to energy transfer.

Longitudinal: parallel. Compressions are crowded, rarefactions spread out.

A cork bobs while ripples travel. Matter does not travel overall with the wave.

Amplitude and wavelength

Amplitude: maximum displacement from rest.

Wavelength: equivalent point to next equivalent point.

Crest-to-trough height is twice amplitude.

Frequency and period

Frequency: complete waves per second, Hz.

Period: seconds for one complete vibration.

T = 1/f. 20 Hz gives 0.050 s.

A time graph gives period. A distance graph gives wavelength.

Wave equation

v = fλ. Speed in m/s, wavelength in m.

5 Hz × 0.060 m = 0.30 m/s.

At a boundary, frequency stays constant.

Changed speed means changed wavelength.

Ripple tank

Use a lamp, dipper and shallow water. Keep depth fixed.

Frequency: dipper setting or waves/time.

Measure several wavelengths then divide. Six crests enclose five wavelengths.

Repeat. Calculate v = fλ.

String and sound

String: stationary loops each span λ/2. Use signal-generator f and v = fλ.

Sound: two microphones record the same pulse.

v = separation/time delay. Use electronic timing and repeat.

Secure loads. Keep water away from electrics.
