Find the mass of aluminium obtained from 20.4 g Al₂O₃. Equation: 2Al₂O₃ → 4Al + 3O₂. Aᵣ: Al = 27, O = 16. Assume complete conversion.

QuestionMulti-step application

3.2 g methane reacts with 12.8 g oxygen to produce 8.8 g carbon dioxide and 7.2 g water. Deduce the balanced equation. Mᵣ: CH₄ = 16, O₂ = 32, CO₂ = 44, H₂O = 18.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Moles: CH₄ 0.20; O₂ 0.40; CO₂ 0.20; H₂O 0.40.
  • Divide by 0.20 to get 1 : 2 : 1 : 2.
  • CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O.
  • Atom totals and masses balance: 16.0 g reactants gives 16.0 g products.
QuestionMulti-step application

Nitrogen and hydrogen react: N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃. Mix 0.20 mol N₂ with 0.30 mol H₂. Identify the limiting reactant and calculate the amount of ammonia and the amount of nitrogen left.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • 0.30 mol H₂ needs 0.30/3 = 0.10 mol N₂, less than the 0.20 mol available.
  • Hydrogen is limiting.
  • Ammonia formed = 0.30 × 2/3 = 0.20 mol.
  • Nitrogen left = 0.20 − 0.10 = 0.10 mol.
QuestionMulti-step application

At the same temperature and pressure, 80 cm³ hydrogen is mixed with 50 cm³ oxygen and ignited. Equation: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. Calculate the oxygen volume left after reaction and explain why the equation does not give the volume of liquid water.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • 80 cm³ H₂ needs 40 cm³ O₂.
  • Hydrogen is limiting, so oxygen left = 50 − 40 = 10 cm³.
  • Gas volume ratios equal mole ratios at the same temperature and pressure.
  • Liquid water is not a gas, so the gas-volume rule cannot give its volume.
QuestionMulti-step application

A student heats 20.0 g CaCO₃ and collects 8.4 g CaO. Calculate the percentage yield. Equation: CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂. Mᵣ: CaCO₃ = 100, CaO = 56.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • CaCO₃ moles = 20.0/100 = 0.200 mol.
  • CaO theoretical moles = 0.200; theoretical mass = 0.200 × 56 = 11.2 g.
  • Yield = 8.4/11.2 × 100.
  • 75%.
QuestionMulti-step application

For X₂O₃ + 3H₂ → 2X + 3H₂O, the atom economy for X is 75.0%. Calculate Aᵣ of X. Aᵣ: H = 1, O = 16.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Let Aᵣ of X = x. Desired mass from equation = 2x; reactant total = 2x + 54.
  • 2x/(2x + 54) = 0.750.
  • 2x = 1.5x + 40.5, so 0.5x = 40.5.
  • x = 81.0.
