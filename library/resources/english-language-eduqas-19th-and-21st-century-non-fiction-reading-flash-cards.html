QuestionReading skills

What is the difference between form and purpose?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Form is the kind of text. Purpose is what the writer wants it to achieve; one form can serve several purposes.

What makes a shared-issue plan useful?

QuestionEvidence

How do you distinguish a writer’s claim from an established fact?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Attribute it accurately: the writer argues, reports or suggests. Assess the evidence supplied rather than treating every assertion as proven.
QuestionApplication

Can you assume an older text is more formal?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • No. Tone must be supported by the actual language and presentation, not only the date.
QuestionComparison

Why is “whereas” not enough to make a developed comparison?

Answer

How can a concession affect an argument?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer

QuestionReading skills

How should an unfamiliar word be approached?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Read its sentence and neighbours, identify its grammatical role, and test a meaning against the argument.
QuestionEvidence

Why must the source of each quotation remain clear?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • A comparison depends on knowing which writer expresses each idea. Misattribution can reverse the interpretation.
QuestionApplication

What should an answer about a writer’s attitude explain?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • State the attitude, support it with relevant words or choices, and explain how the text conveys it.
QuestionEvaluation

Should you invent statistics to challenge a source?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • No. Evaluate the evidence and reasoning actually given, using supported observations rather than fabricated counter-evidence.
QuestionReading skills

How does a reason differ from a method?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • A reason explains why an action is worthwhile. A method explains how it is carried out.
QuestionComparison

Must two texts express complete opposites to be compared?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • No. They may share a broad aim while differing in emphasis, flexibility, tone or method.
QuestionExam application

What should a final two-text check include?

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Check the shared focus, use of both sources, accurate attribution and the requested balance of ideas and methods.
