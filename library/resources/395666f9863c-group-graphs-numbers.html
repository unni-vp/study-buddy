Choosing the right graph

Data or purposeSuitable presentation
CategoriesBar chart
Continuous grouped dataEqual-interval histogram
Change over timeLine graph
Relationship between two variablesScatter graph
Parts of a wholePie chart
Temperature and rainfall through a yearClimate graph
Population age and sexPopulation pyramid
Direction and frequencyRose chart
Values at places on a mapProportional symbols

Building a clear presentation

Use a suitable title, labelled axes, units, even scale and an accurate key. Choose intervals that use the available space without distorting the pattern.

To analyse, use pattern + evidence + exception:

  1. State the overall trend.
  2. Support it with values and locations.
  3. Identify and suggest a reason for an anomaly.
