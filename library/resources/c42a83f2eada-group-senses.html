Eye structures and light

The eye detects light intensity and colour. Light is refracted to form an image on the retina.

StructureFunction
CorneaTransparent curved front; refracts much of the incoming light
IrisMuscles adjust pupil size and therefore how much light enters
PupilOpening through which light enters; it is not a muscle
LensChanges shape to focus on near or distant objects
Ciliary musclesChange tension in the suspensory ligaments
Suspensory ligamentsConnect to the lens and transmit tension, changing its shape
RetinaContains receptors sensitive to light; starts nervous signals
Optic nerveCarries impulses from retina to brain
ScleraTough outer layer; protects and supports the eye
