Listening and dictation questions & answers

1 mark2 marks3 marks4 marks

1 mark

1. In listening comprehension, how many times is each item played?

Answer:

Twice.

2. In dictation, what language should you write?

Answer:

French.

3. How many times is each dictation sentence played?

Answer:

Three times.

4. Translate: maintenant.

Answer:

Now.

5. Translate: sauf.

Answer:

Except.

6. Translate: trop cher.

Answer:

Too expensive.

7. Translate: moins souvent.

Answer:

Less often.

8. Translate: ne...jamais.

Answer:

Never.

9. Write cinquante as a number.

Answer:

50.

10. Write quinze as a number.

Answer:

15.

2 marks

11. For this written listening-skills example, identify the current preference: Je voulais sortir, mais maintenant je préfère lire à la maison.

Answer:

Reading at home.

Mais maintenant moves from the earlier wish to the current preference.

12. Read: Mon frère aime le foot, mais moi, je préfère le tennis. Which sport belongs to each person?

Answer:
  • Brother: football.
  • Speaker: tennis.

13. Read: La chambre était petite, mais propre. Give the drawback and positive detail.

Answer:
  • It was small.
  • It was clean.

14. Read: Le train part à huit heures, pardon, à huit heures et demie. Give the final departure time and the clue that it changes.

Answer:

8:30; pardon introduces a correction.

15. Read: Il n'y a pas assez de bus. Why is 'there are no buses' inaccurate?

Answer:

The speaker says there are not enough buses. It does not mean there are none.

16. Correct the final word: J'ai regarde un film.

Answer:

J'ai regardé un film.

The avoir perfect needs a past participle.

17. Correct the final word: Je vais regardé un film.

Answer:

Je vais regarder un film.

Aller + infinitive gives the future plan.

18. Correct: Les enfants joue.

Answer:

Les enfants jouent.

The plural subject takes -ent, even when it is silent.

19. Explain two useful checks on the second play of a comprehension item.

Answer:
  • Confirm the requested detail and who says it.
  • Check a negative, contrast, correction or time reference that could change the answer.

20. What are the two parts of the dictation assessment?

Answer:
  • Communication of meaning: 5 marks.
  • Transcription and grammatical accuracy: 5 marks.

Both are assessed across the extracts as a whole.

3 marks

21. Read: Hier, nous avons visité un musée. Demain, nous allons aller au parc parce qu'il fera beau. Give yesterday's activity, tomorrow's place and the reason.

Answer:
  • Visited a museum.
  • The park.
  • The weather will be nice.

22. Explain the three dictation plays and what to do during each.

Answer:
  • Full sentence: establish the meaning.
  • Short sections: write words and order.
  • Full sentence again: check missing words and grammar.

23. Correct: Mes amis est arrivé hier. (male or mixed group)

Answer:

Mes amis sont arrivés hier.

Amis is plural; use sont; arrivé takes plural -s after être.

24. Explain how a learner should handle an unfamiliar word in dictation.

Answer:
  • Listen for its sound groups.
  • Use those sounds to attempt a plausible spelling.
  • Check how the word fits the sentence rather than abandoning the whole extract.

4 marks

25. Read: D'habitude, je voyage en voiture. L'année dernière, j'ai pris le train parce que c'était moins cher. Demain, je vais prendre le bus. Give the usual transport, last year's transport, its advantage and tomorrow's transport.

Answer:
  • Usually: car.
  • Last year: train.
  • It was cheaper.
  • Tomorrow: bus.

26. Explain why translating dictation into English and counting one mark per word are both mistakes.

Answer:
  • Dictation requires the French heard.
  • English translation does not fulfil that task.
  • The meaning is assessed holistically for 5 marks.
  • Spelling and grammatical accuracy are assessed holistically for a further 5, not one per word.
