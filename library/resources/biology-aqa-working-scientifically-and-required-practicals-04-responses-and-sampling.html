Responses and ecological sampling

Reaction-time setup

Zero ruler at finger gap, same hand/posture.

Unpredictable release reduces anticipation.

Catch distance converted to reaction time.

Repeat both safe, defined conditions.

Reaction-time validity

Practice and balanced condition order.

Several consenting participants.

Compare mean times and variability.

Learning/fatigue and sample choice limit conclusions.

Light-response seedlings

Similar species, age and starting size.

One-sided versus suitable all-round light.

Same water, temperature, medium and time.

Record lengths plus labelled growth drawings.

Gravity-response seedlings

Horizontal setup with suitable control.

Darkness removes directional light effect.

Shoots: auxin promotes lower-side elongation.

Roots: high lower-side auxin inhibits elongation.

Population estimate

Random coordinates, equal quadrats, counting rule.

Mean count × habitat area/quadrat area.

Many representative samples, correct units.

Estimate is not an exact population census.

Distribution gradient

Regular quadrats along transect.

Measure abundance and abiotic factor at sites.

Repeat parallel transects; consider other factors.

Protect habitat and choose safe routes.
