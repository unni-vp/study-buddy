Metal recovery, recycling and life cycles

Biological extraction

Low-grade ore has little metal compound.

Phytomining: plants absorb, harvest, burn.

Bioleaching: bacteria produce leachate.

Ash/solution contains compounds, not pure metal.

Copper recovery

Scrap iron displaces less-reactive copper.

Fe+CuSO₄ →FeSO₄+Cu.

Electrolysis can also recover metal.

Compare speed, land, energy, yield and pollution.

LCA stages

Raw materials and manufacture/packaging.

Use throughout lifetime, then disposal/recovery.

Include transport at every stage.

Compare the same service and lifetime.

LCA limitations

Energy/water/resources can be quantified.

Pollutant-effect weighting involves judgements.

Selective scope can mislead advertising.

State assumptions and data quality.

Reduce, reuse, recycle

Reduce unnecessary demand.

Reuse bottles

washing/return transport matter.

Recycle by melting/reforming or appropriate process.

Sorting needs depend on final product properties.

Compare bags and bottles

Paper renewable input but processing impacts.

Plastic often finite feedstock but low transport mass.

Durability and actual reuse count affect outcome.

Avoid universal “best” claims without evidence.
