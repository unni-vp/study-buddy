Causes & consequences

Natural causes of climate change

CauseHow it changes climateImportant qualification
Orbital changesChanges in the shape of Earth's orbit, its tilt and the direction of its axis alter the seasonal and geographical distribution of sunlight.They act over thousands of years and help explain glacial cycles, rather than the rapid warming of recent decades.
Solar variationChanges in the Sun's energy output slightly alter the energy received by Earth.The approximately 11-year sunspot cycle is a small variation; it does not explain the modern warming trend on its own.
Major volcanic eruptionsSulphur dioxide reaching the stratosphere forms reflective aerosols, reducing incoming sunlight and causing temporary cooling.Not every eruption has this effect. Ash tends to settle faster, while aerosols can persist longer.

Tilt changes the contrast between seasons. Precession is the slow wobble of the axis, which changes the timing of seasons relative to Earth's position in its orbit. It does not mean Earth's daily rotation stops or reverses.
