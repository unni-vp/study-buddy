An object accelerates uniformly from 3.0 m/s to 7.0 m/s over 10 m. Calculate acceleration using v² − u² = 2as.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Rearrange: a = (v² − u²)/(2s).
  • v² − u² = 7.0² − 3.0² = 40 m²/s².
  • a = 40/(2 × 10).
  • a = 2.0 m/s², using constant acceleration.
QuestionMulti-step application

A 50 kg load is lifted 2.0 m in 5.0 s by a motor with efficiency 0.80. Use g = 10 N/kg. Calculate input power.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Useful energy = mgh = 50 × 10 × 2.0 = 1,000 J.
  • Useful power = 1,000/5.0 = 200 W.
  • Input power = useful power/efficiency = 200/0.80.
  • 250 W.
QuestionMulti-step application

Velocity rises linearly from 0 to 6.0 m/s in 4.0 s, then stays at 6.0 m/s for another 5.0 s. Calculate total displacement.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Accelerating section: triangle area = ½ × 4.0 × 6.0 = 12 m.
  • Steady section: rectangle area = 5.0 × 6.0 = 30 m.
  • Total = 12 + 30 = 42 m.
  • All velocities are positive, so distance also equals 42 m.
QuestionMulti-step application

An ideal transformer has a 240 V primary with 1,200 turns and a secondary with 60 turns. Secondary current is 2.0 A. Calculate secondary voltage and primary current.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Vₛ = 240 × 60/1,200 = 12 V.
  • Secondary power = 12 × 2.0 = 24 W.
  • Ideal primary power also equals 24 W.
  • Iₚ = 24/240 = 0.10 A.

Answer
