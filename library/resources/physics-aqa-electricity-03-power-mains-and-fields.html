Power, mains & fields

Energy & power

  • P = VI; P = I²R; E = Pt; E = QV. Use W, V, A, Ω, J, s, C.
  • 12 V × 2 A = 24 W; for 30 s → 720 J. Double I at fixed R → four times heating power.

Mains & protection

  • UK: 230 V, 50 Hz ac; polarity reverses. Cell: dc.
  • Live brown; neutral blue near 0 V; earth green/yellow, 0 V. Earth normally has no current.
  • Live-to-case fault → earth current → fuse/breaker disconnects. Live can remain dangerous with switch open.

National Grid

  • Step up V for transmission; for same P = VI, current falls.
  • Cable heating I²R falls. Step down V for consumers. High voltage does not lower cable resistance.

Charging by friction

  • Electrons transfer between insulators; protons remain in nuclei. Equal opposite charges result.
  • Gain electrons → negative. Lose electrons → positive. Like charges repel, unlike attract.

Electric field

  • Force on a positive test charge defines direction. Isolated + sphere: outwards; − sphere: inwards.
  • Closer to sphere → stronger field. Lines never cross.

Sparks

  • Large charge imbalance can give a strong field. Air ionises and becomes conducting.
  • Charge flows suddenly through the air, reducing the imbalance. Force can act before contact.
