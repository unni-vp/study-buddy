Urban change

City size, influence & growth

TermMeaningImportant distinction
CityA large, densely populated urban settlement with varied functions.Official definitions and boundaries differ between countries.
MegacityAn urban area with more than 10 million people.This describes population size, rather than its global influence.
World cityA city with major international influence through finance, business, culture or politics.A world city does not have to be a megacity.
UrbanisationAn increase in the proportion of a population living in urban areas.This is a percentage, not simply an increase in city population.
Urban growthAn increase in the number of people living in urban areas.Numbers can rise without the urban share increasing.

Many ACs already have a high urban share and relatively slow urban growth. Many LIDCs and EDCs have faster growth as migration and births add to city populations. Many of the world's largest cities are in Asia, while rapid growth also occurs across parts of Africa. A fast-growing country is not necessarily the most urbanised one.
