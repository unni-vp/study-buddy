QuestionApply and calculate

Explain how the Liverpool environmental-quality data was collected.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • At each site, factors including litter, graffiti, noise, traffic, building condition and attractiveness were assessed.
  • Each factor was given a score using the survey scale.
  • Scores were added to produce a total that could be compared between sites.

QuestionApply and calculate

Explain why bedload may become smaller and rounder downstream.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Stones are transported for longer distances downstream.
  • They collide with each other and the river bed.
  • Attrition chips edges, making them smaller and rounder.
QuestionApply and calculate

Explain why River Alyn velocity tended to increase downstream.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Tributaries and runoff add water, increasing discharge.
  • The channel becomes larger and usually more efficient.
  • Proportionally less water is slowed by contact with the bed and banks.
QuestionMulti-step application

Explain the main conclusion from the Liverpool investigation.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Environmental quality varied across the sampled parts of the town centre.
  • Central shopping areas generally had the highest quality scores.
  • These areas also had high pedestrian flow and concentrated retail and services.
  • Quieter streets away from the centre generally had lower scores and fewer pedestrians.
QuestionMulti-step application

Explain the main conclusion from the River Alyn investigation.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Width and depth generally increased downstream.
  • Velocity also tended to increase as the channel became more efficient.
  • Bedload became smaller and rounder through transport and attrition.
  • The overall pattern matched river theory, although local conditions may have affected individual sites.
QuestionMulti-step application

Explain how the reliability of pedestrian-flow data could be improved.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Repeat counts at each site rather than relying on one result.
  • Use the same count duration and clear counting rules.
  • Repeat at comparable times or days to reduce temporary effects.
  • Calculate a mean so one unusual count has less influence.
QuestionMulti-step application

Explain how the River Alyn investigation could improve its bedload sample.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Use the same systematic selection rule at every site.
  • Measure a larger number of stones.
  • Use the same equipment and roundness chart consistently.
  • Calculate a mean size and compare the distribution rather than one stone.
