QuestionApply and calculate

Explain why extrapolation is less reliable than interpolation.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Extrapolation predicts beyond the measured data range.
  • The existing trend may not continue there.
  • No observations are available to check the estimate directly.

Answer
  • State whether correlation is positive, negative or absent and comment on its strength.

QuestionMulti-step application

Explain why systematic sampling may be suitable along an urban transect.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Regular intervals give coverage from one end of the route to the other.
  • The rule is simple and repeatable, improving reliability.
  • It reduces the researcher's freedom to choose only convenient sites.
  • However, a repeating pattern could coincide with the interval and bias the sample.
QuestionMulti-step application

Explain how a geographer could improve the accuracy and reliability of river velocity measurements.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Measure a fixed distance carefully and use the same method at every site.
  • Repeat readings and calculate a mean to reduce the influence of unusual results.
  • Use calibrated equipment and record units consistently.
  • Keep the position in the channel or release method consistent where possible.
