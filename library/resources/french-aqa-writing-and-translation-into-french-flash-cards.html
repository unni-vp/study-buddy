QuestionApply the language

Explain the difference between je travaillerai, je vais travailler and je travaillerais.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Je travaillerai: I will work, simple future.
  • Je vais travailler: I am going to work, near future.
  • Je travaillerais: I would work, conditional.
