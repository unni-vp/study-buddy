Circuit investigations

Measurement circuit

  • Low-voltage supply, switch, ammeter and test component in series. Voltmeter across test component.
  • Variable resistor controls current. Never put an ammeter directly across a supply.

Quality & safety

  • Record matched V and I; calculate R = V/I. Secure connections; check meter ranges and zero.
  • Repeat; avoid hot wire; switch off between readings. A repeated zero error is not removed by averaging.

Length of wire

  • Change measured wire length using movable contact and ruler.
  • Keep material, cross-sectional area and temperature fixed. Low current limits heating.
  • R vs length should be proportional for uniform wire at constant temperature.

Combinations

  • Measure each resistor, then the same pair in series and parallel.
  • Series total = sum. Parallel total is less than either. Extra parallel path increases total current at fixed V.

I–V method

  • Vary pd, record paired I/V readings across a useful range. Reverse supply for negative values.
  • Repeat for resistor, lamp and diode; protective series resistor for diode. Stay within ratings.

Graph conclusions

  • Ohmic: straight through origin if temperature constant. Lamp: flatter at larger |V| as it heats.
  • Diode: forward conduction, tiny reverse current. Draw smooth best-fit curves; do not connect noisy points mechanically.
