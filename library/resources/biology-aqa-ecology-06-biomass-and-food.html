Biomass transfer and food security

Trophic levels

1: producers. 2: primary consumers.

3: secondary. 4: tertiary consumers.

Apex predator has no predator in that web.

Decomposers digest externally and absorb products.

Mass-scaled pyramid

Example masses, same comparison basis.

Transfer losses

Not all material eaten or absorbed.

Faeces egested. Urine excreted.

Respiration releases carbon dioxide/water.

Next/previous biomass ×100 gives efficiency.

Food-security threats

Population growth and changing diets.

Pests, pathogens, rainfall failure.

Input costs and conflict affect access.

Enough food requires sustainable supply.

Farming and fishing

Suitable warmth/limited movement improve growth efficiency.

Protein supports growth

consider welfare and waste.

Biotechnology

GM bacteria produce purified human insulin.

GM crops improve yield or nutrition.

Golden rice supplies a vitamin-A precursor.
