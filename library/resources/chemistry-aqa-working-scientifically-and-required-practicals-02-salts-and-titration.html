Salt preparation and titration

Soluble salt method

Warm dilute acid, add excess insoluble oxide/carbonate.

Filter excess solid, keep filtrate.

Evaporate some water then cool.

Separate, wash gently and dry crystals.

Why these steps

Excess solid ensures acid used up.

Insoluble excess is removable by filtration.

Cooling gives crystals, not just concentrated liquid.

Avoid damaging sample by heating dry.

Titration setup

Pipette/filler transfers known alkali.

Suitable indicator and white tile.

Burette acid, tip filled, funnel removed.

Rinse with correct solutions.

Endpoint measurement

Read bottom meniscus at eye level.

Swirl, then add dropwise.

Titre = final−initial reading.

Rough run then concordant careful repeats.

Concentration steps

Convert cm³ to dm³.

Moles =cV.

Use balanced equation ratio.

Unknown c =moles/its volume.

g dm⁻³ =mol dm⁻³ ×Mr.

Error and safety

Overshoot increases titre. Residual water dilutes burette contents.

Extra flask water does not change pipetted moles.

Eye protection, filler, safe heating.

Mean cannot correct a consistent bias.
