QuestionInverse square

y is inversely proportional to x². When x = 3, y = 8. Find y when x = 6.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Write y = k/x².
  • k = 8 × 3² = 72.
  • At x = 6, y = 72/36 = 2.
QuestionDirect square

y is directly proportional to x². When x = 4, y = 48. Find the positive x when y = 75.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • y = kx² and k = 48/16 = 3.
  • 75 = 3x², so x² = 25.
  • The requested positive value is x = 5.

QuestionDensity

A metal block has mass 540 g and volume 200 cm³. Find its density in g/cm³ and kg/m³.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Density = mass/volume = 540/200 = 2.7 g/cm³.
  • 1 g = 0.001 kg and 1 cm³ = 0.000001 m³.
  • Therefore 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³, giving 2700 kg/m³.

QuestionSimilarity

QuestionCompound growth

£1500 earns 4% compound interest each year. Find its value after 3 years, to the nearest penny.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Use the annual multiplier 1.04.
  • Value = £1500 × 1.04³ = £1687.296.
  • The final amount is £1687.30. Keep unrounded values until the final money rounding.
QuestionCompound decay

A machine loses 12% of its value each year. Its initial value is £20000. Find its value after 2 years.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • The yearly multiplier is 1 − 0.12 = 0.88.
  • Value = £20000 × 0.88².
  • The result is £15488.
QuestionRates of change

A tangent to a volume-time curve passes through (2,15) and (8,39). Time is in minutes and volume is in litres. Estimate the instantaneous rate at the tangent point. Explain the units.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Tangent gradient = (39 − 15)/(8 − 2).
  • This is 24/6 = 4 litres per minute.
  • It estimates the rate at that moment, rather than necessarily the average rate over a longer interval.
