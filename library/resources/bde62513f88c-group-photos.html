Describing photos

Describing both photos

Talk about both images. Roughly 1½ minutes is recommended for the description; coverage need not be exactly equal. At least one relevant statement about each photo is necessary, but two minimal statements do not by themselves make a developed response.

PurposePhraseMeaning
introduceSur la première photo, je vois...In the first photo, I see...
locateau premier plan / à l'arrière-planin the foreground / background
describe an actionles personnes jouent / mangent / travaillentthe people play / eat / work
add an observationil fait beau; les personnes sourientthe weather is nice; the people are smiling
qualify an inferenceils ont l'air contentsthey look happy
move to second imageSur la deuxième photo...In the second photo...

Use visible details first: people, setting, actions and objects. Ils ont l'air... marks an impression; do not present an invented name, age or relationship as a visible fact.

Describe what is there. If you do not know one object, choose a different accurate detail. A comparison can help when it is supported, but is not a compulsory formula.
