Material cycles and decomposition

Carbon into organisms

Photosynthesis incorporates carbon dioxide.

Feeding transfers organic molecules.

Death and waste supply decomposers.

Carbon atoms are recycled, not created.

Carbon returned

Plants, animals and microbes respire.

Respiration releases carbon dioxide.

Burning biomass/fuels also releases it.

Mineral ions return to soil for root uptake.

Water cycle

Evaporation and plant transpiration.

Cooling vapour condenses into droplets.

Precipitation returns water to land.

Groundwater/runoff feed rivers and seas.

Microbial decay

Enzymes secreted outside cells digest material.

Soluble products enter microorganisms.

Suitable water and warmth support activity.

Too-hot conditions denature enzymes.

Compost versus biogas

Aerated compost supports aerobic decay.

Compost provides natural fertiliser.

Anaerobic decay produces methane fuel.

Biogas needs oxygen exclusion, moisture and warmth.

Follow material and energy

Materials cycle through living/non-living parts.

Energy enters mainly as sunlight.

Energy transfers through organisms and surroundings.

It is not recycled back into sunlight.
