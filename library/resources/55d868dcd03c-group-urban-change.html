Urban change

  • Suburbanisation: people and activities move from the inner city to the urban edge.
  • Counter-urbanisation: people move from cities to smaller settlements or rural areas.
  • Re-urbanisation: people and investment return to inner-city areas.

These movements affect housing demand, traffic, land values, services and the character of communities.

TrendWhy it happensPossible consequences
SuburbanisationHouseholds seek space; land at the edge may be cheaper, and transport allows commuting.Urban sprawl, greenfield development, longer journeys and greater demand for roads and services.
Counter-urbanisationSome households leave cities for smaller settlements, seeking environment, space or remote-working opportunities.Commuting villages, higher house prices and changed local services; access differs by income.
Re-urbanisationRegeneration, new homes, jobs and amenities attract people back to inner areas.Brownfield reuse and city-centre spending, but possible higher rents and displacement.
