Circuit practicals

Meters and resistance

Ammeter in series. Voltmeter across test component.

R = V/I, not current alone.

Check wiring before switching on.

Use low voltage and limit current.

Resistance and length

Move contact along same wire, measuring selected length.

Control material, diameter and temperature.

Record V and I, calculate R and repeat.

Plot R against length. Switch off between readings.

Series and parallel

Measure total V and I for each arrangement.

Series: resistances add.

Parallel: extra paths reduce total R.

Avoid short circuits and compare stable temperatures.

Gather I–V data

Adjust supply or variable resistor. Record paired V and I.

Reverse polarity for suitable negative readings.

Repeat. Plot I on y and V on x.

Protect diode and avoid overheating.

Resistor and lamp

Ohmic resistor: constant-temperature straight line through origin.

I–V gradient is 1/R.

Lamp: filament heats, R increases, gradient falls.

Lamp heating is part of its changing behaviour.

Diode and evaluation

Diode: significant current one way, little in reverse.

Use a suitable range and current limit.

Connect voltage across the component, not an unrelated section.

Explain how any uncontrolled heating affects results.
