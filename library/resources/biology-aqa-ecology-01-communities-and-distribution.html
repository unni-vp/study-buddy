Communities, adaptations and distribution

Levels and dependence

Individual, population, community, ecosystem.

Food, shelter, pollination and seed dispersal link species.

Stable communities fluctuate around balance.

Removing one species can affect many others.

Competition

Plants: light, space, water, mineral ions.

Animals: food, mates, territory.

Resources must be limited for competition.

Explain effects on survival and reproduction.

Abiotic conditions

Light, temperature, moisture.

Soil pH and mineral content.

Wind intensity and direction.

Carbon dioxide for plants, oxygen for aquatic animals.

Living influences

Food availability.

New predators or pathogens.

Competitors can prevent successful breeding.

Use a context-linked biological mechanism.

Adaptation types

Structural: fur or waxy leaves.

Behavioural: nocturnal activity.

Functional: concentrated urine.

Extremophiles tolerate extreme conditions.

Changing distribution

Temperature, water and atmospheric gases change.

Seasonal, geographical or human causes.

Predator peaks usually lag behind prey peaks.

Correlation does not isolate a single cause.
