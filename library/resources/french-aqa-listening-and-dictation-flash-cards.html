QuestionApply the language

Explain how a learner should handle an unfamiliar word in dictation.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Listen for its sound groups.
  • Use those sounds to attempt a plausible spelling.
  • Check how the word fits the sentence rather than abandoning the whole extract.

QuestionDevelop a response

Explain why translating dictation into English and counting one mark per word are both mistakes.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Dictation requires the French heard.
  • English translation does not fulfil that task.
  • The meaning is assessed holistically for 5 marks.
  • Spelling and grammatical accuracy are assessed holistically for a further 5, not one per word.
