Sorting

Comparing sorting methods

FeatureBubble sortMerge sort
Main methodCompares neighbouring items and swaps those in the wrong order.Splits into smaller lists, then merges them in order.
ProgressAfter a full pass, another item is in its final position. A pass with no swaps shows completion.One-item lists are merged into larger sorted lists until one remains.
Large listsCan require many comparisons and swaps, especially for reverse-ordered data.Generally more time-efficient for large lists.
Trade-offSimple to understand and implement.Needs extra memory for temporary lists.

Compare the same task and data. Explain the checks, comparisons or swaps needed rather than saying only that one method is faster.

When explaining an algorithm, state the starting condition, the repeated action, how the search area or list changes, and the stopping condition.
