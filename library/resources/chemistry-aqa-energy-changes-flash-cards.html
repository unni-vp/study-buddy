QuestionExplain and compare

An exothermic reaction transfers energy to the surroundings. Explain why this does not break the law of conservation of energy.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • The products have less energy than the reactants.
  • The decrease equals the energy transferred to the surroundings; energy is transferred, not created.

QuestionApply and calculate

On an energy profile, reactants are at 70 kJ, the peak is at 160 kJ and products are at 30 kJ, using the same reference scale. Calculate activation energy and overall energy change, then classify the reaction.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Activation energy = 160 − 70 = 90 kJ.
  • Overall change = 30 − 70 = −40 kJ.
  • Exothermic, because the products are lower in energy.

QuestionApply and calculate

Calculate the energy change for H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl. Bond energies: H–H 436, Cl–Cl 243, H–Cl 431 kJ/mol.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Bonds broken: 436 + 243 = 679 kJ.

Balance this alkaline fuel-cell half-equation: O₂ + __H₂O + __e⁻ → __OH⁻. Explain whether it is oxidation or reduction.

QuestionMulti-step application

For H₂ + Br₂ → 2HBr, the energy change is −103 kJ for the molar quantities in the equation. H–H bond energy is 436 and Br–Br is 193 kJ/mol. Calculate H–Br bond energy.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Energy to break reactant bonds = 436 + 193 = 629 kJ.
  • Let H–Br bond energy = x; two bonds form, releasing 2x.
  • 629 − 2x = −103, so 2x = 732.
  • x = 366 kJ/mol.

QuestionMulti-step application

Calculate the energy change for CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O and explain its sign. Bond energies in kJ/mol: C–H 413, O=O 498, C=O 805, O–H 464.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Breaking: (4 × 413) + (2 × 498) = 2,648 kJ.
  • Forming: (2 × 805) + (4 × 464) = 3,466 kJ.
  • Change = 2,648 − 3,466 = −818 kJ for the molar equation quantities.
  • It is exothermic: forming bonds releases more energy than breaking bonds requires.
QuestionMulti-step application

A student uses an uninsulated beaker, does not stir and takes only one temperature reading long after mixing. Suggest improvements and explain why they help.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Use an insulated cup and lid to reduce energy transfer to the surroundings.
  • Stir consistently to make mixture temperature more uniform.
  • Record promptly at regular intervals to detect the maximum or minimum, rather than missing it.
  • Repeat and average to reduce the effect of random variation, keeping the starting conditions the same.
QuestionMulti-step application

Write both acidic hydrogen fuel-cell half-equations, using four electrons in each, and show how they give the overall equation.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Oxidation: 2H₂ → 4H⁺ + 4e⁻.
  • Reduction: O₂ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻ → 2H₂O.
  • Add and cancel 4H⁺ and 4e⁻ from both sides.
  • Overall: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O.
