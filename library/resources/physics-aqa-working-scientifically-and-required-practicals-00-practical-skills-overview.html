Practical skills overview

Plan a fair test

Testable hypothesis based on science.

Independent variable changes. Dependent variable is measured.

Control variables prevent another explanation.

Use a suitable range and representative samples.

Choose and measure

Select apparatus for required range and resolution.

Tables: quantity and unit in headings. Record raw readings.

Check zero, read scales at eye level.

Hazard is potential harm. Risk depends on likelihood and seriousness.

Analyse and evaluate

Repeat, mean and range. Check anomalies before excluding them.

Best-fit graphs show relationships. Use data and uncertainty in conclusions.

Problem, effect, improvement: explain the link.

Heating and mechanics

Specific heat: E, m, Δθ. Insulation: compare cooling fairly.

Density: mass and appropriate volume.

Spring: F versus extension.

Acceleration: vary force or mass while controlling the other.

Electricity

Ammeter in series. Voltmeter across component. R = V/I.

Resistance: wire length and series/parallel combinations.

I–V: fixed resistor, filament lamp and diode.

Limit current and heating.

Waves and radiation

Ripple tank and string: measure f and λ, use v = fλ.

Reflection/refraction: ray marks, normal, angles.

IR: matched temperature or starting conditions, distance and exposure.

Control a specific cause of error.
