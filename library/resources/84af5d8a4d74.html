Reading and translation into English questions & answers

1 mark2 marks3 marks4 marks10 marks

1 mark

1. Translate: actuellement.

Answer:

Currently.

2. Translate: une librairie.

Answer:

A bookshop.

3. Translate: ne...que.

Answer:

Only.

4. Translate: malgré.

Answer:

Despite.

5. Translate: sauf.

Answer:

Except.

6. Translate: trop cher.

Answer:

Too expensive.

7. Translate: rapidement.

Answer:

Quickly.

8. Translate: la semaine dernière.

Answer:

Last week.

9. Translate: à l'avenir.

Answer:

In future.

10. Translate: je voudrais.

Answer:

I would like.

2 marks

11. Read: Ma sœur adore la musique, mais mon frère préfère lire. Give each person's preference.

Answer:
  • Sister: music.
  • Brother: reading.

12. Read: Avant, je détestais le sport. Maintenant, je joue au tennis tous les jours. Give the earlier opinion and present activity.

Answer:
  • Used to hate sport.
  • Now plays tennis every day.

13. Read: Le village est calme, mais il manque des magasins. Give an advantage and a disadvantage.

Answer:
  • It is quiet.
  • There is a lack of shops.

14. Read: Les billets ne sont pas chers, mais le voyage est long. Give the positive and negative.

Answer:
  • Tickets are not expensive.
  • The journey is long.

15. Translate: Je viens de finir mes devoirs.

Answer:

I have just finished my homework.

16. Translate: J'habite ici depuis deux ans.

Answer:

I have lived here for two years.

The action still continues.

17. Translate: Je ne regarde plus la télévision.

Answer:

I no longer watch television.

18. Explain what les refers to in Les livres sont chers, mais je les achète.

Answer:

The books. Les is a direct object pronoun replacing les livres.

19. Infer the meaning of impossible in Cette solution est impossible, and identify the word-family clue.

Answer:

Impossible; im- gives the opposite of possible.

20. Infer consommateur from consommer and a shopping context.

Answer:

A consumer. The agent ending refers to a person who consumes/buys products.

3 marks

21. Translate: Hier, nous avons pris le bus parce que les billets étaient moins chers.

Answer:

Yesterday, we took the bus because the tickets were cheaper.

Keep the past event, reason and comparison.

22. Translate: Je voudrais travailler dans une librairie, mais ma sœur préfère la bibliothèque.

Answer:

I would like to work in a bookshop, but my sister prefers the library.

Do not confuse librairie and bibliothèque.

23. Translate: Il n'y a pas assez de bus, donc je vais à pied au collège.

Answer:

There are not enough buses, so I walk to school.

Not enough does not mean no buses.

24. Translate: Les amis que je retrouve au parc sont très gentils.

Answer:

The friends whom/that I meet in the park are very kind.

Que introduces the people met; très modifies kind.

4 marks

25. Translate: L'année dernière, j'ai passé une semaine en France. La ville était belle, mais il pleuvait souvent. À l'avenir, je vais voyager en train.

Answer:

Last year, I spent a week in France. The town/city was beautiful, but it often rained / was often raining. In future, I am going to travel by train.

Preserve the completed visit, background weather, frequency and future plan.

26. Explain four checks to make after translating a passage into English.

Answer:
  • Every phrase is included.
  • Subjects and pronoun references are correct.
  • Time frames and negatives are preserved.
  • Numbers, comparisons and qualifiers retain their precise meanings.

10 marks

27. Translate the whole passage into English: La semaine dernière, je suis allée au cinéma avec ma sœur. Le film était intéressant, mais les billets étaient trop chers. D'habitude, je préfère regarder des films à la maison parce que c'est plus calme. À l'avenir, j'aimerais aller au cinéma moins souvent et économiser de l'argent pour mes prochaines vacances.

Answer:

Last week, I went to the cinema with my sister. The film was interesting, but the tickets were too expensive. Usually, I prefer watching films at home because it is quieter. In future, I would like to go to the cinema less often and save money for my next holidays.

Keep too expensive, quieter, would like and less often. These details give more than the general topic. The model uses natural English word order without adding facts.
