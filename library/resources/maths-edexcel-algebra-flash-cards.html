QuestionComplete square

Write x² − 6x + 11 in completed-square form. Hence state the turning point and whether the graph has real roots.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • x² − 6x + 11 = (x − 3)² + 2.

QuestionQuadratic sequence

Find the nth term of 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, … .

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • First differences are 5, 7, 9, 11. The second difference is 2, giving a leading term n².
  • Subtract n² from each term: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, which is 2n.
  • The nth term is n² + 2n.
QuestionThree brackets

Expand and simplify (x + 1)(x − 2)(x + 3).

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • First, (x + 1)(x − 2) = x² − x − 2.
  • Multiply by x + 3: x³ + 3x² − x² − 3x − 2x − 6.
  • Collect terms: x³ + 2x² − 5x − 6.
QuestionProof

Prove algebraically that the sum of any three consecutive integers is a multiple of 3.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Let the integers be n, n + 1 and n + 2, where n is an integer.
  • Their sum is 3n + 3 = 3(n + 1).
  • Since n + 1 is an integer, the sum is a multiple of 3. One numerical example would not prove the general claim.
