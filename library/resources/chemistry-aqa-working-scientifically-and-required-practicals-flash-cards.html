QuestionApply and calculate

A 25.0 cm³ sodium-hydroxide sample reacts with 20.0 cm³ of 0.100 mol dm⁻³ HCl. Calculate NaOH concentration.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • HCl moles = 0.100 ×0.0200 = 0.00200 mol.
  • HCl:NaOH ratio is 1:1, so alkali moles = 0.00200.
  • Concentration = 0.00200/0.0250 = 0.0800 mol dm⁻³.
QuestionApply and calculate

Convert 0.0800 mol dm⁻³ NaOH into g dm⁻³, showing Mr using Na=23, O=16, H=1.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Mr = 23+16+1 = 40.
  • Mass concentration = molar concentration ×Mr.
  • 0.0800 ×40 = 3.20 g dm⁻³.

QuestionMulti-step application

Describe a controlled investigation comparing temperature rise at different metal masses in a displacement reaction.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Use measured equal solution volumes/concentrations at comparable starting temperatures in insulated cups.
  • Add a range of known metal masses with controlled piece size and mixing.
  • Record temperatures consistently to find each maximum/change, and repeat.
  • Compare ΔT, considering limiting reagent, heat loss and total solution mass; handle chemicals safely.
QuestionMulti-step application

Explain why a correlation between acid concentration and rate needs a controlled method to support causation.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Temperature, solid surface area or volume may also change between trials.
  • Each can affect collisions or the measured endpoint.
  • Keep these consistent while changing only concentration and repeat.
  • Then rate differences are better attributed to concentration, with uncertainty still considered.
QuestionMulti-step application

Describe a safe aqueous electrolysis investigation with inert electrodes.

Select the card or press Space to reveal the answer.

Answer
  • Place the solution in a suitable vessel and separate inert electrodes.
  • Connect to low-voltage DC with named cathode− and anode+.
  • Observe/collect small product samples and use suitable gas/metal tests.
  • Keep conditions controlled, avoid short circuits and use ventilation/eye protection without inhaling gases.
